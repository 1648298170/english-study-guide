最后一课，补齐两大件：**状语从句**（给动作加"何时/何地/为何/如果"的从句）和**介词短语**（英语里最便宜也最 prolific 的修饰成分）。后者有个绝活——一层套一层，像洋葱。本课教你辨身份、剥洋葱，并拆掉最后一个常见结构 it is...for...to...。

## 本课目标

## 核心方法

### 七类状语从句速查表

状语从句 = 给主句动作加背景信息的从句。它不修饰名词（那是定语从句），它修饰动作。

| 类型 | 常用引导词 | 回答的问题 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 时间 | when / while / before / after / until / as soon as | 何时？ |
| 条件 | if / unless / as long as | 什么条件下？ |
| 原因 | because / since / as | 为什么？ |
| 让步 | although / even though / though | 尽管……还是？ |
| 目的 | so that / in order that | 为了什么？ |
| 结果 | so...that / such...that | 到什么程度？ |
| 比较 | than / as...as | 和什么比？ |

翻译总原则：**中文习惯先说背景（时间/条件/原因/让步），再说正事。** 所以英语里挂在句尾的状语从句，中文常提到句首——第⑤步"状语调位"的主战场。

### 七类各一例：快速拆解

每类一句，感受引导词 + 切分位置（均为简版五步）：

```
① 时间  Call me when you get home.
切分    [Call me] [(when you get home).]  → 主干 call me；从句=何时call
调整    到家后给我打个电话。

② 条件  Unless it stops raining, we will stay inside.
切分    [(Unless it stops raining),] [we will stay inside.]
调整    雨要是不停，我们就待在屋里。

③ 原因  Since you are busy, I will handle it myself.
切分    [(Since you are busy),] [I will handle it myself.]
调整    你忙的话，这事我自己来处理。

④ 让步  Although she was tired, she kept working.
切分    [(Although she was tired),] [she kept working.]
调整    虽然很累，她还是继续工作。

⑤ 目的  He spoke slowly so that everyone could follow.
切分    [He spoke slowly] [(so that everyone could follow).]
调整    他讲得很慢，好让每个人都能跟上。

⑥ 结果  The box was so heavy that nobody could move it.
切分    [The box was so heavy] [(that nobody could move it).]
调整    箱子重得没人搬得动。

⑦ 比较  She runs as fast as her brother does.
切分    [She runs] [(as fast as her brother does).]
调整    她跑得和她哥哥一样快。
```

注意⑥：so...that 是"如此……以至于"，that 后是从句；别和第 3 课的句级连词 so（所以）搞混——数谓语：so...that 前后各一套主谓，但结构上是主从，不是并列。

### 介词短语的双重身份：定语还是状语？

同一个介词短语，两种完全不同的工作。判断法只有一条：

> **紧跟名词、回答"哪一个/什么样的" → 定语；说明动作的何时/何地/如何 → 状语。**

对比经典组：

```
A  I read the book on the train.
   on the train 说明 read 发生在哪 → 状语 → 我在火车上读了那本书

B  I read the book on the shelf.
   on the shelf 说明是哪本书 → 定语 → 我读了架子上的那本书
```

卡壳时的测试：对介词短语提问——能问"哪本书？"（which book）就是定语；能问"在哪读的？"（where）就是状语。语义会给你答案。

### 剥洋葱法：多层介词短语逐层拆

英语介词短语可以无限套娃。拆法：**先找到核心名词，由外向内一层层翻译，中文从最外层说起。**

```
原句（短语）  the cover of the book on the table in the room
剥洋葱        核心 = the cover（封面）
              第 1 层 (of the book) → 那本书的 封面
              第 2 层 (on the table) → 桌子上的 那本书
              第 3 层 (in the room) → 房间里的 桌子
中文组装      从最外层说起：房间里的 + 桌子上的 + 那本书的 + 封面
              → 房间里桌子上那本书的封面
```

原理：英语从核心往外长（cover ← book ← table ← room），中文从外往里说（房间→桌子→书→封面）。**方向正好相反**——这就是介词短语难读的根本原因，也是剥洋葱法的解药。

### it is...for...to... 结构：假主语还原术

```
原句  It is important for students to practice every day.
还原  假主语 it 占了句首，真主语是 to practice every day
      还原版：For students, to practice every day is important.
切分  [It is important] [(for students)] [(to practice every day) = 真主语]
翻译  对学生来说，每天练习很重要。
```

识别信号：it is + 形容词 + for sb + to do —— 这个 it 不指代任何东西，只是给"很长的不定式"占座。遇到就把 to do 拎出来当主语，句子立刻现原形。

## 例句拆解示范

### 示范 1 · 热身（日常对话 · 12 词 · 条件从句）

```
原句  If you finish your homework early, we can watch a movie together.
切分  [If you finish your homework early,] [we can watch a movie together.]
      └─ 条件状语从句 ────────────┘ └─ 主句 ────────────────┘
主干  主句 we 【谓语】can watch a movie
配件  (if you finish your homework early) → 条件状语从句
      early → 从句内状语
      together → 方式状语
直译  如果你早点完成作业，我们就可以一起看一部电影。
调整  作业要是早点写完，我们就一起看场电影。
```

### 示范 2 · 标准（职场场景 · 19 词 · 让步从句 + 双重身份介词短语）

```
原句  Although he was tired after the long meeting, he checked the report on his desk carefully before going home.
切分  [(Although he was tired (after the long meeting)),] [he] [checked] [the report (on his desk)] [carefully] [(before going home).]
      └─ 让步状语从句 ────────────────┘ └主┘ └谓语┘ └宾语 + 定语 ──┘ └状语┘ └─ 时间状语 ─┘
主干  he 【谓语】checked the report
配件  (although he was tired) → 让步状语从句
      (after the long meeting) → 从句内时间状语（was tired 是状态，非动作 → 不修饰名词 → 状语）
      long → 定语，修饰 meeting
      (on his desk) → 定语，修饰 report（回答"哪份报告"）
      carefully → 方式状语
      (before going home) → 时间状语；going 是 doing 形式，非谓语
直译  尽管他在长会之后很累，他仔细检查了那份报告（他桌上的）在回家之前。
调整  开完漫长的会议他很累，但回家前还是仔细检查了桌上的那份报告。
```

看点：两个介词短语两种身份——after the long meeting 是状语（何时累），on his desk 是定语（哪份报告）。用提问法各验一遍。

### 示范 3 · 标准（旅行场景 · 21 词 · 目的从句）

```
原句  She left her phone number with the front desk so that the hotel could contact her in case of an emergency.
切分  [She left her phone number (with the front desk)] [(so that the hotel could contact her (in case of an emergency)).]
      └─ 主句 + 对象状语 ────────────────┘ └─ 目的状语从句 ──────────────────┘
主干  she 【谓语】left her phone number
配件  (with the front desk) → 对象状语（留给谁）
      (so that the hotel could contact her) → 目的状语从句
      (in case of an emergency) → 从句内条件状语（介词短语版条件）
直译  她留下了她的电话号码给前台，以便酒店能够联系她，以防有紧急情况。
调整  她把电话号码留给了前台，这样万一有急事，酒店能联系到她。
```

### 示范 4 · 挑战（新闻/纪实风格 · 28 词 · 剥洋葱 + 时间从句）

```
原句  When the fire alarm went off, the manager on the third floor of the building
      near the park calmly guided everyone out of the office within five minutes.

切分  [(When the fire alarm went off),] [the manager (on the third floor) (of the building)
      └─ 时间状语从句 ─────────┘ └─ 主语 + 三层定语（剥洋葱）─────
      (near the park)] [calmly guided] [everyone] [(out of the office)] [(within five minutes).]
      ─────────────┘ └─ 谓语 ─────┘ └宾语┘ └─ 方向状语 ──┘ └─ 时间状语 ──────┘

主干  the manager 【谓语】guided everyone
配件  (when the fire alarm went off) → 时间状语从句
      剥洋葱（定语三层，由内向外组装）：
        核心 = the manager
        第 1 层 (on the third floor) → 三楼的 经理
        第 2 层 (of the building) → 大楼的 三楼
        第 3 层 (near the park) → 公园旁的 大楼
        中文组装：公园旁那栋大楼三楼的经理
      calmly → 方式状语
      (out of the office) → 方向状语
      (within five minutes) → 时间状语
直译  当火警响起时，那个经理（三楼的、大楼的、公园附近的）冷静地引导所有人出了办公室，在五分钟之内。
调整  火警响起时，公园旁那栋大楼三楼的经理临危不乱，五分钟内就把所有人带出了办公室。
```

看点：28 词的长句，谓语只有 2 个（went off / guided）——吓人的部分全是介词短语洋葱。剥完三层，主干短得像小学英语。

### 示范 5 · 挑战（学习方法场景 · 20 词 · it is...for...to... + 定从）

```
原句  It is difficult for many adults to find time to read books in a language that they are still learning.
切分  [It is difficult] [(for many adults)] [(to find time (to read books (in a language (that they are still learning)))) = 真主语]
      └ it 假主语 ─────┘ └─ 逻辑主语 ──┘ └─ 真主语（不定式，内含三层配件）───────────────────┘
主干  （还原后）to find time 【谓语】is difficult（for many adults）
配件  it → 假主语，占位用
      (for many adults) → 不定式的逻辑主语
      (to read books) → 定语，修饰 time（什么时候间：用来读书的）
      (in a language) → 状语（用什么语言读）
      (that they are still learning) → 定语从句，修饰 language
直译  它是很难的，对许多成年人来说，找到时间去读书（用一种他们仍在学习的语言）。
调整  对很多成年人来说，要挤出时间用一门自己还在学的语言读书，确实不容易。
```

看点：假主语还原后，主干短小精悍；真主语内部又是一个洋葱。本句综合了本课全部三件武器——还原术、剥洋葱、定从识别。

## 中文学习者常见误区

| ❌ 错误做法 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 介词短语一律当状语（"在桌上的那份…"读成"在桌上读了…"） | 用提问法判断：which 名词？还是 where/when 动作？ | 同一个短语，挂名词是定语、挂动词是状语，意思天差地别 |
| 多层介词短语从左往右顺着译 | 找核心名词，由外向内组装 | 英语从核心往外长，中文从外往里说，方向相反 |
| 时间/条件从句留在句尾硬译 | 背景从句前置（状语调位） | "我们就待在屋里如果明天下雨"——没人这样说话 |
| it is...for...to... 的 it 硬找指代 | 识别假主语，把 to do 拎出来当真主语 | 这个 it 什么都不指，只占座；找指代只会越找越乱 |
| 把 so that 一律当"所以" | so that = 以便（目的），so = 所以（并列） | 数谓语 + 看搭配：so that 后是从句，句级 so 后是平级分句 |

## 实战练习

任务统一：按五步法完整拆解（原句 → 切分 → 主干 → 配件 → 直译/调整）。介词短语必须标注身份（定语/状语 + 修饰谁），多层介词短语请用剥洋葱法逐层写出。

**热身**

1. Call me when you arrive at the station.
2. He took a taxi because he was afraid of being late.
3. Unless the weather improves, we will cancel the picnic.

**标准**

4. While the children were playing in the garden, their mother was preparing lunch in the kitchen.
5. The letters on the desk in the corner of the office have been there for almost two weeks.
6. During the lecture, she spoke slowly and clearly so that the students could take notes more easily.
7. It is necessary for all the new employees to attend the safety training on Monday morning.

**挑战**

8. Before the bridge was built, villagers had to drive around the mountain for two hours, so most of them simply rowed across the river in small boats.
9. Although the price of the apartment in the city center was far higher than most young families could afford, the agent said that dozens of people had already asked to see it.
10. It is becoming harder for small shops in the old town to compete with the large stores that sell almost everything at lower prices.

## 练习答案

**1.**

```
原句  Call me when you arrive at the station.
切分  [Call me] [(when you arrive (at the station)).]
      └─ 主干 ┘ └─ 时间状语从句 ─────────┘
主干  （你）【谓语】call me（祈使句，主语 you 省略）
配件  (when you arrive) → 时间状语从句
      (at the station) → 从句内地点状语
直译  打电话给我，当你到达车站时。
调整  到了车站就给我打电话。
```

**2.**

```
原句  He took a taxi because he was afraid of being late.
切分  [He took a taxi] [(because he was afraid (of being late)).]
      └─ 主句 ──────┘ └─ 原因状语从句 ──────────┘
主干  he 【谓语】took a taxi
配件  (because he was afraid of being late) → 原因状语从句
      (of being late) → 从句内状语（afraid of 搭配；being 是 doing，非谓语）
直译  他坐了出租车，因为他害怕迟到。
调整  他怕迟到，所以打了车。
```

**3.**

```
原句  Unless the weather improves, we will cancel the picnic.
切分  [(Unless the weather improves),] [we will cancel the picnic.]
      └─ 条件状语从句 ─────────┘ └─ 主句 ───────────────┘
主干  we 【谓语】will cancel the picnic
配件  (unless the weather improves) → 条件状语从句
      unless = if...not（如果不）
直译  除非天气好转，我们将取消野餐。
调整  天气要是不好转，野餐就取消。
```

**4.**

```
原句  While the children were playing in the garden, their mother was preparing lunch in the kitchen.
切分  [(While the children were playing (in the garden)),] [their mother was preparing lunch (in the kitchen).]
      └─ 时间状语从句 ───────────────────┘ └─ 主句 ──────────────────────┘
主干  their mother 【谓语】was preparing lunch
配件  while → 引导时间状语从句（同时进行）
      (in the garden) → 从句内地点状语
      (in the kitchen) → 主句地点状语
直译  当孩子们在花园里玩的时候，他们的母亲正在厨房里准备午餐。
调整  孩子们在花园玩，妈妈在厨房做午饭。（中文两短句更自然）
```

**5.**

```
原句  The letters on the desk in the corner of the office have been there for almost two weeks.
切分  [The letters (on the desk) (in the corner) (of the office)] [have been there] [(for almost two weeks).]
      └─ 主语 + 三层定语（剥洋葱）──────────────┘ └─ 谓语 ─────┘ └─ 时间状语 ────┘
主干  the letters 【谓语】have been there
配件  剥洋葱：核心 = the letters
        第 1 层 (on the desk) → 桌上的 信件
        第 2 层 (in the corner) → 角落里的 桌子
        第 3 层 (of the office) → 办公室的 角落
        中文组装：办公室角落里桌上的那些信
      (for almost two weeks) → 时间状语
直译  那些信件（桌子上、角落里的、办公室的）已经在那里差不多两周了。
调整  办公室角落桌上那摞信，已经搁了快两个星期了。
```

**6.**

```
原句  During the lecture, she spoke slowly and clearly so that the students could take notes more easily.
切分  [(During the lecture),] [she spoke slowly and clearly] [(so that the students could take notes more easily).]
      └─ 时间状语 ──┘ └─ 主句（并列方式状语）────┘ └─ 目的状语从句 ────────────────┘
主干  she 【谓语】spoke
配件  (during the lecture) → 时间状语
      slowly ∥ clearly → 并列方式状语
      (so that the students could take notes more easily) → 目的状语从句
      more easily → 从句内比较状语
直译  在讲座期间，她说得慢并且清楚，以便学生们能更容易地记笔记。
调整  讲课时她说得又慢又清楚，好让学生们更容易记笔记。
```

**7.**

```
原句  It is necessary for all the new employees to attend the safety training on Monday morning.
切分  [It is necessary] [(for all the new employees)] [(to attend the safety training (on Monday morning)) = 真主语]
      └ it 假主语 ─────┘ └─ 逻辑主语 ─────────┘ └─ 真主语 ──────────────┘
主干  （还原后）to attend the training 【谓语】is necessary
配件  it → 假主语
      (for all the new employees) → 逻辑主语
      new → 定语，修饰 employees
      safety → 定语，修饰 training
      (on Monday morning) → 时间状语
直译  它是必要的，对所有新员工来说，参加安全培训在周一上午。
调整  所有新员工都必须参加周一上午的安全培训。
```

**8.**

```
原句  Before the bridge was built, villagers had to drive around the mountain for two hours, so most of them simply rowed across the river in small boats.
切分  [(Before the bridge was built),] [villagers had to drive (around the mountain) (for two hours),]
      └─ 时间状语从句 ────────┘ └─ 分句① ────────────────────────────┘
      [so most of them simply rowed (across the river) (in small boats).]
      └─ 分句②（so 并列）──────────────────────────────────┘

主干  从句 the bridge 【谓语】was built
      分句① villagers 【谓语】had to drive
      分句② most of them 【谓语】rowed
配件  before → 引导时间状语从句
      (around the mountain) → 路线状语
      (for two hours) → 时间状语
      simply → 状语
      (across the river) → 方向状语
      (in small boats) → 方式状语
直译  在桥被建好之前，村民们不得不绕着山开车两个小时，所以他们大多数人干脆划小船过河。
调整  大桥建成前，村民开车进城得绕山两个小时，所以大多数人干脆划着小船过河。
```

**9.**

```
原句  Although the price of the apartment in the city center was far higher than most young families could afford, the agent said that dozens of people had already asked to see it.
切分  [(Although the price (of the apartment) (in the city center) was far higher
      └─ 让步状语从句（内含剥洋葱 + 比较从句）──────────────────
      (than most young families could afford)),] [the agent said] [(that) dozens of people had already asked to see it.]
      ────────────────────────────┘ └─ 主句 ──┘ └─ 宾语从句 ──────────────────┘

主干  主句 the agent 【谓语】said + 宾从
      让步从句 the price 【谓语】was higher
      比较从句 families 【谓语】could afford
配件  although → 引导让步状语从句
      剥洋葱：核心 = the price
        (of the apartment) → 公寓的 价格
        (in the city center) → 市中心的 公寓
      far → 程度状语
      (than most young families could afford) → 比较状语从句
      (that) → 宾语从句引导词
      dozens of → 定语，修饰 people
      already → 状语
直译  尽管那套公寓的价格（市中心的）远远高于大多数年轻家庭能负担的，中介说几十个人已经要求看它了。
调整  市中心那套公寓的价格远超大多数年轻家庭的承受能力，但中介说，已经有几十个人约着要看了。
```

**10.**

```
原句  It is becoming harder for small shops in the old town to compete with the large stores that sell almost everything at lower prices.
切分  [It is becoming harder] [(for small shops (in the old town))]
      └ it 假主语 ─────────┘ └─ 逻辑主语 + 定语 ──────────
      [(to compete (with the large stores (that sell almost everything (at lower prices)))) = 真主语]
      └─ 真主语（内含状语 + 定从）──────────────────────────────────┘

主干  （还原后）to compete 【谓语】is becoming harder
配件  it → 假主语
      (for small shops) → 逻辑主语
      (in the old town) → 定语，修饰 shops（哪些小店）
      (with the large stores) → 对象状语（compete with）
      (that sell almost everything at lower prices) → 定语从句，修饰 stores（9 词 → 前置偏长，可拆）
      (at lower prices) → 从句内方式状语
直译  它正变得更难，对老城区的小商店来说，与大商店（以更低价格出售几乎所有东西的）竞争。
调整  老城区的小店日子越来越难过：那些大商店几乎什么都卖，价格还更低，根本竞争不过。
```

看点：本题集五课之大成——假主语还原（第 5 课）、剥洋葱（第 5 课）、定从（第 4 课）、并列思维拆句（第 3 课）、五步法全程护航（第 2 课）。能独立做对这题，本课毕业。

</details>
