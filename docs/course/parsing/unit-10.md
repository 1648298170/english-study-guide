> ⏱ 预计 4~6 小时，建议拆 3 天（一天一档）｜ 前置：[第 9 课](./unit-09.md) ｜ 难度：B1+（拆句课）

## 本课目标

学完这课，你将能：

- [ ] 不看笔记，独立完成 30 句闯关的 80% 以上
- [ ] 达到速度标准：一档句 2 分钟内拆完，三档句 5 分钟内拆完
- [ ] 把拆句法用进三个考试场景：阅读定位、翻译三步、完形搭配
- [ ] 建立自己的"拆句本"，开始每天 3 句精拆的长期节奏

## 核心方法

**全流程复盘（一句话方法论）**

```
数谓语 → 切连词 → 找主干 → 挂配件 → 调语序
   ↓        ↓         ↓         ↓         ↓
 几个分句？ 在哪断？ 谁干了什么？ 定/状/插/非谓 顺译再调
```

**闯关规则**

- 30 句分三档：第一档 日常与职场（12~20 词）；第二档 四六级风格（20~28 词）；第三档 考研雅思风格（28~40 词）
- 三档例句均为**风格仿写的模拟句**，不是真题原文
- 每句必须走完整五步：原句 → 切分 → 主干 → 配件 → 直译/调整
- 自评标准：一档 9/10、二档 8/10、三档 7/10，达标即通关
- 卡壳超过 5 分钟先跳过，做完再回头看——考试也是这个节奏

**速度训练要点**：先准后快。前 10 句不限时求标记正确，后 20 句再掐表。

## 例句拆解示范

正式闯关前，四个带练句热身，每档各来一句，跟着走一遍。

带练 A（日常）

```
原句  The train that I take every morning was ten minutes late today, so I had to run all the way to the office.
切分  [The train (that I take every morning)] [was【谓】 ten minutes late today,] [so] [I had to run【谓】 all the way to the office]
      └ 主语 + 定语从句 ────────┘  └─ 谓语 + 表语 ─────┘ └连┘ └─ 谓语 + 状语 ────────┘
主干  The train was late + I had to run
配件  that I take every morning → 定语从句；today / all the way → 状语
直译  （我每天早上乘的）火车今天晚点了十分钟，所以我不得不一路跑到办公室
调整  我每天早上坐的那趟车今天晚点了十分钟，害得我一路狂奔到办公室
```

带练 B（职场）

```
原句  The contract, which both sides discussed for three weeks, will be signed once the lawyers agree on the final details.
切分  [The contract, (which both sides discussed for three weeks),] [will be signed【谓】] [(once the lawyers agree on the final details)]
      └ 主语 + 非限定定语从句 ───────────┘  └─ 谓语（被动）┘ └─ 时间状语从句 ─────────┘
主干  The contract will be signed
配件  which both sides discussed for three weeks → 非限定定语从句；once... → 时间状语从句
直译  这份合同（双方讨论了三周），将在律师们就最终细节达成一致后被签署
调整  双方围绕这份合同谈了三个星期，等律师敲定最后细节后即可签约
```

带练 C（四六级风格 · 模拟句）

```
原句  Volunteers who joined the beach clean-up last weekend collected more than two tons of plastic waste, most of which came from takeout boxes.
切分  [Volunteers (who joined the beach clean-up last weekend)] [collected【谓】 more than two tons of plastic waste,] [(most of which came from takeout boxes)]
      └ 主语 + 定语从句 ───────────────┘  └─ 谓语 + 宾语 ──────┘  └ 非限定定语从句 ─────┘
主干  Volunteers collected plastic waste
配件  who joined... → 定语从句；most of which came from takeout boxes → 非限定定语从句
直译  （上周末参加海滩清理的）志愿者收集了两吨多塑料垃圾，（其中大部分来自外卖盒）
调整  上周末参加海滩清理的志愿者捡了两吨多塑料垃圾，大半都来自外卖盒
```

带练 D（考研雅思风格 · 模拟句）

```
原句  That many species are dying out faster than scientists predicted, largely because of human activity, is no longer a matter of debate among researchers.
切分  [(That many species are dying out faster than scientists predicted),] [【插】largely because of human activity【插】,] [is【谓】 no longer a matter of debate] [among researchers]
      └── That 主语从句（内含 than 比较从句）────────────────┘  └ 插入原因状语 ┘  └─ 谓语 + 表语 ─┘ └ 状语┘
主干  (That 主语从句整体作主语) is no longer a matter of debate
配件  than scientists predicted → 比较从句；largely because of human activity → 插入的原因状语；among researchers → 状语
直译  （许多物种正在以快于科学家预测的速度灭绝，主要因为人类活动），在研究者中不再是一个有争议的问题
调整  物种灭绝的速度比科学家预测的更快，而且主要是人类活动所致，这在研究者之间已不再是争议
```

## 中文学习者常见误区

| ❌ 误区 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 30 句一口气做完、对完答案就翻篇 | 每天 3 句精拆，错题抄拆解步骤 | 精拆一句的收获大于泛做十句；手上抄一遍，结构感才长得出来 |
| 阅读遇到长句，从头到尾逐词重读 | 定位题干关键词，只拆跟题目相关的那一句 | 考试时间有限，拆句是手术刀，不是犁地的犁 |
| 翻译时先查生词，再按词序拼中文 | 先拆主干骨架，挂上配件，查词放到最后 | 生词只是配件上的一个点；主干清楚了，生词常常能猜个八九不离十 |
| 拆解标记每次换花样，想到哪标到哪 | 符号固定、流程固定：( ) 框从句、【谓】标谓语、【插】剥插入 | 固定标记才能形成肌肉记忆；考场上靠的是手感，不是灵感 |

## 实战练习 · 30 句闯关

### 第一档 · 日常与职场（10 句，12~20 词）

1. I'm sorry for the delay, but the file you asked for is too big to send by email.
2. Could you let me know whether the meeting on Friday has been moved to another room?
3. The woman who just left is the new manager everyone has been talking about.
4. Thanks for the update. I'll pass it on to the team as soon as I get back.
5. Please remind everyone that the office will be closed next Monday for a public holiday.
6. I've been trying to reach you all morning, but your phone seems to be off.
7. The coffee machine on the third floor is broken again, so we'd better use the one downstairs.
8. My neighbor, a retired teacher, waters my plants whenever I travel for work.
9. Don't forget to double-check the numbers before you send the report to the head office.
10. To be honest, I'd rather work from home than spend two hours on the train every day.

### 第二档 · 四六级风格（10 句，20~28 词，模拟句）

11. Students who take part-time jobs during college can gain work experience, but they must learn to balance work and study.
12. With more people moving to big cities, housing prices in these areas have risen sharply over the past ten years.
13. The old library, which was built in 1990, remains the most popular place on campus during the busy exam season.
14. Encouraged by their teachers, more and more students have started reading English news every day in order to improve their language skills.
15. Although online shopping has made our daily life more convenient, it has also caused serious problems for many small local shops.
16. What matters most in learning a language is not how many words you know, but how bravely you use them.
17. The government has promised to build more hospitals in rural areas so that more farmers can enjoy better medical services.
18. Affected by the rising cost of living, many young people now prefer sharing a flat to living on their own.
19. It is widely reported that the number of college students choosing to work in smaller cities has doubled since 2015.
20. Those who exercise regularly not only keep fit but also handle pressure more easily than those who seldom work out.

### 第三档 · 考研雅思风格（10 句，28~40 词，模拟句）

21. What makes this discovery remarkable is not the technology behind it, but the fact that a team of young researchers questioned an idea that most experts had accepted for decades.
22. Never before have scientists had such powerful tools for studying the human brain, and yet, strangely enough, the deeper they look into it, the more mysteries they seem to find.
23. The theory, first put forward in the 1970s and long dismissed by most mainstream scholars, has recently regained wide attention thanks to new evidence collected from rocks in southern Africa.
24. Only when the last factory in the small town finally closed down did people begin to realize how heavily the local economy had depended on that single industry.
25. Cities that invest heavily in public transport, as several European studies have clearly shown, tend to enjoy cleaner air, quieter streets and stronger local businesses in the long run.
26. If the surprising experiment can be repeated by other teams around the world, an outcome that many researchers now openly doubt, the whole field of sleep medicine may well be rewritten within a decade.
27. Hardly had the new policy been officially announced when critics across the country began to argue that it would hurt, rather than help, the very people it was designed to protect.
28. What the survey reveals, beyond the dry numbers, is a generation of young people who work longer hours than their parents did yet feel less secure about the future.
29. So central is language to human life that without it neither education nor trade, not even the simplest daily cooperation, would be possible, a fact we rarely stop to consider.
30. Having spent years studying why some children learn faster than others, the professor concluded that motivation, rather than talent, plays the decisive role in the long process of learning.

## 练习答案

<details><summary>第一档 · 参考答案（1~10，先自己拆完再点开）</summary>

**1.**

```
原句  I'm sorry for the delay, but the file you asked for is too big to send by email.
切分  [I'm sorry for the delay,] [but] [the file (you asked for)] [is【谓】 too big (to send by email)]
      └── 谓语 + 状语 ──┘ └连┘ └ 主语 + 定语从句 ┘  └ 谓语 + 表语 + 结果 ─┘
主干  The file is too big
配件  you asked for → 定语从句（省略 that）；too...to → "太……而不能"；for the delay → 原因状语
直译  抱歉拖延了，但（你要的那个文件）太大而不能用邮件发送
调整  抱歉回复晚了，你要的文件太大，邮件根本发不出去
```

**2.**

```
原句  Could you let me know whether the meeting on Friday has been moved to another room?
切分  [Could you let【谓】 me know] [(whether the meeting on Friday has been moved to another room)]
      └──── 谓语 + 宾补 ────┘  └─ 宾语从句（内含被动）───────────┘
主干  Could you let me know (从句)
配件  whether → 从句引导词；has been moved → 被动；on Friday → 介短定语；to another room → 状语
直译  你能告诉我（周五的会议是否已被移到另一个房间）吗
调整  能不能告诉我，周五的会议是不是换到别的房间了
```

**3.**

```
原句  The woman who just left is the new manager everyone has been talking about.
切分  [The woman (who just left)] [is【谓】 the new manager (everyone has been talking about)]
      └ 主语 + 定语从句 ─┘  └─ 谓语 + 表语 + 定语从句 ──────────┘
主干  The woman is the new manager
配件  who just left → 定语从句；everyone has been talking about → 定语从句（省略 that）
直译  （刚离开的那个）女人是（每个人一直在谈论的）新经理
调整  刚才走的那位，就是大家一直在议论的新经理
```

**4.**

```
原句  Thanks for the update. I'll pass it on to the team as soon as I get back.
切分  [Thanks for the update.] [I'll pass【谓】 it on to the team] [(as soon as I get back)]
      └── 固定表达 ──┘   └─ 主谓 + 宾 + 状语 ─┘   └ 时间状语从句 ─┘
主干  I'll pass it on to the team
配件  as soon as I get back → 时间状语从句；for the update → 原因状语
直译  谢谢更新。我一回去就会把它转达给团队
调整  谢谢同步消息，我一回去就转告团队
```

**5.**

```
原句  Please remind everyone that the office will be closed next Monday for a public holiday.
切分  [Please remind【谓】 everyone] [(that the office will be closed next Monday for a public holiday)]
      └── 谓语 + 宾语 ──┘  └─ 宾语从句（内含被动 + 状语）─────────┘
主干  Please remind everyone (从句)
配件  will be closed → 被动；next Monday → 时间状语；for a public holiday → 原因状语
直译  请提醒每个人（办公室下周一将因公共假日关闭）
调整  请提醒大家：下周一公共假日，办公室不开门
```

**6.**

```
原句  I've been trying to reach you all morning, but your phone seems to be off.
切分  [I've been trying【谓】 to reach you] [all morning,] [but] [your phone seems【谓】 to be off]
      └── 谓语（现在完成进行）──┘ └状语┘ └连┘ └─ 主语 + 谓语 + 表语 ─┘
主干  I've been trying to reach you + your phone seems off
配件  all morning → 时间状语；to reach you → 宾语；to be off → 表语
直译  我一整个早上一直在试着联系你，但你的手机似乎关机了
调整  我打了一早上电话都没找到你，你手机好像关机了
```

**7.**

```
原句  The coffee machine on the third floor is broken again, so we'd better use the one downstairs.
切分  [The coffee machine (on the third floor)] [is broken【谓】 again,] [so] [we'd better use【谓】 the one (downstairs)]
      └ 主语 + 介短定语 ────┘   └─ 谓语 + 表语 + 状语 ┘ └连┘ └─ 主谓 + 宾 + 定语 ─┘
主干  The machine is broken + we'd better use the one
配件  on the third floor / downstairs → 后置定语；again → 状语
直译  （三楼的）咖啡机又坏了，所以我们最好用（楼下那台）
调整  三楼的咖啡机又坏了，咱们还是用楼下那台吧
```

**8.**

```
原句  My neighbor, a retired teacher, waters my plants whenever I travel for work.
切分  [My neighbor,【同位】a retired teacher,] [waters【谓】 my plants] [(whenever I travel for work)]
      └ 主语 + 贴名牌 ─────┘  └─ 谓语 + 宾语 ─┘ └─ 时间状语从句 ─┘
主干  My neighbor waters my plants
配件  a retired teacher → 同位语；whenever I travel for work → 时间状语从句
直译  我的邻居（一位退休教师），每当我在外出差时给我的植物浇水
调整  我的邻居是位退休教师，每次我出差，她都帮我浇花
```

**9.**

```
原句  Don't forget to double-check the numbers before you send the report to the head office.
切分  [Don't forget【谓】 to double-check the numbers] [(before you send the report to the head office)]
      └── 谓语 + 宾语 ─────────┘  └─ 时间状语从句 ─────────┘
主干  Don't forget to double-check the numbers
配件  before... → 时间状语从句（状语前置：中文先说）
直译  别忘了（在你把报告发给总部之前）再核对一遍数字
调整  报告发给总部之前，别忘了把数字再核对一遍
```

**10.**

```
原句  To be honest, I'd rather work from home than spend two hours on the train every day.
切分  【插】To be honest, [I'd rather work【谓】 from home] [(than spend two hours on the train every day)]
       剥掉插入语  └─ 主语 + 谓语 ─┘   └─ 比较结构 + 状语 ──────────┘
主干  I'd rather work from home
配件  To be honest → 插入语；than... → would rather...than 结构；every day → 时间状语
直译  说实话，我宁愿在家工作，而不是每天花两小时在火车上
调整  说实话，我宁可在家办公，也不想每天在火车上耗两个小时
```

切分  [(What matters most in learning a language)] [is【谓】 not (how many words you know) but (how bravely you use them)]

**17.**

```
原句  The government has promised to build more hospitals in rural areas so that more farmers can enjoy better medical services.
切分  [The government has promised【谓】 to build more hospitals] [(in rural areas)] [(so that more farmers can enjoy better medical services)]
      └── 主语 + 谓语 + 宾语 ─────────┘ └ 地点状语 ┘ └─ 目的状语从句 ──────────┘
主干  The government has promised to build more hospitals
配件  so that... → 目的状语从句；in rural areas → 地点状语
直译  政府承诺在农村地区建更多医院，以便更多农民能享受更好的医疗服务
调整  政府承诺在农村新建更多医院，让更多农民看得上病、看得好病
```

**18.**

```
原句  Affected by the rising cost of living, many young people now prefer sharing a flat to living on their own.
切分  [(Affected by the rising cost of living),] [many young people] [now prefer【谓】 sharing a flat (to living on their own)]
      └ done 状语，people 被"影响" ──┘  └ 主语 ─┘ └─ 谓语 + prefer A to B ─┘
主干  Many young people prefer sharing a flat
配件  Affected by... → done 原因状语；prefer A to B → 结构；now → 时间状语
直译  （受上涨的生活成本影响），许多年轻人现在更喜欢合租而不是独自居住
调整  生活成本越来越高，不少年轻人如今更愿意合租，而不是一个人住
```

**19.**

```
原句  It is widely reported that the number of college students choosing to work in smaller cities has doubled since 2015.
切分  [It] [is widely reported【谓】] [(that the number of college students (choosing to work in smaller cities) has doubled since 2015)]
      └占位┘└─ 谓语（被动）─┘  └─ 真主语（后置）────────────────────┘
主干  It is widely reported，真主语是 that 从句
配件  choosing to work in smaller cities → doing 后置定语；has doubled → 从句谓语；since 2015 → 时间状语
直译  （选择去小城市工作的大学生数量自 2015 年以来翻了一番）被广泛报道
调整  据媒体报道，2015 年以来，选择去小城市就业的大学生数量翻了一番
```

原句  Those who exercise regularly not only keep fit but also handle pressure more easily than those who seldom work out.
切分  [Those (who exercise regularly)] [not only keep【谓】 fit but also handle【谓】 pressure] [(more easily)] [(than those who seldom work out)]
      └ 主语 + 定语从句 ─┘  └─ not only...but also 并列谓语 ────┘ └状语┘ └─ 比较从句 + 定语从句 ─┘
主干  Those keep fit and handle pressure
配件  who exercise regularly / who seldom work out → 定语从句；not only...but also；than... → 比较结构
直译  （经常锻炼的那些人）不仅保持健康，而且比（很少锻炼的那些人）更轻松地应对压力
调整  经常锻炼的人不仅身体好，抗压能力也比不爱动的人强
```

**21.**

```
原句  What makes this discovery remarkable is not the technology behind it, but the fact that a team of young researchers questioned an idea that most experts had accepted for decades.
切分  [(What makes this discovery remarkable)] [is【谓】 not (the technology behind it) but (the fact (that a team of young researchers questioned an idea (that most experts had accepted for decades)))]

配件  behind it → 介短定语；that a team... questioned... → 同位语从句（fact 的内容）；that most experts had accepted for decades → 定语从句（修饰 idea）
直译  （使这个发现非凡的）不是（它背后的技术），而是（这个事实：一组年轻研究者质疑了（大多数专家已接受几十年的）一个观点）

原句  Never before have scientists had such powerful tools for studying the human brain, and yet, strangely enough, the deeper they look into it, the more mysteries they seem to find.
切分  【倒装还原】Scientists have never had such powerful tools before.
      [(Never before) have【助→搬回】scientists] [had【谓】 such powerful tools (for studying the human brain),] [and yet,【插】strangely enough【插】,] [(the deeper they look into it, the more mysteries they seem【谓】 to find)]

原句  The theory, first put forward in the 1970s and long dismissed by most mainstream scholars, has recently regained wide attention thanks to new evidence collected from rocks in southern Africa.

直译  这个理论（20 世纪 70 年代首次提出、长期被多数主流学者忽视），最近重新获得广泛关注，多亏了（从南部非洲岩石中收集的新证据）

切分  【倒装还原】People began to realize... only when the last factory finally closed down.

## 考试场景应用

**场景一：阅读题拆句法**

三步走：

1. **定位**：抓题干关键词（人名、数字、专有名词），回原文锁定那一句
2. **只拆相关句**：选项对应的往往就一两句，用五步法拆它，别的句子不看
3. **对照选项**：先比主干（谁干了什么），再比配件（时间、原因、范围）

警惕陷阱选项的两大套路：**配件真、主干假**（细节都对，主语换了人）；**主干真、配件假**（事没错，时间或范围被偷换）。

**场景二：翻译题三步**

1. **先拆**：数谓语、切连词，标出主干和配件
2. **后译**：按"语序三律 + 转换四法 + 化整为零"组装中文
3. **再检查**：对照三条清单——时态（了/过/将 补了吗）、单复数（students → 学生"们"/各位）、搭配（动宾是否成对：make a decision → "做决定"不是"制造决定"）

**场景三：完形填空的线索**

空格不是孤立的。拆空格所在的小句：先判断主干缺什么（谓语？宾语？连词？），再看前后搭配和逻辑连词（but / so / however 决定方向）。搭配线索 + 结构判断，双保险再选。

**学习节奏建议**

- **每天 3 句精拆 > 30 句泛做**：一句拆透、抄透，胜过十句囫囵吞
- **建立"拆句本"**：左边抄原句，中间写自己的五步拆解，右边贴订正；每周末把错题重拆一遍，连续两次全对的句子打勾毕业
- 拆句本是你的私人题库，考前翻它，比翻任何技巧书都管用
