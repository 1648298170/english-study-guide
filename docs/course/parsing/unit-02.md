> ⏱ 预计 4~6 小时，建议拆成 3 天完成 | 前置：[第 1 课 · 成分地图](./unit-01.md) | 难度：B1+（拆句课）

上一课你拿到了地图（成分）和心脏（谓语）。这一课把它们组装成一台完整的手术流程——**拆句五步法**。这是全课的基石：后面三课（并列、定从、状从）讲的都是"某一类断点怎么切"，而五步法是总纲。本课写最厚，值得你多待几天。

## 本课目标

- [ ] 能背出五步法，并说清每一步"做什么、为什么"
- [ ] 拿到长句，第一反应是数谓语，而不是查单词
- [ ] 能独立完成 20 词左右句子的完整五步拆解
- [ ] 卡住时会用"急救箱"三招自救
- [ ] 译文能主动脱离英文语序，输出通顺中文

## 核心方法

### 五步法总览

```
第①步  数谓语 → 定分句数（心脏几颗？）
第②步  找连词/从句引导词 → 落刀切分（刀口在哪？）
第③步  每个分句内找主干（主谓宾 / 主系表）
第④步  挂配件（定语、状语、补语各就各位）
第⑤步  按中文语序重组 → 先直译，再调整
```

一句话记住：**一数、二切、三主干、四挂件、五重组。**

下面用同一个例句把五步完整走一遍：

```
原句  The teacher said that the students who finished the exercises could leave early.

第①步  数谓语：said / finished / could → 3 个谓语 → 3 套主谓结构
第②步  落刀：that 前、who 前各有一刀
第③步  主干：主句 the teacher said...；宾从 the students could leave
第④步  配件：(who finished the exercises) → 定语，修饰 students；early → 状语
第⑤步  重组：直译→ 老师说（完成练习的学生们）可以早走
              调整→ 老师说，做完练习的同学可以早点走
```

### 第①步详解：数谓语，定分句数

动词形状的词全部圈出来，过"三问"（第 1 课），数出真谓语。这一步决定全局：**3 个谓语却只切了 1 刀，说明你漏刀了；1 个谓语却切了 3 刀，说明你切到肉里了。**

### 第②步详解：在连词 / 从句引导词处切分

刀口只认两种词：

| 刀口 | 词 | 效果 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 句级连词 | and / but / or / so / yet / 分号 | 两边是平级分句，切开各自独立 |
| 从句引导词 | that / which / who / whom / whose / when / where / because / if / although / so that... | 从句用 ( ) 整体框住，标注类型 |

**从句套从句怎么办？先切最外层。** 把整坨内层从句先当一个"大名词"打包放下，等外层结构理清了再回到包里拆。

### 第③步详解：各分句找主干

每个分句独立套骨架卡：主谓 / 主谓宾 / 主谓双宾 / 主谓宾补 / 主系表。此时不看任何配件，只回答"谁，做了什么/是什么"。

### 第④步详解：挂配件

把划掉的非谓语、介词短语、副词、从句逐一归位：定语挂名词（回答"哪一个"），状语挂动词（回答"何时何地如何为何"），补语补意思。每挂一件，标注它修饰谁。

### 第⑤步详解：重组三招

| 招式 | 英文习惯 | 中文习惯 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 定语前移 | 长修饰放名词后 | "……的"放名词前 | the book (I read yesterday) → 我昨天读的那本书 |
| 状语调位 | 状语常在句尾 | 时间/条件状语常移到句首或动词前 | We left (because it rained) → 因为下雨，我们就走了 |
| 长句切短 | 一句说到底 | 拆成几个短句 | 定语从句长时另起一句（第 4 课细讲） |

直译版允许翻译腔——它是检查理解的草稿；调整版才交卷。

### 常见卡点急救箱

| 症状 | 急救 |
| --- | --- |
| 谓语找不到 | 反推法：先找形容词、副词（-ly 词），它们总粘着动词或系动词；有比较级（faster/better）必有动词 |
| 从句套从句 | 先切最外层，把内层整坨当名词放下，理完外面再进里面 |
| 非谓语干扰 | to do / doing / done 一律先加括号划掉，它们绝不当谓语 |
| that 分不清 | that 后面是完整句 → 从句引导词；that 后面是名词 → 指示代词"那个" |

## 例句拆解示范

### 示范 1 · 热身（日常对话 · 12 词 · 并列句）

```
原句  I called her office twice yesterday morning, but nobody answered either call.
切分  [I called her office twice yesterday morning,] [but nobody answered either call.]
      └─ 分句① ──────────────────┘ └─ 分句②（but 转折）────────┘
主干  分句① I 【谓语】called her office
      分句② nobody 【谓语】answered either call
配件  twice → 频率状语
      yesterday morning → 时间状语
      either → 定语，修饰 call
直译  我打了她办公室两次昨天上午，但是没有人接任何一个电话。
调整  昨天上午我给她办公室打了两次电话，但都没人接。
```

看点：两个谓语（called / answered）= 两个分句，but 就是天然刀口。

### 示范 2 · 标准（日常出行 · 16 词 · 定从 + 并列）

```
原句  The train that goes to Beijing leaves at eight, so we still have plenty of time.
切分  [The train (that goes to Beijing)] [leaves at eight,] [so we still have plenty of time.]
      └─ 主语 + 定语从句 ──────┘ └分句①谓语+状语┘ └─ 分句②（so 结果）────────┘
主干  分句① the train 【谓语】leaves
      分句② we 【谓语】have time
配件  (that goes to Beijing) → 定语从句，修饰 train（4 词 → 前置法）
      at eight → 时间状语
      still → 状语
      plenty of → 定语，修饰 time
直译  那趟火车（开往北京的）八点出发，所以我们仍然有充足的时间。
调整  开往北京的那趟火车八点才发车，所以我们时间还很充裕。
```

看点：全句 3 个谓语（goes / leaves / have），但 goes 藏在从句里。切完才发现"火车"身上挂了个小从句。

### 示范 3 · 标准（新闻风格模拟句 · 21 词）

```
原句  Scientists in several countries say that the ice in the Arctic
      is now melting faster than most experts expected ten years ago.

切分  [Scientists (in several countries)] [say] [(that) the ice (in the Arctic) is now melting
      └─ 主语 + 定语 ────┘ └谓语┘ └─ 宾语从句（比较从句藏在句尾）───────────────
      faster (than most experts expected ten years ago).]
      ───────────────────────────────────────────┘

主干  主句 scientists 【谓语】say + 宾从
      宾从主干 the ice 【谓语】is melting
      比较从句 experts 【谓语】expected
配件  (in several countries) → 定语，修饰 scientists
      (that) → 宾语从句引导词
      (in the Arctic) → 定语，修饰 ice
      now → 时间状语
      faster → 比较状语
      (than most experts expected ten years ago) → 比较状语从句
      ten years ago → 从句内时间状语
直译  科学家们（几个国家的）说，（北极的）冰现在融化得更快，比大多数专家十年前预计的。
调整  多国科学家表示，北极冰层现在的融化速度，超过了大多数专家十年前的预计。
```

看点：三个谓语（say / is melting / expected）各归各位。注意 than 后面藏着一个完整的主谓（experts expected）——比较从句最容易被漏数。

### 示范 4 · 挑战（职场邮件风格 · 28 词）

```
原句  Since the client has asked for a revised plan by Friday,
      I suggest that we hold a short meeting tomorrow morning
      to go over the main changes together.

切分  [Since the client has asked for a revised plan by Friday,]
      └─ 原因状语从句 ────────────────────────────┘
      [I suggest (that) we hold a short meeting tomorrow morning (to go over the main changes together).]
      └─ 主句（内含宾语从句 + 目的状语）─────────────────────────────┘

主干  从句 the client 【谓语】has asked
      主句 I 【谓语】suggest + 宾从
      宾从 we 【谓语】hold a meeting
配件  since → 引导原因状语从句
      revised → 定语，修饰 plan
      by Friday → 时间状语
      (that) → 宾语从句引导词
      short → 定语，修饰 meeting
      tomorrow morning → 时间状语
      (to go over the main changes together) → 目的状语（说明开会目的）
直译  既然客户已经要求一份修改过的方案在周五之前，我建议我们开一个短会明天上午，以便一起过一遍主要改动。
调整  客户要求周五前交修改后的方案，所以我建议明天上午开个短会，大家一起过一遍主要改动。
```

看点：28 词，3 个谓语，三层结构（状从套主句套宾从）。注意 to go over 是 to do——第①步就该划掉的干扰项。

### 示范 5 · 挑战（考试风格模拟句 · 35 词）

```
原句  The survey conducted by a well-known university shows that
      students who read English articles regularly for at least six months
      make much more progress in reading than those who only do grammar exercises in class.

切分  [The survey (conducted by a well-known university)] [shows] [(that) students
      └─ 主语 + 后置定语 ──────────────┘ └谓语┘ └─ 宾语从句 ────────────
      (who read English articles regularly for at least six months) make much more progress
      └─ 定语从句，修饰 students ─────────────────────┘ └─ 宾从主干 ──────
      (in reading) (than those (who only do grammar exercises in class)).]
      └─ 范围┘ └─ 比较从句（内又套定从，修饰 those）──────────┘

主干  主句 the survey 【谓语】shows + 宾从
      宾从主干 students 【谓语】make progress
配件  (conducted by a well-known university) → 过去分词短语作定语，修饰 survey
      (that) → 宾语从句引导词
      (who read English articles regularly for at least six months) → 定语从句，修饰 students
      much → 程度状语
      (in reading) → 范围状语
      (than those who only do grammar exercises in class) → 比较状语从句
      (who only do grammar exercises in class) → 定语从句，修饰 those
直译  那项调查（一所知名大学做的）表明，（定期读英语文章至少六个月的学生）在阅读上取得大得多的进步，比起那些（只在课堂上做语法练习的人）。
调整  一所知名大学的调查表明：坚持定期阅读英语文章至少六个月的学生，阅读进步明显大于那些只在课堂上刷语法题的学生。
```

看点：35 词、4 个谓语（shows / read / make / do）。这正是考研、四六级阅读里"一眼绝望、拆开平常"的典型句型——每层都是你学过的东西，只是套了三层。

## 中文学习者常见误区

| ❌ 错误做法 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 遇到生词就停下来查 | 先数谓语、切结构，最后查词 | 结构清楚后，很多词义能从主干猜出来；先查词等于蒙眼拆弹 |
| that 一律当"那个" | 先看 that 后面：完整句 → 引导词；名词 → 指示代词 | that that book 里两个 that 身份完全不同 |
| 照英文语序直译到底 | 第⑤步必须重组（前移、调位、切短） | 中文定语在前、状语灵活，硬译必出翻译腔 |
| 一口气从第一个词读到最后 | 分句逐块击破，读一块消化一块 | 长句是积木不是流水；按分句读取，工作记忆才够用 |

## 实战练习

任务统一：按五步法完整拆解（原句 → 切分 → 主干 → 配件 → 直译/调整）。

**热身**

1. I was tired, so I went to bed early.
2. If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
3. She didn't come to the party because she was ill.

**标准**

4. The young engineer who fixed our printer last week has already moved to another city in the south.
5. When the long meeting finally ended, everyone in the room looked relieved and started to relax.
6. The hotel that we stayed at in Paris last summer was small but very comfortable.
7. Officials said that the bridge would be closed for repairs until the end of next month.

**挑战**

8. The manager explained that the project had been delayed because the materials arrived late and several workers were absent during the same week.
9. Anyone who wants to join the volunteer program should send an email to the office before the end of this month.
10. Researchers have found that children who eat breakfast every day tend to perform better in class than those who often skip it.

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**1.**

```
原句  I was tired, so I went to bed early.
切分  [I was tired,] [so I went to bed early.]
      └─ 分句① ─┘ └─ 分句②（so 结果）┘
主干  分句① I 【谓语】was tired；分句② I 【谓语】went to bed
配件  early → 状语
直译  我很累，所以我早早地睡觉了。
调整  我太累了，所以早点睡了。
```

**2.**

```
原句  If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
切分  [If it rains tomorrow,] [we will stay at home.]
      └─ 条件状语从句 ────┘ └─ 主句 ────────┘
主干  主句 we 【谓语】will stay；从句 it 【谓语】rains
配件  tomorrow → 从句内时间状语
      at home → 地点状语
直译  如果明天下雨，我们将待在家里。
调整  明天要是下雨，我们就待在家。
```

**3.**

```
原句  She didn't come to the party because she was ill.
切分  [She didn't come to the party] [because she was ill.]
      └─ 主句 ─────────────┘ └─ 原因状语从句 ┘
主干  主句 she 【谓语】didn't come；从句 she 【谓语】was ill
配件  (to the party) → 地点状语（come 的去向）
直译  她没有来参加聚会，因为她病了。
调整  她生病了，所以没来聚会。
```

看点：调整版把原因提到前面——中文习惯"先因后果"。这是第⑤步"状语调位"的标准操作。

**4.**

```
原句  The young engineer who fixed our printer last week has already moved to another city in the south.
切分  [The young engineer (who fixed our printer last week)] [has already moved] [to another city (in the south)].
      └─ 主语 + 定语从句 ────────────────┘ └─ 谓语 ─────┘ └─ 状语 + 定语 ────┘
主干  the engineer 【谓语】has moved
配件  young → 定语，修饰 engineer
      (who fixed our printer last week) → 定语从句，修饰 engineer
      already → 状语
      (to another city) → 地点状语
      (in the south) → 定语，修饰 city
直译  那位年轻工程师（上周修好我们打印机的）已经搬到了另一座城市（在南方的）。
调整  上周帮我们修好打印机的那位年轻工程师，已经搬去南方另一座城市了。
```

**5.**

```
原句  When the long meeting finally ended, everyone in the room looked relieved and started to relax.
切分  [When the long meeting finally ended,] [everyone (in the room)] [looked relieved] and [started to relax].
      └─ 时间状语从句 ─────────┘ └主语+定语┘ └─ 谓语① ─┘     └─ 谓语② ──┘
主干  everyone 【谓语】looked relieved and 【谓语】started to relax
      （两个并列谓语，共享一个主语 everyone）
配件  when → 引导时间状语从句
      long → 定语，修饰 meeting
      finally → 从句内状语
      (in the room) → 定语，修饰 everyone
直译  当漫长的会议终于结束，房间里的每个人看起来松了口气并开始放松。
调整  漫长的会议终于结束，屋里每个人都如释重负，开始放松下来。
```

**6.**

```
原句  The hotel that we stayed at in Paris last summer was small but very comfortable.
切分  [The hotel (that we stayed at in Paris last summer)] [was small but very comfortable.]
      └─ 主语 + 定语从句 ──────────────────┘ └─ 谓语 + 并列表语 ──────┘
主干  the hotel 【谓语】was small
配件  (that we stayed at in Paris last summer) → 定语从句，修饰 hotel
      ★ that 在从句中作 at 的宾语（stay at the hotel），所以可省略
      in Paris / last summer → 从句内地点、时间状语
      but very comfortable → 并列表语
直译  那家旅馆（我们去年夏天在巴黎住的）很小但是非常舒服。
调整  我们去年夏天在巴黎住的那家旅馆不大，但非常舒服。
```

**7.**

```
原句  Officials said that the bridge would be closed for repairs until the end of next month.
切分  [Officials said] [(that) the bridge would be closed for repairs (until the end of next month).]
      └─ 主句 ─┘ └─ 宾语从句 ──────────────────────────┘
主干  主句 officials 【谓语】said；从句 the bridge 【谓语】would be closed
配件  (that) → 宾语从句引导词
      (for repairs) → 目的状语（为维修而封闭）
      (until the end of next month) → 时间状语
直译  官员们说，那座桥将被关闭以维修直到下个月底。
调整  官方表示，大桥将封闭维修，一直到下月底。
```

**8.**

```
原句  The manager explained that the project had been delayed because the materials
      arrived late and several workers were absent during the same week.

切分  [The manager explained] [(that) the project had been delayed]
      └─ 主句 ─────────┘ └─ 宾语从句 ──────────────┘
      [because the materials arrived late and several workers were absent (during the same week).]
      └─ 原因状语从句（内部又是一个 and 并列）────────────────────┘

主干  主句 the manager 【谓语】explained；宾从 the project 【谓语】had been delayed
      原因从句内 分句① the materials 【谓语】arrived；分句② several workers 【谓语】were absent
配件  (that) → 宾语从句引导词
      because → 引导原因状语从句
      late → 方式状语
      (during the same week) → 时间状语
直译  经理解释说，项目被推迟了，因为材料到得晚，而且几个工人在同一周缺席。
调整  经理解释说，项目之所以延期，是因为材料到货晚，加上同一周还有几名工人缺勤。
```

**9.**

```
原句  Anyone who wants to join the volunteer program should send an email to the office before the end of this month.
切分  [Anyone (who wants to join the volunteer program)] [should send] [an email] [to the office] [before the end of this month].
      └─ 主语 + 定语从句 ────────────────┘ └─ 谓语 ─┘ └宾语┘ └─ 状语 ─┘ └─ 时间状语 ────────┘
主干  anyone 【谓语】should send an email
配件  (who wants to join the volunteer program) → 定语从句，修饰 anyone
      (to the office) → 对象状语
      (before the end of this month) → 时间状语
直译  任何人（想加入志愿者项目的）应该发一封邮件到办公室，在这个月底之前。
调整  想参加志愿者项目的人，应在本月底前给办公室发一封邮件。
```

**10.**

```
原句  Researchers have found that children who eat breakfast every day tend
      to perform better in class than those who often skip it.

切分  [Researchers have found] [(that) children (who eat breakfast every day) tend to perform
      └─ 主句 ───────────┘ └─ 宾语从句 ─────────────────────────────
      better (in class) (than those (who often skip it)).]
      ─────────────────────────────────────┘

主干  主句 researchers 【谓语】have found；宾从 children 【谓语】tend to perform
配件  (that) → 宾语从句引导词
      (who eat breakfast every day) → 定语从句，修饰 children
      (in class) → 地点状语
      (than those who often skip it) → 比较状语从句
      (who often skip it) → 定语从句，修饰 those
直译  研究者们已经发现，（每天吃早餐的孩子）倾向于在课堂上表现更好，比起那些（经常跳过它的人）。
调整  研究人员发现：每天吃早餐的孩子，课堂表现往往比经常不吃早餐的孩子更好。
```
