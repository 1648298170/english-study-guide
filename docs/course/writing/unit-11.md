> **预计学习时间**：4~6 小时，建议拆 3 天（第 1 天学图表类型与描述语言库，第 2 天学三段结构与句型库，第 3 天精读两篇范文并做练习） | **前置**：[写作课 · 第 10 课](./unit-10.md) | **难度**：B1+（写作课）

## 本课目标

- [ ] 我能分清柱状图、线图、饼图、表格各自擅长展示什么
- [ ] 我能按"平缓→剧烈"给变化动词和程度副词分档，说出 increase 和 soar 的力度差
- [ ] 我会用描述占比、平稳、波动的地道表达写数据句
- [ ] 我能按"引言改写 + 主体趋势 + 结论总结"三段结构写图表作文，并且**不写个人观点**
- [ ] 我能独立完成一篇柱状图或线图作文（150 词以上）

## 核心内容

图表作文只有一件事：**把图表里的数字翻译成文字，把变化写成故事**。四六级、考研英语二、雅思 Task 1 都考它。规则简单，语言精准即可得分。

### 一、图表类型速览

| 图表 | 英文名 | 长什么样 | 擅长展示 | 描述重点 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 柱状图 | bar chart | 一根根竖条比高矮 | **不同类别之间的大小比较** | 谁最高、谁最低、差距多少 |
| 线图 | line graph | 点连成的线爬坡下坡 | **随时间的变化趋势** | 上升下降、快慢、拐点 |
| 饼图 | pie chart | 一个圆切成几块 | **部分占整体的比例** | 谁占大头、占比变化 |
| 表格 | table | 行列交叉的数字格 | 任何数据，尤其多维度 | 先找最大最小值和变化方向 |

看图第一步永远是回答三个问题：**比什么？谁最大？怎么变？** 答完再动笔。

### 二、数据描述语言库

#### 上升趋势（按力度从平缓到剧烈分档）

| 档位 | 动词 | 力度感觉 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 温和 | increase, rise, grow | 稳稳上升，最通用 |
| 中等 | climb | 带一点"爬坡"的费劲感 |
| 明显 | jump, surge | 一眼可见的蹿升 |
| 剧烈 | soar, rocket, boom | 冲天火箭，必须配大数字 |

用法示例：The price **rose** slowly. / The price **climbed** steadily. / The price **soared** from 100 to 500.

#### 下降趋势（同样分档）

| 档位 | 动词 | 力度感觉 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 温和 | decrease, decline, fall | 平缓下滑 |
| 中等 | drop, go down | 明显下落 |
| 剧烈 | plummet, plunge | 跳崖式暴跌 |

用法示例：Sales **declined** gently. / Sales **dropped** sharply. / Sales **plummeted** to a record low.

#### 占比、平稳、波动

- **占比**：account for / make up / constitute（占……比例）
  - Online sales **account for** 40% of the total.（线上销售占总数的 40%）
- **平稳**：remain stable / remain steady / level off（保持平稳 / 趋平）
  - The figure **remained stable** at around 200 for five years.（数字五年稳定在 200 左右）
- **波动**：fluctuate / rise and fall repeatedly
  - Prices **fluctuated** between 30 and 50 during the decade.（十年间价格在三五十之间波动）

#### 程度副词分档

| 档位 | 副词 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 轻微 | slightly, marginally | 略微 |
| 中等 | steadily, moderately | 稳步、适度 |
| 显著 | significantly, markedly, considerably | 显著地 |
| 剧烈 | dramatically, sharply, rapidly | 急剧地 |

副词和动词要**同档匹配**：slightly soar 自相矛盾，plummet slightly 也是车祸现场。改完动词看副词，两档合一才算一句话成立。

#### 时间表达

- from 2010 to 2020（从 2010 到 2020）
- over the period / over the decade（在这一时期 / 这十年间）
- during the same period（同期）
- by 2020（到 2020 年为止，配完成时：had doubled）
- between 2010 and 2015（在 2010 到 2015 之间）

### 三、图表作文三段结构

以雅思 Task 1 为标准（四六级、考研英语二同样适用）：

```text
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 第一段 · 引言（1~2 句）                       │
│   改写题目：The chart shows... 换词复述       │
│   绝不照抄题目原句！                          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 第二段 · 主体（2~3 段，每段 3~5 句）          │
│   只写最重要的趋势：最大变化 + 对比 + 拐点    │
│   每个趋势必须有数据支撑（具体数字）          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 第三段 · 结论（1~2 句）                       │
│   总结总体趋势或最突出的发现                  │
│   ⚠️ 不写个人观点、不提建议、不喊口号         │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**三条铁律**：

1. **引言必改写**：题目说 The graph below shows the number of...，你要换成 The graph illustrates how the number of... changed...。原句照抄 = 任务没完成。
2. **数据是命根**：每个趋势句都要带具体数字，"上升了很多"不如"从 200 万上升到 800 万"。
3. **不给观点**：这是 Task 1 与 Task 2 的分水岭。图表作文里没有 I think，没有 should，没有 obviously good。**数据会说话，你只做翻译**。

### 四、句型库（直接套用）

**开篇句**：

- As is shown in the chart, ...（如图所示……）
- The chart illustrates the changes in... between... and...（该图展示了……在……期间的变化）
- The graph compares... across four categories.（该图比较了四类……）

**趋势句**（主语 + 动词 + 副词 + 数据）：

- The number of... witnessed a sharp increase, reaching 5 million in 2020.（……的数量急剧增长，2020 年达到 500 万）
- ... rose significantly from 20% to 65% over the period.（……期间从 20% 显著上升至 65%）
- There was a dramatic growth in..., which peaked at 800 in 2018.（……出现急剧增长，2018 年达到峰值 800）
- ... declined steadily, hitting a low of 1.2 million by the end of the decade.（……稳步下降，十年末触底 120 万）
- The figure for... remained stable at around 300 throughout the period.（……的数字全程稳定在 300 左右）

**对比句**：

- While... increased, ... decreased over the same period.（同期一升一降）
- ... was almost three times higher than that of...（……几乎是……的三倍）
- ... surpassed / overtook ... in 2015.（……在 2015 年反超了……）

**结尾句**：

- Overall, the chart reveals a clear upward trend in...（总体呈现明显上升趋势）
- In summary, ... remained the dominant category throughout the period.（……始终占据主导）

### 五、柱状图范文 1 篇

**题目**：The bar chart below shows the number of visitors to three museums in a city in 2015 and 2025 (in ten thousands).（原创数据：History Museum 120→180；Art Museum 90→260；Science Museum 150→140，单位：万人）

**范文**：

> The bar chart compares the number of visitors to three museums in one city in 2015 and 2025, measured in ten thousands.
>
> In 2015, the Science Museum was the most popular, attracting 1.5 million visitors, followed by the History Museum with 1.2 million, while the Art Museum received only 0.9 million. Over the following decade, however, the three museums experienced very different fortunes. The Art Museum witnessed a dramatic increase, with its visitors nearly tripling to 2.6 million, which made it the most visited museum in 2025. The History Museum also grew steadily, rising by half to 1.8 million. By contrast, the Science Museum saw a slight decline, dropping from 1.5 million to 1.4 million, and slipped from first to last place.
>
> Overall, the chart shows a sharp divergence: art rose to the top of the city's culture, while science lost its lead.

**点评**：第一段用 measured in ten thousands 完成题目改写；第二段先摆 2015 年排位，再写十年变化，nearly tripling（几乎翻三倍）和 slipping from first to last（从第一滑到末位）两个表达是亮点；结论只总结趋势、不解释原因、不给建议，严守翻译官本分。

### 六、线图范文 1 篇

**题目**：The line graph below shows the percentage of people in a town reading paper books and e-books from 2010 to 2020.（原创数据：纸书 70%→58%→45%→40%；电子书 10%→30%→48%→55%，每五年一档其中 2015、2020 为中间节点，2010/2012/2016/2020 四节点近似）

**范文**：

> The line graph illustrates how the popularity of paper books and e-books changed in one town between 2010 and 2020, measured in percentage of readers.
>
> At the start of the period, paper books dominated reading habits: 70% of readers chose them, whereas e-books were read by a mere 10%. The two lines then moved in opposite directions. The figure for e-books climbed steadily to 30% by 2014 and continued to surge, overtaking paper books around 2016 and reaching 55% in 2020. Paper books, on the other hand, declined consistently throughout the decade, falling from 70% to 40%, although the drop slowed down noticeably in the final years.
>
> Overall, the decade saw a complete reversal of reading habits, with e-books replacing paper books as the town's main reading choice.

**点评**：overtaking... around 2016 精准点出交叉拐点，这是线图作文的关键得分动作；whereas 和 on the other hand 两处对比让双线叙事不乱；结尾 a complete reversal（彻底逆转）一词收拢全篇，依然零观点。

## 常见误区

| ❌ 错误示范 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 引言照抄题目：The chart below shows the number of visitors... | 改写：The chart compares visitor numbers to... | 照抄题目不计入字数也不计分，等于第一段白写。至少换两个词：show→illustrate/compare，number→figure |
| The number increased very big. | The number increased significantly / soared. | increase 不与 big 搭配，这是中式直译"增长很大"。程度用副词 significantly/sharply 表达 |
| 全文堆数据：2015 年 100 万，2016 年 120 万，2017 年 145 万…… | 选最大、最小、拐点、反超等关键数据，其余归入趋势句 | 图表作文考的是"筛选与概括"，不是听写数字。八组数据里挑三组重点，剩下的用 steadily 之类的趋势词打包 |
| I think the government should build more museums.（结论段） | Overall, the chart reveals a clear upward trend in... | Task 1 不给观点不提建议。你一开口"我认为该怎样"，任务边界就破了，直接掉档 |
| The price soared slightly from 100 to 102. | The price rose slightly from 100 to 102. | 动词档位（soar 剧烈）与副词档位（slightly 轻微）打架，句子自相矛盾。写完每个趋势句都检查"动词副词是否同档" |

## 实战练习

**A. 词汇分档（第 1~3 题）**

1. 把下列动词按"温和→剧烈"排序：surge, rise, plummet, decline, rocket, drop
2. 给下面每组配上同档副词：①climb（中等）②plummet（剧烈）③increase（轻微）
3. 翻译三句话（用上分档词）：①价格略微下降。②游客数量急剧飙升。③占比十年间保持平稳。

**B. 数据句改写（第 4~7 题）**

4. 用 account for 改写：Online shopping was 35% of all retail sales.
5. 用 witness 改写：The number of cyclists grew sharply and reached 2 million in 2020.
6. 用 There was a... 句型改写：Car ownership fell dramatically between 2010 and 2020.
7. 指出这句的档位矛盾并改正：The population of the town plummeted slightly from 50,000 to 48,000.

**C. 拐点与交叉（第 8 题）**

8. 某图中 A 线 2010 年 20%、2015 年 45%、2020 年 60%；B 线 2010 年 50%、2015 年 43%、2020 年 30%。用两句话写出：①两条线何时交叉 ②交叉前后各发生了什么（用 overtake / surpass）。

**D. 段落与整篇（第 9~11 题）**

9. 写一个"双线对比"主体段（4~5 句）：某国 2010-2020 年出国游（outbound travel）从 15% 升到 38%，国内游从 60% 降到 41%，数据自编合理即可。
10. **整篇实战（柱状图）**：The bar chart shows the preferred ways of learning English among students in one university in 2015 and 2025: mobile apps (30%→68%), paper dictionaries (45%→12%), online courses (25%→20%)。写 150 词左右完整作文（引言改写 + 主体 + 结论）。
11. **整篇实战（线图）**：The line graph shows a city's air quality index (AQI) and the number of cyclists from 2010 to 2020: AQI 160→90（持续下降），骑行人数 2 万→9 万（持续上升）。写 150 词左右完整作文，结论可点出两线的关系但**不提建议**。

**E. 语病特训（第 12 题）**

12. 下面的图表作文片段有 4 处错误（档位矛盾、中式搭配、观点越界、照抄题式），找出并改正：

> As is shown in the chart, the chart shows the sales of phones. The sales increased very quickly and big in 2015. I believe phone companies should sell cheaper phones. Overall, the sales decreased slightly.

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看参考答案与点评</summary>

**第 1 题**：温和→剧烈：rise（温和升）/ decline（温和降），drop（中降）/ surge（剧升），plummet（剧降）/ rocket（剧升）。
排序（混合升降各按力度）：rise, decline → drop, surge → plummet, rocket。
点评：六词两两一组最好记：rise/decline 一组温和，surge/drop 一组中等偏强，rocket/plummet 一组顶级力度（一升一降）。

**第 2 题**：①climbed steadily / moderately ②plummeted dramatically / sharply ③increased slightly / marginally
点评：副词跟动词必须住在同一档。背的时候成对背："climb steadily" 而不是孤立背两个词。

**第 3 题**：
- The price decreased slightly. / The price fell marginally.
- The number of visitors soared dramatically.（或 surged sharply）
- The share remained stable throughout the decade.
点评：第三句的"占比"用 the share / the percentage / the proportion 都行，remain stable 是"平稳"专用搭配。

**第 4 题**：Online shopping **accounted for** 35% of all retail sales.（或 Online shopping **made up** 35% of total retail sales.）
点评：account for 是图表作文第一高频短语，注意过去式 accounted。

**第 5 题**：The number of cyclists **witnessed a sharp increase, reaching 2 million in 2020.**
点评：witness 让"数字"当主语，句子立刻书面化；reaching 伴随状语顺手带出终点数据，一句两用。

**第 6 题**：**There was a dramatic fall/decline in** car ownership between 2010 and 2020.
点评：There be 句型是动词句的安全备胎——想不出动词时，把动词名词化（fall/decline/growth/rise）就行。

**第 7 题**：矛盾在 plummeted（跳崖式暴跌）配 slightly（略微），且数据只降 4%。改正：The population **declined slightly** from 50,000 to 48,000.
点评：判断标准看数字幅度：4% 属轻微档。动词选剧烈档时，数字必须惊人（比如砍半）。

**第 8 题**（参考）：The two lines crossed around 2015, when A overtook B for the first time. Before that, B had stayed well above A, but after the crossover A continued climbing while B fell steadily, and by 2020 A was twice as high as B.
点评：线图的高分点全在拐点：crossed / overtook / before that / after the crossover，四个表达把交叉前后讲得明明白白。

**第 9 题**（参考段）：
> The two travel styles moved in opposite directions over the decade. Outbound travel rose steadily from 15% to 38%, as flights became cheaper and visas easier. By contrast, domestic travel declined from 60% to 41%, although it remained the more popular choice throughout the period. It is worth noting that the gap between the two narrowed from 45 to just 3 percentage points, and outbound travel was on the way to overtaking domestic travel by the end of the decade.
点评：最后一句"差距从 45 个百分点缩到 3 个"是数据作文的高级动作——自己算出差值，展示概括能力。

**第 10 题**（参考范文）：
> The bar chart compares three ways of learning English among students at one university in 2015 and 2025.
>
> Mobile apps were the biggest story of the decade. Their share more than doubled, climbing from 30% to 68%, which made them the dominant choice by 2025. Paper dictionaries suffered the sharpest fall, with their share dropping from 45% to a mere 12%, even though they had been the most popular tool in 2015. Online courses, by contrast, changed little, remaining at a similar level of around 20% throughout.
>
> Overall, the chart reveals a clear shift from traditional tools to mobile learning, with apps replacing paper dictionaries as students' first choice.
点评：more than doubled / a mere 12% / the biggest story 都是概括力加分项；三项数据按"变化最大→最小"排序，而不是按图表原始顺序复述，这正是"筛选"的体现。

**第 11 题**（参考范文）：
> The line graph shows the relationship between air quality and cycling in one city from 2010 to 2020, with the AQI falling and cyclist numbers rising throughout.
>
> At the beginning of the period, the city suffered serious air pollution, with an AQI of 160, while only 20,000 residents cycled regularly. As the AQI declined steadily to 90 by 2020, the number of cyclists climbed continuously, reaching 90,000, more than four times the 2010 figure. The two decades' movements mirror each other almost perfectly: every improvement in air quality was accompanied by a further rise in cycling.
>
> Overall, the graph shows two encouraging trends moving in opposite directions, with cleaner air going hand in hand with a growing cycling population.
点评：结论只陈述两线的同步关系（mirror each other / hand in hand），没有写"政府应该继续治理空气"——任务边界守住了。注意"因果暗示"可以（was accompanied by），"政策建议"不行。

**第 12 题**：四处错误——
① 照抄题式：As is shown in the chart, the chart shows... 重复啰嗦，改正：The chart compares the sales of phones over the period.（引言只需一种"展示"表达）
② 中式搭配：increased very quickly and big → rose sharply（big 不能作副词）
③ 观点越界：I believe phone companies should sell cheaper phones → 删除（Task 1 无观点无建议）
④ 档位矛盾：decreased slightly 无数据支撑且与 quickly 矛盾，改正要配数据：fell from 5 million to 3 million。
点评：这四类错误覆盖了图表作文八成的失分点，写完自查这四条，等于多了一道保险。
