前五课你解决了"写对句子、组好段落、接顺逻辑"，今天给文章穿上像样的衣服：词汇。一篇通篇 good、bad、important、I think 的作文，就像一套逻辑严密却穿睡衣的发言。但要先立一条军规：词汇升级不是堆大词，是换准词。这一课给你三样东西：高频词升级表、搭配表、正式度匹配表，外加一条"3 个高级词就够用"的克制心法。

## 本课目标

学完本课，你可以：

- [ ] 用同义替换策略升级 15 组高频词，并根据场景选择最贴切的那个
- [ ] 说出搭配意识是什么，改正 20 组高频动宾 / 形名搭配错误
- [ ] 根据文体（考场作文、正式邮件）调整词汇正式度
- [ ] 用"准确 > 高级、贴切 > 华丽、3 个亮点够用"三原则给自己的段落做词汇手术

## 核心内容

### 一、同义替换策略：一个词的面具箱

为什么阅卷人看见 good 就皱眉？不是因为 good 错，而是因为它什么都能形容，等于什么都没说。升级的思路不是"背更大的词"，是"按场景选更准的词"。good 有三个常用替补，分工不同：

- beneficial（对健康、学习有益）：Regular review is beneficial to long-term memory.
- favorable（条件、环境有利）：The policy created a favorable environment for small businesses.
- rewarding（经历值得、有回报）：Teaching adults to write is a rewarding job.

看懂了吗？同一个"好"，落在"好处、环境、经历"三个场景，用的词完全不同。这就是场景分用。高频词升级表，每组给 2~3 个替补 + 示范：

| 基础词 | 升级替补 | 场景示范 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| good | beneficial / favorable / rewarding |（见上） |
| bad | negative / adverse / harmful | Smoking has harmful effects on sleep. / adverse weather conditions |
| important | crucial / vital / essential | Sleep is essential for memory. / a crucial question |
| think | argue / believe / maintain / hold the view that | Some experts argue that remote work reduces creativity. |
| big | significant / considerable / substantial | a significant change in consumer habits |
| small | slight / minor / limited | a slight improvement / minor problems |
| many | numerous / a variety of / a number of | numerous opportunities |
| very | extremely / highly / particularly | a highly effective method |
| get | obtain / acquire / receive | obtain a certificate / acquire new skills / receive feedback |
| make | create / produce / generate | generate jobs / produce results |
| show | demonstrate / reveal / indicate | The survey indicates that most students prefer morning classes. |
| help | assist / support / contribute to | Regular practice contributes to steady progress. |
| problem | issue / challenge / concern | a key concern for parents |
| famous | well-known / renowned | a renowned university |
| happy | delighted / pleased / satisfied | delighted to share this news with you |

think 那一行值得单独说：议论文里 I think 换成 I would argue that / I believe that，气势立刻不同；引用别人观点用 Some experts argue that...，maintain 和 hold the view that 是更书面的替补。

### 二、搭配意识：单词不成家，词组才是家

第 3 课病例 3、4（price expensive、learn knowledge）的病根就是搭配。记住：**英语的地道单位不是单词，是搭配**。heavy rain 不能说 big rain，这不是逻辑问题，是人家祖祖辈辈就这么说。高频搭配速查表，左列是中文思维的高发直译，右列是地道搭配：

| ❌ 直译版 | ✅ 地道搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| big rain | heavy rain | The flight was delayed by heavy rain. |
| do measures | take measures | The city has taken measures to cut traffic. |
| do a decision | make a decision | It is hard to make a decision under pressure. |
| make research | do / conduct research | The team conducted research on sleep habits. |
| learn knowledge | gain / acquire knowledge | Travel helps us gain knowledge about other cultures. |
| open the light | turn on the light | He turned on the light and sat down. |
| play the phone | use my phone / spend time on my phone | I spent two hours on my phone last night. |
| the price is cheap | the price is low | The hotel offers lower prices in winter. |
| powerful coffee | strong coffee | I need a cup of strong coffee. |
| crowded traffic | heavy traffic | Heavy traffic delayed us for an hour. |
| do progress | make progress | You are making steady progress. |
| do mistakes | make mistakes | Making mistakes is part of learning. |
| write diary | keep a diary | I have kept a diary for three years. |
| do sports | play sports / exercise | We play basketball every Friday. |
| learn experience | gain experience | She gained experience in a local company. |
| big opportunity | great / golden opportunity | Studying abroad is a golden opportunity. |
| many informations | a lot of information | The website offers a lot of useful information.（information 不可数） |
| many advices | advice（不可数） | Thank you for your advice. |
| make effort（缺冠词） | make an effort | He made an effort to arrive on time. |
| pay attention on | pay attention to | Please pay attention to the deadline. |

背法建议：别背单词，背"对子"。每学一个新动词，顺手记它的 2~3 个常用宾语（take: measures / a break / responsibility）；每学一个名词，记它的形容词搭档（rain: heavy / light；coffee: strong / weak）。

### 三、词组优于单词：给句子换节奏

有时一个"动词 + 名词"的词组比单个大词更自然、更有节奏感，也更容易写对：

| 单词版 | 词组版 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| try | make an effort | The school made an effort to help slow learners. |
| use | make use of | We should make use of free online resources. |
| join | take part in | Over two hundred students took part in the competition. |
| affect | have an influence on | Diet has a strong influence on mood. |
| cause | lead to / give rise to | Poor sleep can lead to poor memory. |
| consider | take ... into consideration | The school takes students' needs into consideration. |
| matter | play a role in | Reading plays a central role in language learning. |

注意方向：不是"词组永远更好"，而是**长短交错**。一整段全是大词会喘不过气，偶尔用一个词组让句子落地。play a role in 和 have an influence on 这两个词组在考场出现率极高，建议整句背下。

### 四、正式度匹配：什么场合穿什么衣服

词汇分正式度。考场上、正式邮件里，把口语词换成书面词，是最低成本的升级：

| 口语版 | 书面版 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| get | obtain / receive | Students who pass will receive a certificate. |
| a lot of | a great deal of（+不可数）/ a large number of（+可数） | She saved a great deal of time. |
| kids | children | Children learn languages faster than adults. |
| find out | discover / identify | The study identified three main causes. |
| look into | investigate | The committee investigated the complaint. |
| ask for | request | You may request a full refund within seven days. |
| thing | factor / aspect / issue | Price is only one factor in the decision. |

写投诉信、申请信、报告时，这张表尤其值钱。⚠️ 同时记住匹配原则：正式度要全篇统一。一封信里前一句 You know 后一句 notwithstanding，比通篇口语更怪。定稿前扫一遍，把文体"校准"到同一档。

### 五、词汇升级三原则

1. **准确 > 高级**。一个用错的高级词是硬伤，扣分比普通词狠；一个用对的普通词零风险。没把握的词别上考场。
2. **贴切 > 华丽**。heavy rain 永远比 a precipitation event of significant intensity 好（后者根本不是人话）。升级要贴着场景走（回到第一节的场景分用）。
3. **3 个高级词够用**。一篇 150 词的作文，3~5 处准确的亮点（一个好动词、一个地道搭配、一个书面替换）足以让阅卷人标注"语言丰富"。通篇亮点的文章像挂满圣诞树，谁都不信。

### 六、词汇手术示范：差版段落升级全记录

**手术台上的差版**：

> I think online learning is very good. It can help students learn a lot of knowledge. Also, it is very convenient. Students can study at any time. In a word, online learning is important for us.

诊断：I think、very good、learn knowledge（搭配错）、very convenient、In a word（中式总结腔）、important，通篇基础词，还有一处硬伤搭配。

**手术后**：

> In my view, online learning is highly beneficial. It enables students to gain a wide range of knowledge. Moreover, it offers remarkable convenience: learners can study whenever it suits them. Clearly, online learning plays a vital role in modern education.

手术记录（每刀都有理由）：

| 原文 | 换成 | 手术类型 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I think | In my view | 观点句正式化 |
| very good | highly beneficial | 场景分用：说"有益"用 beneficial |
| help students learn a lot of knowledge | enables students to gain a wide range of knowledge | 修搭配硬伤 + 词组升级 |
| Also, it is very convenient | Moreover, it offers remarkable convenience | 口语连词换书面 + 名词化 |
| Students can study at any time | learners can study whenever it suits them | whenever 从句增加句式变化 |
| In a word | Clearly | 去中式总结腔 |
| important | plays a vital role in | 词组化升级 |

数一数：7 处手术，正好落在"3~5 处亮点，外加修掉硬伤"的区间。对比朗读两版，同样的内容，气质完全不同。这就是你要在考场上做的事。

## 常见误区

| ❌ 误区做法 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 大词 = 高分，生僻词往上堆 | 准确自然 = 高分，没把握的词不用 | 用错的高级词是硬伤；阅卷人一眼识破背词表的人 |
| 全文处处升级，句句亮点 | 3~5 处亮点就够，其余用准普通词 | 通篇华丽反而失真，还增加出错面 |
| 换词不换搭配（obtain experience ❌） | 换词连带检查搭配（gain experience ✅） | 搭配跟着词走，升级词常带新搭档 |
| 口语词和书面词混在一篇 | 定稿前统一校准正式度 | 文体混搭比通篇朴素更扎眼 |

## 实战练习

### 第一档 · 选词升级（第 1~4 题，选出最合适的一项）

1. The new policy has a(n) ______ effect on small businesses.
   - A. bad  B. adverse  C. worse
2. The survey ______ that most students prefer morning classes.
   - A. shows  B. indicates  C. speaks
3. Taking notes by hand is ______ for memory.
   - A. good  B. beneficial  C. nice
4. Some experts ______ that remote work reduces creativity.
   - A. think  B. argue  C. guess

### 第二档 · 搭配改错（第 5~8 题，改正每句中的搭配错误）

5. We must do measures to protect the river.
6. There was a big rain last night, and the streets were flooded.
7. She made research on study habits for two years.
8. Thank you for your many advices.

### 第三档 · 升级与产出（第 9~12 题）

9. 一句三刀：把 I think reading is very good for students. 至少升级两处（think 和 very good 都换掉，注意搭配）。
10. 段落手术：给下面这段差版做词汇手术，要求至少升级 6 处，并修掉所有搭配硬伤：

    > I think morning study is very good. It can help students get a lot of knowledge. Also, a quiet morning is very good for thinking. In a word, morning study is important.

11. 小作文：用本课升级表里的 3 个词（或词组），写 5 句"Why I am learning English"，要求每句至少 8 词，风格统一。
12. 建卡任务：从你最近写过的作文里挑出重复率最高的 5 个词（大概率是 good / important / think / very / a lot of），给每个建一张"升级卡"：正面写基础词，背面写 2 个升级替补 + 一个自己写的例句。

## 练习答案

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**第 1 题**：B（adverse effect 是固定搭配，形容政策、条件、影响，正式书面）。A 的 bad effect 语法不错但过于口语；C 的 worse 是比较级，句中没有比较对象。

**第 2 题**：B（indicate 正式书面，survey / study / data 的标准搭档）。A 的 shows 语法正确但平淡（真题风格里 survey indicates 更常见）；C 的 speaks 搭配错误，survey 不能"speak"。

**第 3 题**：B（be beneficial for / to 是"有益于"的标准书面表达）。A 和 C 语法没错，但这道题练的就是升级意识。

**第 4 题**：B（argue 是引用、陈述观点的书面动词）。C 的 guess 是"瞎猜"，用在专家身上语义不合。

**第 5 题**：We must take measures to protect the river.

点评：measure 的动词搭档是 take，措施"拿"不"做"。顺手记 take measures to do sth 整个词组。

**第 6 题**：There was heavy rain last night, and the streets were flooded.（或 It rained heavily last night...）

点评：雨的搭档是 heavy / light。 flooded 是"被淹没"的现成分词，原句用得就对，值得保留。

**第 7 题**：She did research on study habits for two years.（升级版：She conducted research on study habits for two years.）

点评：research 的搭档是 do / conduct / carry out，唯独不是 make。

**第 8 题**：Thank you for your advice.（或 Thank you for all your advice.）

点评：advice 不可数，没有复数、不挂 many。同类不可数高频词：information、furniture、progress（make progress 不加 s）。

**第 9 题**：参考：In my view, reading is highly beneficial for students. / I believe that reading brings students real benefits.

点评：think → In my view / I believe that；very good → highly beneficial / real benefits。注意第二版用 brings...benefits 顺手秀了双宾结构，一次升级两处得分点。

**第 10 题**：参考手术版：

> In my view, morning study is highly rewarding. It enables students to gain a wide range of knowledge. Moreover, a quiet morning offers an ideal environment for clear thinking. Clearly, morning study plays a vital role in effective learning.

手术对照：I think → In my view；very good → highly rewarding（说"有回报的经历"用 rewarding，第二处的 very good for thinking 则升级为 offers an ideal environment，避开重复）；help students get a lot of knowledge → enables students to gain a wide range of knowledge（修 get knowledge 的搭配软肋）；Also → Moreover；In a word → Clearly；important → plays a vital role in。共 6 刀以上，正式度统一。

评分自检点：① 是否至少升级 6 处；② 是否没有任何新增语法错误；③ 搭配是否全部成立（gain knowledge、ideal environment）；④ 正式度是否统一（没有残留 a lot of、very good 这类口语词）。

**第 11 题**：参考范例：

- I am learning English because it plays a central role in my career plan.
- Reading English news daily is beneficial to my vocabulary.
- A second language gives me access to a wider world.
- I believe that steady practice leads to steady progress.
- Clearly, this skill will remain valuable for decades.

评分自检点：① 是否准确用了 3 个以上升级词 / 词组（plays a role in / beneficial / leads to / valuable）；② 每句是否至少 8 词且风格统一书面；③ 是否避开了 good、very、I think 三个基础词；④ 五句是否句式有变化（不全是 I 开头）。

**第 12 题**：开放任务。示例卡片：

```text
正面：good
背面：beneficial（对健康学习）/ rewarding（经历有回报）
例句：Keeping a diary in English is a rewarding habit.
```

点评：升级卡的价值在于"例句是你自己写的"，带着你的语境，记忆比任何词表都牢。五张卡建好后贴在写作本内页，每次写作前扫一眼，升级词的出场率会肉眼可见地上升。
