> 📅 预计用时：4~6 小时 · 建议拆 3 天 | 🎯 难度：B1+（写作课） | 📚 前置：[第 4 课](./unit-04.md) · [B1 第 11 课](../b1/unit-11.md)（语法主线收尾）；配合语法详解课食用更佳

上节课你学会了搭汉堡包，句子有了分工。但把六个句子端上桌还不够，它们得像齿轮一样咬合：句与句之间要有看得见的"咬合装置"。这就是今天的主题，衔接（cohesion）与连贯（coherence）。雅思把它列为四大评分维度之一（CC），四六级阅卷人 30 秒扫结构时，靠的也是这些信号。这一课给你一整套连接词弹药库，外加指代、平行、代词一致三种"隐形咬合"技术。

## 本课目标

学完本课，你可以：

- [ ] 按六大功能（递进、转折、因果、举例、对比、总结）调用 30 个以上连接词，并放对标点
- [ ] 用 this / that / such + 总结名词做指代衔接，消灭通篇重复名词
- [ ] 识别并写出平行结构（not only... but also 等），保持代词一致
- [ ] 把一段"散沙文"改造成咬合顺畅的连贯段落
- [ ] 判断连接词是否过度堆砌，做到用词克制

## 核心内容

### 一、衔接与连贯：两件不同的事

先分清两个词：

- **连贯（coherence）是逻辑层**： ideas 本身有顺序，先因后果、先观点后例子，读者跟着思路走不绕路。
- **衔接（cohesion）是语言层**：把这种逻辑用看得见的装置标出来，连接词、指代词、重复的关键词。

打个比方：连贯是路修得直，衔接是路牌立得清。路不直，路牌立满也没用（这就是后面要讲的"堆砌警告"）；路直了，关键路口没路牌，读者照样迷路。两者配合，文章才顺。

### 二、连接词六大功能弹药库

按功能分六类背，考场上按"我想干什么"取用。每类给出常用词和例句，⚠️ 注意标点提示。

**第一类：递进（在已有理由上加码）**

| 连接词 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| furthermore | Furthermore, the plan cuts long-term costs.（此外，该计划削减长期成本。） |
| moreover | Moreover, online courses are often free. |
| in addition | In addition, students can replay lessons at any time. |
| also（句中位置） | Reading also improves writing.（also 放实义动词前） |
| what is more | What is more, the app works offline. |
| not to mention | The trip was cheap, not to mention the amazing food.（更别说还有美食） |

用法提示：furthermore / moreover 语气正式，是议论文主力；句首使用时后面加逗号。

**第二类：转折（踩刹车，换方向）**

| 连接词 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| however | The idea sounds simple. However, putting it into practice is hard. |
| nevertheless | The task was difficult; nevertheless, we finished it on time. |
| nonetheless | The results were limited. Nonetheless, the study is worth reading. |
| yet（连词直连） | It is a simple method, yet it works well.（yet 前用逗号，不用句号） |
| still | The price is high. Still, many students buy it. |

用法提示：however 句首时后接逗号；nevertheless / nonetheless 更书面，同一个句子只用一个。

**第三类：因果（推理的传动轴）**

| 连接词 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| therefore | The evidence is clear; therefore, we should act now. |
| consequently | He skipped the basics. Consequently, his progress was slow. |
| as a result | As a result, more adults are studying online. |
| thus（正式） | Thus, reading and writing grow together. |
| hence（正式） | The demand is rising; hence, new courses keep appearing. |
| for this reason | For this reason, I start my day with English. |

用法提示：therefore / thus / hence 是"推理"专用（前提 → 结论），比 because 的语气更正式；句首使用加逗号。

**第四类：举例（把抽象变具体）**

| 连接词 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| for example | For example, a ten-minute podcast fits any commute. |
| for instance | For instance, my cousin learned Spanish from videos alone. |
| such as（+名词） | Cheap resources, such as podcasts and e-books, are everywhere. |
| take ... as an example | Take my father as an example: he began learning English at fifty. |
| a case in point | A case in point is the rise of study apps.（一个现成的例子） |

⚠️ 高发错误：such as 后面只能跟名词或动名词（such as reading and swimming），不能跟完整句子；要跟句子用 for example。

**第五类：对比（两条路并排看）**

| 连接词 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| in contrast | City life is fast. In contrast, village life follows the seasons. |
| on the other hand | Online learning is flexible. On the other hand, it demands self-discipline. |
| whereas（连词） | Some prefer paper books, whereas others like e-readers. |
| while（连词） | While exams measure accuracy, real communication measures fluency. |
| unlike（+名词） | Unlike his brother, Tom enjoys public speaking. |
| by comparison | By comparison, the second method is cheaper. |

用法提示：whereas / while 是连词，直接连接前后两个分句，中间逗号隔开，前面不用句号。

**第六类：总结（收网）**

| 连接词 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| in conclusion | In conclusion, small daily habits beat rare big efforts. |
| to sum up | To sum up, the benefits clearly outweigh the risks. |
| in short | In short, practice is the shortest path. |
| all in all | All in all, it was a rewarding semester. |
| in brief | In brief, the plan has three strengths. |
| on the whole | On the whole, student feedback was positive. |

用法提示：总结词只出现在结论段或段尾，一篇文章用一次就够。in conclusion 最正式，四六级考研结尾段首选。

### 三、指代衔接：this / that / such 的隐形丝线

连接词是明线，指代是暗线。技巧：用 this / such + 一个"总结名词"接住上文，避免同一个名词反复出现。

先看病句（名词复读机）：

> Online learning is flexible. Online learning is also cheap. Online learning helps busy people.

改后（丝线缝合）：

> Online learning is flexible. It is also cheap, and this convenience helps busy people.（It 指代 online learning，this convenience 把前面两个优点打包带走）

指代模板：this / that / such + [总结名词]。总结名词是收纳箱：method（方法）、approach（路子）、trend（趋势）、problem（问题）、advantage（好处）、change（变化）。

- Many schools now teach with tablets. This approach saves paper but creates new problems of distraction.
- Some learners copy model essays word for word. Such a habit brings quick scores but slow growth.

指代的高级感在于：它证明你的每个句子都"记得"上一句说了什么，这正是 CC 维度要考核的能力。

### 四、平行结构：让句子左右对称

平行结构（parallelism）指并列的成分用同一种形式，像对仗一样整齐有力。核心规则：**and / or / not only...but also 连接的东西必须长得一样**。

```text
名词对名词：  I need a pen, a notebook, and a quiet room.
动名词对动名词：I enjoy reading, writing, and hiking.
不定式对不定式：He wants to travel, to learn, and to teach.
```

错误示范与修正：

- ❌ I enjoy reading, to write, and hiking. → ✅ I enjoy reading, writing, and hiking.
- ❌ Not only the course is cheap but also it is useful. → ✅ The course is not only cheap but also useful.（cheap 和 useful 对称）
- ✅ 进阶版（倒装）：Not only does it save time, but it also saves money.（not only 放句首时助动词提前，考场上用一次很出彩）

平行用在列举理由时威力最大：The policy saves time, cuts costs, and reduces stress. 三个动词短语并排，节奏干脆，像鼓点。

### 五、代词一致：别中途换人

段落里的人称要稳定，we / you / they 别互相乱串：

- ❌ When we study online, you can pause the video anytime.（前半句 we，后半句变 you，读者不知道"你"是谁）
- ✅ When we study online, we can pause the video anytime.

另外，代词必须有明确的"主人"（先行词）：

- ❌ My mother asked my teacher for advice, but she refused.（she 是谁？妈妈拒绝还是老师拒绝？）
- ✅ My mother asked my teacher for advice, but the teacher refused.（关键信息处直接复用名词，牺牲一点简洁换清楚）

### 六、实战改造：把"散沙文"缝成连贯段

看一段典型的散沙文（每句都对，但互不相认）：

> Many adults return to study. Studying online is popular. Online courses are flexible. Adults have jobs. Adults have families. Online courses let adults study at night. Some online courses are free. Free courses lower the cost of learning.

八个短句，八粒沙子。改造三步：合并同类（jobs + families 并成一组）、装上指代与连词、显化因果。改后：

> Many adults now return to study, and most of them choose online courses. The main reason is flexibility: because these learners often have jobs and families, they need to study at night, and online courses make this possible. Moreover, the cost is low, as many such courses are even free. For busy adults, therefore, online learning is often the only realistic choice.

改造笔记：

| 技术动作 | 对应位置 |
| --- | --- |
| 合并同类短句 | Adults have jobs. Adults have families. → because these learners often have jobs and families |
| 指代缝合 | them / they / this / such courses 全部指向前文 |
| 因果显化 | The main reason is... / as many such courses are even free |
| 连接词归位 | and / Moreover / therefore 各司其职 |
| 收网句 | therefore 把全段逻辑收进最后一句 |

对照读两版，你会发现内容一个字没多，只是"齿轮咬上了"。这就是衔接的力量。

### 七、堆砌警告：连接词不是越多越好

新手学会连接词后最常见的翻车：每句开头都立一个路牌。

> Moreover, I like English songs. However, I also like English movies. In addition, I read English news every day. Therefore, my English is improving. Furthermore, I plan to take an exam next year. In conclusion, I love English.

六个路牌，读者晕路。原则：**连接词是为已有逻辑服务的路标，大约每 2~3 句一个即可**；相邻句子如果本就是并列流水（songs、movies、news），用 and 或者干脆什么都不用，反而清爽。路修直了再立牌，立牌立在转弯处。

## 常见误区

| ❌ 误区做法 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 连接词越多越连贯，每句开头放一个 | 每 2~3 句一个，立在逻辑转弯处 | 堆砌路牌等于没有路牌，机械感还扣 CC 分 |
| however 和 but 随便互换 | 但书：but 轻便口语，however 句首加逗号更正式 | 两者语法位置不同，乱换会出现标点错误 |
| such as 后面跟完整句子 | such as + 名词 / 动名词；跟句子用 for example | such as 是介词性短语，带不动一整句 |
| 代词先行词模糊也懒得管 | 关键信息处复用名词，次要处用代词 | 指代不清读者要回头猜，CC 直接掉档 |

## 实战练习

### 第一档 · 选词填空（第 1~5 题，每空一词，从本课词库选）

用合适的连接词填空（注意大小写和标点）：

1. More people now study on their phones. ______, short lessons fit into small pieces of free time, like a bus ride.
2. A phone is always with you. ______, learning can happen anywhere, even in a queue.
3. Some learners open apps without clear goals. ______, they soon lose interest and quit.
4. Clear goals matter more than screen time. ______, learners should plan each week before they study.
5. ______, the phone is a good teacher, but only for learners with a plan.

### 第二档 · 句子手术（第 6~10 题）

6. 用指代技术改写，消灭重复的 study apps：Many students download study apps. The study apps promise quick progress, but the study apps rarely work as the study apps should.
7. 修正平行结构错误：I enjoy reading, to write, and running.
8. 修正代词不一致：When we prepare for an exam, you should sleep well the night before.
9. 合并句对（两句并一句，用括号里的连接词）：
   - ① The bus is cheap. The bus is often slow. (but)
   - ② He practiced listening daily. He improved quickly. (as a result)
10. 把下句中的 but 改写成 However 引导的句子（注意标点）：The course is cheap, but the quality is high.

### 第三档 · 成段改造（第 11~12 题）

11. 散沙改造：把下面的散沙段改写成连贯段落，要求至少用 1 次指代（this/such + 名词）、1 个连接词、1 次因果显化：

    > City bikes are becoming popular. City bikes are cheap to use. A ride often costs less than a bottle of water. People worry about safety. Some riders do not wear helmets. Cities are building more bike lanes. Bike lanes make riding safer.

12. 连接词体检：下面这段连接词严重堆砌。删掉多余的连接词（最多保留 1 个），改写成自然流畅的版本：

    > Moreover, I like English songs. However, I also like English movies. In addition, I read English news every day. Therefore, my English is improving. Furthermore, I plan to take an exam next year. In conclusion, I love English.

**第 1~5 题**：参考答案（其他合理选择同样给分）：

1. For example（后面是"公交车上学习的具体场景"，典型举例信号）
2. Moreover / In addition（在"随时学"之上加码"到处学"）
3. However / As a result（逻辑上两者都通：However 强调转折，As a result 强调"没目标所以放弃"的因果；选哪个取决于你想强调哪种关系，这正是连接词的意义）
4. Therefore / For this reason（前句给原则，后句给行动建议，典型推理）
5. In conclusion / To sum up（收网句）

点评：第 3 题的"两可"值得琢磨：连接词的选择就是逻辑关系的选择。写作前想清楚两句之间是转折还是因果，比背词重要。

**第 6 题**：参考：Many students download study apps. These apps promise quick progress, but they rarely work as they should.

点评：三次重复的 study apps，第一次后全部用 These apps / they 接管。也可升级用总结名词：Such apps promise quick progress, but this promise rarely holds true in daily use.（this promise 收纳前文，更见功力。）

**第 7 题**：I enjoy reading, writing, and running.

点评：三个动名词对齐。平行结构的检验法：把 and / or 前后的东西单独拎出来读，读起来"同类"就对了。

**第 8 题**：When we prepare for an exam, we should sleep well the night before.

点评：全文统一用 we。若想换成 you 也行，但要两句都换：When you prepare for an exam, you should sleep well the night before. 一段之内人称不换岗。

**第 9 题**：

- ① The bus is cheap but often slow.（或 The bus is cheap, but it is often slow.）
- ② He practiced listening daily, and as a result he improved quickly.（或 He practiced listening daily; as a result, he improved quickly.）

点评：but 直连两个分句中间逗号即可；as a result 是副词性短语，前面需要 and 或分号来"挂靠"，不能独立当连词连接两个完整句，这是高频语法暗坑。

**第 10 题**：The course is cheap. However, the quality is high.

点评：However 不能像 but 那样直连两个分句，必须拆成两句（或用分号：The course is cheap; however, the quality is high.），后面加逗号。这一改一换，正是"标点跟着词性走"。

**第 11 题**：参考改写：

> City bikes are becoming popular, mainly because they are cheap: a ride often costs less than a bottle of water. Safety, however, remains a concern, as some riders do not wear helmets. To make riding safer, cities are now building more bike lanes. This change, together with the low price, explains why more and more people are switching to bikes.

改造对照：① 因果显化：mainly because / as；② 指代：This change 收纳"修自行车道"；③ 连接词：however 转折、to make riding safer 目的衔接；④ 合并同类：cheap 与"一瓶水"两个短句并成一句。

评分自检点：是否至少 1 次指代 + 1 个连接词 + 1 次因果显化；是否没有新增原文没有的信息；改后是否 4~5 句、逻辑顺滑。

**第 12 题**：参考改写：

> I like English songs and movies, and I read English news every day. My English is improving, so next year I plan to take an exam. Clearly, I love English.

点评：songs / movies / news 本是天然并列，一个 and 就够；improving 与 plan 之间的因果用轻巧的 so 连接；结尾 In conclusion 换成 Clearly，去掉了总结腔，六个路牌减到一个。原文 6 句变 3 句，信息一个不少。这就是"路修直了再立牌"。
