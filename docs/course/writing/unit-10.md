> **预计学习时间**：4~6 小时，建议拆 3 天（第 1 天学论证四法，第 2 天学让步段与深度自查，第 3 天研究两篇整篇范文并做练习） | **前置**：[写作课 · 第 9 课](./unit-09.md)（骨架必须先过关） | **难度**：B1+（写作课）

## 本课目标

- [ ] 我能用举例、对比、因果、引用四种方法各写一个论证段
- [ ] 我会用 "Admittedly..., However..." 让步段，主动化解反方最强论点
- [ ] 我掌握链式追问法：每个论点后面追问"为什么？所以呢？"把论证逼到深处
- [ ] 我背下教育、科技、环境、健康四大话题各 5 条万能论据
- [ ] 我能逐段拆解一篇高分议论文，说出每一段用的论证方法

## 核心内容

上一课搭好了骨架，这一课长肌肉。**论点决定你"说什么"，论证决定你"能不能说服人"**。同一个论点，论证方法不同，得分能差一个档。

### 一、论证四法详解

#### 法一：举例论证（Example）

**方法说明**：用具体的人、事、数据支撑抽象论点。最直观、最好写，是主力论证法。例子贵具体不贵大，身边小事胜过空泛的"很多人"。

**句型库**：

- Take... for instance, ...（以……为例）
- A case in point is...（一个典型的例子是……）
- For example, ... / For instance, ...
- My own experience is a good illustration.（我自己的经历就是好例证）

**示范段**（论点：早起提升效率）：

> Mornings offer the mind its sharpest hours. **Take my roommate for instance**: after switching his study schedule from midnight to 7 a.m., his grades rose from average to top ten within one semester. **A case in point is** the routine of most writers, who famously guard their morning hours for serious work. Clearly, the early hours reward those who use them.

#### 法二：对比论证（Contrast）

**方法说明**：把两种做法、两个群体、前后两种状态放在一起对照，优劣自现。对比一出，说服力翻倍，因为没有参照物的论述是悬空的。

**句型库**：

- Unlike..., ...（与……不同）
- By contrast, ... / In contrast, ...（相比之下）
- While..., ...（虽然……但是，注意两句的对照关系）
- ...whereas...（……然而……，书面味更浓）

**示范段**（论点：纸质书优于电子书）：

> Paper books keep the reader's mind anchored. **Unlike** scrolling on a screen, which invites endless distraction, turning a physical page builds a quiet, single-minded rhythm. **By contrast**, e-reading often turns into tab-switching: one minute a chapter, the next a chat window. The same book, read on paper and on a phone, simply is not the same experience.

#### 法三：因果论证（Cause & Effect）

**方法说明**：说清"为什么会这样"或"这样会导致什么"，把论点和结果用逻辑链条焊死。因果是论证的发动机，一个段子里最好至少有一条因果链。

**句型库**：

- This is because...（这是因为……）
- As a result, ... / Consequently, ...（结果是……）
- This, in turn, leads to...（这反过来又导致……）
- The reason is simple: ...（原因很简单：……）

**示范段**（论点：通勤时间长伤害健康）：

> Long commutes quietly damage both body and mind. **This is because** sitting in traffic or packed trains adds hours of physical inactivity to an already sedentary day. **As a result**, regular exercise becomes nearly impossible, and stress built up on the road follows workers into the evening. Over time, this cycle leads to poor sleep, weight gain, and burnout.

#### 法四：引用论证（Authority）

**方法说明**：借助研究结论、专家观点、普遍共识来撑腰。注意分寸：考试中引用"某研究表明""人们普遍认为"这类**模糊但诚实**的表述完全够用，**切忌编造具体机构、具体百分比**。

**句型库**：

- According to..., ...（根据……）
- It is widely believed that...（人们普遍认为……）
- Studies have shown that...（研究表明……）
- As... once pointed out, "..."（正如……曾指出的）

**示范段**（论点：学第二语言越早越好）：

> Starting a second language early gives children an unfair advantage. **It is widely believed that** young learners absorb pronunciation far more naturally than adults. **Studies have shown that** children raised bilingual switch tasks faster and delay memory decline later in life. **As** educators **once pointed out**, the ear for language, once closed, rarely reopens.

**四法混搭提示**：高分段落常常一法为主、一法为辅。比如举例段里加一句 As a result，或因果段里加一个 Take...for instance。单独使用是及格线，混合使用是高分线。

### 二、让步段：Admittedly..., However...

只会进攻的议论文是险的，因为阅卷人心里住着一个反方。让步段的思路：**主动说出对方最有力的理由，再夺回来**。这比假装对方不存在高明得多，直接展示你的思辨能力。

**模板**：

```text
Admittedly, [反方最强的那条理由，说得诚实些].（让步：1~2 句）
However, [这个理由的局限 / 你的回应 / 它其实可以被解决].（反驳：1~2 句）
In fact, [把话题拉回你的立场，可再补一句论证].（回枪：1 句）
```

**示例一**（话题：大学生该不该强制上体育课，你的立场：该）：

> **Admittedly**, required PE classes occupy hours that could otherwise go to major courses, and some students genuinely dislike organized sports. **However**, the alternative is worse: without a fixed schedule, the students who need exercise most are exactly the ones who skip it. **In fact**, two hours of weekly exercise buys back far more study efficiency than it costs.

**示例二**（话题：该不该禁止校园手机，你的立场：禁）：

> **Admittedly**, phones serve real purposes on campus, from checking schedules to contacting family in emergencies. **However**, these needs can be met by smartwatches and school landlines, whereas the harm of constant scrolling cannot be outsourced. **In fact**, every school that banned phones reports the same thing: louder hallways and brighter eyes.

使用位置：让步段通常放在**最后一个主体段**，先把自己 2~3 个分论点打完，再"顺手"拆掉对方弹药库，然后干干净净进结论。

### 三、论证深度自查：链式追问法

大多数"论证不充分"的病灶相同：论点和结论之间缺了中间环节。解药是链式追问法：每个论点写完后，连问两个问题——

- **"为什么？"（Why?）**——逼你补上从论点到理由的缺环。
- **"所以呢？"（So what?）**——逼你补上从理由到意义的缺环。

示范。浅层版：

> Exercise improves study.（就这样，论证完了？）

追问手术：

```text
论点：Exercise improves study.
为什么？ → 因为运动促进大脑血液循环，提升注意力。（补因果）
所以呢？ → 所以学生每天运动 30 分钟，学习一小时的效果胜过枯坐两小时。（补意义）
再为什么？ → 这正是"劳逸结合"千年不废的原因。（可再升华）
```

手术后成段：

> Exercise improves study. **This is because** physical activity increases blood flow to the brain, which directly sharpens attention and memory. **As a result**, a student who exercises thirty minutes a day often achieves more in one focused hour than a tired classmate does in two. That, in simple terms, is why "all work and no play" has never been good advice.

每写完一个主体段，用"为什么？所以呢？"扫一遍。哪一环答不上来，哪一环就是你的漏洞。

### 四、四大高频话题万能论据库

考题千变，话题就那几个。每个话题背熟 5 条论据（英语+中文），考场上按需取用。

**教育 Education**：

1. Small classes allow teachers to notice every student's weakness.（小班教学让老师关注每个学生的弱项）
2. Interest is the best teacher; forced learning rarely lasts.（兴趣是最好的老师，强迫学习难持久）
3. Exams measure memory more than creativity.（考试更多考记忆力而非创造力）
4. Reading widely builds vocabulary faster than any word list.（广泛阅读比词表更快地积累词汇）
5. Failure in school teaches lessons no textbook contains.（在校失败教会课本里没有的东西）

**科技 Technology**：

1. Smartphones have turned waiting time into learning time.（智能手机把等待时间变成学习时间）
2. Face-to-face conversation builds trust that screens cannot.（面对面交流建立屏幕给不了的信任）
3. AI handles repetitive work, freeing humans for creative tasks.（AI 处理重复劳动，解放人类去做创造）
4. Online maps have made paper directions a lost art.（在线地图让看纸质地图成为失传技艺）
5. Technology is a good servant but a bad master.（科技是好的仆人，却是坏的主人）

**环境 Environment**：

1. Sorting garbage turns waste into resources.（垃圾分类变废为宝）
2. Bike-sharing cuts both traffic and carbon at once.（共享单车同时减少拥堵和碳排放）
3. Every reused shopping bag is one less bag in the ocean.（每重复使用一个购物袋，海里就少一个）
4. Green spaces cool cities better than air conditioners.（绿地比空调更会给城市降温）
5. Environmental damage is easy to start and hard to undo.（环境破坏易发难收）

**健康 Health**：

1. Seven hours of sleep beat seven cups of coffee.（七小时睡眠胜过七杯咖啡）
2. Regular exercise is the cheapest medicine known to science.（规律运动是科学已知最便宜的药）
3. Screen time before bed steals both sleep and mood.（睡前刷屏偷走睡眠也偷走情绪）
4. A slow home-cooked meal nourishes more than fast food.（一顿慢慢做的家常饭比快餐滋养得多）
5. Mental health deserves check-ups as regular as physical ones.（心理体检应像体格检查一样常规）

用法：论据是半成品，上场时套进四法句型里。比如教育论据 1 + 举例句型 = "Take small classes for instance: teachers get to notice every student's weakness."

### 五、议论文整篇范文 2 篇

#### 范文一（题目：Should college students take a gap year before starting work?）

> **【引言】** Every June, millions of fresh graduates rush from classrooms into offices without a pause. Yet a growing number of students now choose a gap year between graduation and employment. In my view, a well-planned gap year is a worthy investment rather than a delay.
>
> **【主体 1 · 举例论证】** First, a gap year turns book knowledge into living knowledge. Take a friend of mine for instance: after graduating in tourism management, he spent eight months traveling and working in three hostels, and returned with practical English, people skills, and a clear career direction. A case in point is that he was hired before most of his classmates precisely because of this experience, not despite it.
>
> **【主体 2 · 因果论证】** Second, rushing into a first job often locks students into the wrong track. This is because few graduates truly understand what an industry feels like from the inside until they work in it. As a result, many discover their mismatch only after two or three wasted years, when changing careers is far more costly. A gap year, spent on internships or trial projects, lowers the price of that discovery.
>
> **【让步段】** Admittedly, a gap year without a plan can decay into a year of sleeping in and gaming. However, the fault lies in the planning, not the pause itself. In fact, the same risk applies to unplanned first jobs, which merely look productive.
>
> **【结论】** In conclusion, a gap year, wisely planned, is not time lost but tuition paid to reality: it tests careers before committing, and tests oneself before competing. Education does not end at graduation; sometimes it only begins there.

**逐段点评**：

- 引言：两句话铺现象（rush from classrooms into offices 画面感强），第三句直接亮论点，干净。
- 主体 1：举例法教科书示范。例子有细节（八个月、三家旅馆），末句 not despite it 的转折是点睛之笔。
- 主体 2：因果法双链条。This is because... As a result... 层层推进，最后一句 lowers the price of that discovery 把抽象收益说具体。
- 让步段：承认真实风险（睡懒觉打游戏），但把矛头指向"没规划"而非"间隔年"本身，四两拨千斤。
- 结论：tuition paid to reality（交给现实的学费）是重申，末句"教育不终于毕业，有时始于毕业"完成升华。

#### 范文二（题目：Is it better to live in a big city or a small town?）

> **【引言】** Where to settle after graduation is a question every young person must answer. Some chase the lights of big cities; others prefer the calm of small towns. As far as I am concerned, for young people, big cities are the better first chapter, even if not the final one.
>
> **【主体 1 · 对比论证】** Big cities multiply opportunities, while small towns ration them. Unlike a small town, where one large factory may be the only employer, a big city offers dozens of industries within a single subway line. By contrast, young people in small towns often find their major simply unused. The same talent, planted in different soils, grows to different heights.
>
> **【主体 2 · 因果 + 引用论证】** Cities also accelerate growth. This is because cities gather the best colleagues, museums, lectures, and failures to learn from. It is widely believed that one learns fastest by watching the strongest at work, and the strongest almost all perform on the big-city stage. As a result, three years in a metropolis can teach what a decade elsewhere might not.
>
> **【让步段】** Admittedly, big-city life is expensive, crowded, and sometimes lonely. However, these pressures fall hardest on those who plan to stay forever; for those who come to learn and then choose freely, the city is a school, not a prison.
>
> **【结论】** In conclusion, big cities offer the young their widest choices, fastest lessons, and hardest tests, which is exactly what a first chapter of life should contain. One can always trade the stage for a garden later; it is far harder to do the reverse.

**逐段点评**：

- 引言：better first chapter, even if not the final one 立场精准，给后文留足腾挪空间。
- 主体 1：对比法主打。Unlike... By contrast... 两个句型把两个世界并置，末句"同样的种子，不同土壤，长成不同高度"是全段记忆点。
- 主体 2：因果与引用双法混搭，the strongest almost all perform on the big-city stage 呼应了 stage 意象。
- 让步段：妙在重新定义战场——压力伤害的是"想永居的人"，对"来学本事的人"是学费。反方论点被结构性化解。
- 结论：trade the stage for a garden（从舞台换到花园易，反过来难）呼应全文意象，升华不喊口号。

## 常见误区

| ❌ 错误示范 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 论点后直接跟：This is very important and everyone knows it. | 论点后接具体论证：举例/对比/因果/引用至少其一 | "这很重要，人人都知道"是零信息量填充。人人都知道的事不用你论证，论证的对象恰恰是"有人不信"的部分 |
| According to a Harvard study in 2019, 87.3% of people... | Studies have shown that many people... / It is widely believed that... | 考场无法查证，编造精确数据一旦被识破，全篇可信度归零。模糊但诚实的表述才是安全的高分做法 |
| 让步段写成倒戈：Admittedly, the opposite view is also reasonable, so both are fine. | Admittedly, 反方有理。However, 你的回应。立场从头到尾不变 | 让步是战术不是投降。让完不夺回来，等于自己拆自己论点，结论段将无家可归 |
| 例子只有 "For example, my friend works very hard." | For example, my friend Li, who worked night shifts for two years, was promoted to team leader. | 例子无细节 = 论证无证据。名字、时间、动作、结果，四个元素至少给两个，例子才立得起来 |
| 全文每个主体段都是 For example... 开头论证 | 四法轮换：举例、对比、因果、引用交替上 | 五段全是 for example，说明论证工具箱里只有一把锤子。方法多样本身就是语言能力加分项 |

## 实战练习

**A. 论证方法辨识（第 1~2 题）**

1. 指出下面段落用了四种方法中的哪几种：Unlike their parents, who saved every coin, today's young people spend on experiences. This is because experiences, unlike objects, grow sweeter with memory. As a result, a concert ticket often buys more lasting happiness than a new phone.
2. 从四大话题论据库里各挑 1 条，分别套进 Take... for instance / Unlike... / This is because... / It is widely believed that... 四个句型，写出 4 句完整论证句。

**B. 让步段写作（第 3~4 题）**

3. 话题"大学生应该自带电脑上课"（你的立场：应该），写一个让步段（Admittedly + However + In fact，3~5 句），反方理由自选（如：分心玩游戏）。
4. 话题"外卖是否毁了聚餐文化"（你的立场：毁了），写一个让步段。这次先写出反方最强论点，再动笔。

**C. 链式追问手术（第 5~6 题）**

5. 用"为什么？所以呢？"两连问，把这个浅层论点扩写成 4 句的完整论证段：Reading paper books helps sleep.
6. 同上，扩写：Team sports teach cooperation.

**D. 论据库调用（第 7~8 题）**

7. 题目：Should schools reduce homework? 从教育论据库里选 2 条，各套一个论证句型，组成一个主体段（主题句 + 2 个论证句 + 小结句）。
8. 题目：Is remote work good for companies? 从科技和健康论据库各取 1 条，混合成一个主体段。

**E. 整篇实战（第 9~10 题）**：用第 9 课骨架 + 本课四法，写完整议论文（40 分钟计时，250~300 词）。

9. 题目（原创仿写）：Some people say that watching movies is a waste of time, while others believe movies teach us about life. What is your opinion?（要求：至少用到四法中的三种 + 一个让步段）
10. 题目（原创仿写）："Should cities build more roads or improve public transport?"（要求：论证段里必须有一条完整的"为什么？所以呢？"链）

**F. 拆解训练（第 11~12 题）**

11. 把范文二的第一主体段抄写一遍，然后用不同颜色/标记标出：对比句型 ×2、比喻句 ×1。
12. 自选任一范文段落，把它"降级"：删掉所有论证句只留论点，感受论点裸奔的苍白（写一段 50 字左右的对比感悟即可）。

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看参考答案与点评</summary>

**第 1 题**：用了三种方法——对比论证（Unlike their parents...）、因果论证（This is because... / As a result...）。两法混搭，正是高分段的配方。
点评：识别他人的论证方法是自我提升的第一步。读范文时养成"贴标签"的习惯：这一句在干什么活？

**第 2 题**（参考句）：
- 举例：Take small classes for instance, teachers get to notice every student's weakness before it grows.
- 对比：Unlike paper directions, online maps never argue with you at a crossroads.
- 因果：Regular exercise is the cheapest medicine known to science, because it prevents what pills can only treat.
- 引用：It is widely believed that interest is the best teacher, and forced learning rarely lasts.
点评：论据库 + 句型 = 弹药 + 枪。背论据时顺手配上句型，考场调用零延迟。

**第 3 题**（参考段）：
> Admittedly, laptops in class invite games and videos, and some students scroll through entire lectures. However, the solution is classroom rules, not a blanket ban, just as schools manage phone use instead of banning pockets. In fact, note-taking software and online dictionaries make laptops closer to textbooks than to toys.
点评：让步诚实（确实有人玩），反驳有力（该管的是行为不是工具），回枪一句"电脑更像课本而不是玩具"收束。

**第 4 题**（参考段）：
> Admittedly, delivery apps save time for busy workers and let families skip the cooking after hard days. However, convenience is not culture: what dies with home cooking is not just a meal, but the table where family news was shared. In fact, the fastest way to empty a dinner table is to fill every stomach separately.
点评：反方最强论点（省时）先承认，再重新定义战场——省的是时间，死的是文化。末句"让每张胃各自填饱，是清空餐桌最快的方式"用具体画面说抽象道理。

**第 5 题**（参考段）：
> Reading paper books helps sleep. This is because a paper page demands no backlight and offers no pop-up, so the brain leaves the day's noise behind instead of chasing one more link. As a result, twenty minutes of reading often slides naturally into sleep, while twenty minutes of scrolling slides into another hour.
点评：一条"为什么"（无蓝光无弹窗）+ 一条"所以呢"（读完自然入睡），末尾 reading/scrolling 的对仗收拢全段，还顺手用了对比法。

**第 6 题**（参考段）：
> Team sports teach cooperation. This is because no single player can win alone, so passing, trusting, and covering for teammates become daily practice rather than classroom theory. As a result, former team athletes often become the colleagues who listen, share credit, and step up when a project stalls.
点评：链条从"球场规则"走到"职场品质"，So what 的答案落到了真实人生上，这就是论证的落点。

**第 7 题**（参考段）：
> Schools should reduce homework, because quantity has quietly replaced quality. Take exam preparation for instance: piling up worksheets mostly trains tiredness, not thinking, since interest is the best teacher and forced learning rarely lasts. Small, well-designed assignments would let teachers notice every student's weakness instead of grading mountains at midnight. Less marking, in short, could mean more teaching.
点评：教育论据 2（兴趣是最好的老师）和论据 1（小班关注弱项）都化了妆进场，加上举例句型和一个因果链，五句成段。

**第 8 题**（参考段）：
> Remote work, handled well, serves both employees and companies. This is because long commutes quietly damage health: sitting in traffic adds hours of inactivity, and stress on the road follows workers into the evening. By contrast, remote workers often trade commute time for exercise or focused work, which is why many companies report stable output with happier teams. Technology, in short, is a good servant when the office learns to let go.
点评：健康论据（通勤伤害）+ 科技论据（科技是好仆人）双库调用，By contrast 补上对比法，一段三法。

**第 9 题**（参考范文，约 280 词）：
> Some people see movie-watching as hours burned for nothing. In my view, however, good movies are among the cheapest teachers life offers.
>
> First, movies compress other lives into two hours. This is because a film hands us choices and failures we have not yet met, so we rehearse decisions safely in the dark. Take "The Pursuit of Happyness" for instance: generations of students have learned more about persistence from its two hours than from a shelf of self-help books.
>
> Second, movies build empathy that news reports cannot. Unlike statistics, which numb us, a well-told story puts us inside a stranger's shoes. As a result, after watching a film about refugees or miners, audiences donate, discuss, and remember in ways no chart achieves.
>
> Admittedly, plenty of movies are indeed time-killers, and binge-watching crowds out both sleep and study. However, the same is true of books, and nobody calls reading a waste. In fact, the problem is choice, not the medium.
>
> In conclusion, movies are not an escape from life but a rehearsal for it, teaching through stories what life teaches through scars. Choose well, and the cinema is a classroom with better lighting.
点评：主体 1 因果+举例，主体 2 对比+因果，让步段用"书也一样"以子之矛攻子之盾，结论 rehearsal for it / a classroom with better lighting 双重升华。全文四法用满，链条完整。

**第 10 题**（参考范文，约 270 词）：
> Congestion chokes nearly every growing city, and the classic answer has always been more roads. I believe, however, that improving public transport is the wiser road.
>
> More roads, counterintuitively, breed more traffic. This is because every new lane temporarily eases jams, which invites more people to buy cars and drive, until the lane fills again. As a result, cities that widened highways for decades, like many in America, still rank among the most congested on earth.
>
> Public transport, by contrast, moves people by the thousand, not by the carload. Unlike a road, which serves only those who can afford to drive, a metro line serves students, workers, and elders alike. It is widely believed that one full bus removes about forty cars from the road, which is land no highway can match.
>
> Admittedly, new metro lines cost far more than a road and take years to build. However, roads also cost more than their budgets, once noise, pollution, and demolished neighborhoods are counted. In fact, the question is not which is cheaper today, but which city our grandchildren can breathe in.
>
> In conclusion, more roads feed the disease, while better transit treats it: the choice is between wider jams and lighter skies. Cities that understand this will own the future's cleanest streets.
点评：主体 1 是标准的"为什么？所以呢？"链（新路→吸引更多车→还是堵），主体 2 对比+引用，让步段把成本之争升维到"孙辈能不能呼吸"，结论 wider jams or lighter skies 的对仗收束。

**第 11 题**：自查——两个对比句型（Unlike a small town... / By contrast, young people...）应标出；比喻句是末句 "The same talent, planted in different soils, grows to different heights."
点评：抄写是笨功夫也是最灵的功夫，抄一遍比读三遍记得牢。

**第 12 题**：开放题。常见感悟方向：论点单独站着像口号/裸奔；删掉论证后段落从"说服"退化成"宣布"；正是那些例子、对比和因果链让论点"长出了手脚"。
点评：见过贫瘠，才懂得丰饶。这个练习会让你从此舍不得让论点裸奔。
