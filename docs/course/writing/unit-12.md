## 本课目标

- [ ] 我能不看讲义完成 8 篇三种文体的全流程写作（审题 → 提纲 → 成文 → 自检）
- [ ] 我能用 15 项修改清单给自己或别人的作文逐条打钩体检
- [ ] 我能按"3-5-17-5"节奏在 30 分钟内完成一篇考试作文
- [ ] 我修完了 8 项毕业标准，知道自己的写作强项和短板
- [ ] 我形成了自己的错题本：每次写作的 top 3 错误类型

## 核心内容

### 一、考试时间分配：3-5-17-5

30 分钟的考场作文（四六级），每一分钟都有岗位：

| 阶段 | 时长 | 干什么 | 心法 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 审题 | 3 分钟 | 圈关键词，定文体（书信/议论文/图表），定立场 | 看错题 = 全盘皆输，这 3 分钟是保险费 |
| 提纲 | 5 分钟 | 论点句 + 2~3 个分论点 + 每段一个备用论据 | 写出关键词即可，不写整句 |
| 写作 | 17 分钟 | 按提纲填肉，不回头改 | 卡住的词绕过去，写作期禁止自我怀疑 |
| 检查 | 5 分钟 | 用 15 项清单扫一遍，只改硬伤 | 时态、单复数、拼写是三大金矿 |

练熟后 17 分钟够写 160 词。省时间的从来不是写得快，而是**不返工**。

### 二、8 篇全流程实战

每篇给出四样东西：题目、审题提示、提纲模板。范文和点评在练习答案区，写完再看。

#### 第 1 篇 · 建议信（应用文）

**题目**：Suppose you are Li Ming, a student at Sunshine University. Write a letter to the library director to suggest two or three improvements to the library. You should write about 120 words.

**审题提示**：收信人是谁？——图书馆长（正式，Dear Sir or Madam 或 Dear Director）；核心任务是什么？——提建议（不是投诉！语气要建设性）；几条建议？——题目说 two or three，别超载。

**提纲模板**：

```text
第一段：身份 + 来意（作为常客，想提几条改进建议）
第二段：建议 1 + 理由 / 建议 2 + 理由 /（建议 3 + 理由）
第三段：希望建议被考虑 + 感谢
```

#### 第 2 篇 · 感谢信（应用文）

**题目**：Suppose you are Li Ming. You have just returned from a two-week exchange program in London, where you stayed with the Smith family. Write a letter to Mrs. Smith to express your gratitude. You should write about 120 words.

**审题提示**：感恩点必须具体——想想寄宿家庭会为你做什么：接机、做饭、练口语、带你逛城市；细节是感谢信的命（见第 8 课）；落款用 Yours sincerely（认识了，正式关系）。

**提纲模板**：

```text
第一段：报平安 + 点明感谢
第二段：细节回忆（挑 2~3 件具体的事）+ 这些事带给你的改变
第三段：再次感谢 + 邀请来中国
```

#### 第 3 篇 · 观点对比类议论文

**题目**：Some people prefer to travel alone, while others prefer to travel with companions. Which do you prefer? Give reasons for your answer. You should write about 180 words.

**审题提示**：必须站队（Which do you prefer），骑墙 = 跑题；"prefer" 是主观选择题，理由要能自圆其说；记得让步段安置对方最大优点。

**提纲模板**：

```text
论点：我更偏好 X
  引言钩子：现象/问题式开头
  主体 1：理由一（+ 论证方法）
  主体 2：理由二（+ 换一种论证方法）
  让步段：Admittedly 对方优点..., However...
  结论：重申 + 升华
```

#### 第 4 篇 · 现象评论类议论文

**题目**：Nowadays, more and more young people want to become internet influencers instead of taking traditional jobs. What do you think of this phenomenon? You should write about 180 words.

**审题提示**：评论现象 ≠ 一味批判也不必一味赞美，但必须有明确态度；用"为什么会这样"（因果）和"意味着什么"（影响）双轴分析；论点可以是"可以理解但需要清醒"这类有层次的立场。

**提纲模板**：

```text
论点：对现象的明确态度（有层次，不骑墙）
  引言：现象描述（数据/画面感）
  主体 1：现象成因（为什么年轻人向往）
  主体 2：清醒剂（这行的真相 / 风险）
  让步/结论：辩证收束 + 升华
```

#### 第 5 篇 · 问题解决类议论文

**题目**：Many students find it hard to concentrate on their studies because of smartphone addiction. What are the possible causes and what solutions can you suggest? You should write about 180 words.

**审题提示**：两问都要答——原因 + 解决方案，漏一问扣一半结构分；通常"一段原因 + 一段方案（2~3 条）"；方案要具体可执行，"少玩手机"不算方案。

**提纲模板**：

```text
引言：现象（图书馆里人人刷手机的画面）+ 点题
主体 1：原因 2 个（app 设计成瘾 / 缺乏自控环境）
主体 2：方案 2~3 条（物理隔离 / 替代习惯 / 结伴监督），每条一句方案 + 一句原理
结论：总结 + 呼吁
```

#### 第 6 篇 · 柱状图作文

**题目**：The bar chart below shows the main means of commuting in Green City in 2015 and 2025: private car (55%→38%), bus (30%→26%), bicycle (15%→36%). Write a report describing the information. You should write about 150 words.

**审题提示**：先答三问——比什么（三种通勤方式份额）？谁变化最大（自行车 +21 个百分点，私家车 -17 个百分点）？怎么变（绿色出行崛起）？反超拐点：自行车和公交 2025 年接近，私家车仍第一？不，2025 年私家车 38% 仍是第一，自行车 36% 逼近反超——这就是亮点句。

**提纲模板**：

```text
引言：改写题目（compares the changes in...）
主体：按变化幅度排序写三项，每项带数据，点出"自行车逼近反超"
结论：Overall 总结趋势（绿色通勤上升），零观点
```

#### 第 7 篇 · 线图作文

**题目**：The line graph below shows coffee consumption and tea consumption per person per year in one country from 2010 to 2020: coffee (2 kg→6 kg, steady rise), tea (8 kg→5 kg, steady fall). Write a report describing the information. You should write about 150 words.

**审题提示**：双线反向，交叉点在哪？（大约 2017 年前后，咖啡反超茶）——交叉点是全文最高分句的位置；两条线各配一组分档动词（咖啡 climbed steadily / 茶 declined gradually）；结尾 a reversal of taste 之类的概括。

**提纲模板**：

```text
引言：改写题目（illustrates changes in...）
主体 1：2010 起点 + 各自动态（一升一降）
主体 2：交叉拐点 + 2020 终点数据
结论：Overall，偏好逆转，零观点
```

#### 第 8 篇 · 自由话题

**题目**：Write a short essay (about 150 words) about a small habit that has changed your life in some way. You may consider: what the habit is, how you started it, and what difference it has made. Give your essay a title.

**审题提示**：唯一没有模板的一篇，但骨架仍在——讲清"习惯 → 起点 → 改变"的时间线就是结构；选具体的习惯（每天走 6000 步、睡前记三行日记、给家人每周打个电话），别选"坚持学习"这种空泛的；题目要求自拟标题，别忘了。

**提纲模板**：

```text
标题：习惯本身或它的意义
第一段：习惯是什么 + 画面化开场
第二段：怎么开始的（一个小契机）
第三段：带来的改变（1~2 个具体变化）+ 收束
```

### 三、修改清单（15 项逐条勾选）

写完必查。打印出来贴在桌上，每次写作后逐条打钩。

**内容 5 查**：

| # | 检查项 | 自查问题 | ✓ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 切题 | 我写的是题目问的那个问题吗？文体对吗（信/议/图）？ | ☐ |
| 2 | 论点明确 | 读者能在 30 秒内找到我的立场句吗？ | ☐ |
| 3 | 论证充分 | 每个分论点后面有例子/对比/因果/引用至少一样吗？ | ☐ |
| 4 | 字数达标 | 够到要求字数的下限了吗（120/150/180）？ | ☐ |
| 5 | 无跑题 | 有没有哪一段写着写着离开了题目？删掉心疼也要删 | ☐ |

**结构 4 查**：

| # | 检查项 | 自查问题 | ✓ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | 三段清晰 | 引言/主体/结论功能齐全？主体段有 2~3 段吗？ | ☐ |
| 7 | 主题句 | 每个主体段第一句就是该段分论点吗？ | ☐ |
| 8 | 衔接词 | First/Moreover/However/Therefore 用起来了吗？段落之间有桥吗？ | ☐ |
| 9 | 结论呼应 | 结论重申了立场并呼应引言吗？有没有塞进新观点？ | ☐ |

**语言 6 查**：

| # | 检查项 | 自查问题 | ✓ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | 时态一致 | 叙事一个时态到底了吗？该用过去的地方没写现在吧？ | ☐ |
| 11 | 主谓一致 | 每个动词和主语的数对上了吗（he does / they do）？ | ☐ |
| 12 | 拼写 | 通读一遍只看拼写：its/it's、their/there、receive、believe | ☐ |
| 13 | 搭配 | 动词名词配对地道吗（take measures 不是 make measures）？ | ☐ |
| 14 | 中式表达 | 有没有"直译汉语"的句子（increase big / open light）？ | ☐ |
| 15 | 标点 | 英文标点全用半角了吗？句号后有空格吗？没连写逗号长句吗？ | ☐ |

使用要领：**前三次全查，之后只查你的常错项**。错题本里出现三次的错误类型，永久进入你的"重点监控行"。

### 四、毕业标准清单（8 项）

- [ ] 1. 8 篇实战全部完成，每篇都按"审题 → 提纲 → 写作 → 自检"四步走完
- [ ] 2. 8 篇中至少 6 篇，15 项清单自检硬伤不超过 2 处
- [ ] 3. 建议信、感谢信能不看模板在 25 分钟内写合格
- [ ] 4. 议论文能稳定写出"引言三式任选其一 + 两个主体段 + 一个让步段 + 结论"的完整骨架
- [ ] 5. 图表作文动词副词档位零矛盾，每个趋势句都带数据
- [ ] 6. 30 分钟计时作文至少练过 3 次，每次都能写完（写完比写好重要）
- [ ] 7. 错题本积累了至少 10 条个人高频错误，并能说出每条的纠正办法
- [ ] 8. 第 8 篇自由话题被你修改过至少两稿，且第二稿比第一稿你自己能说出好在哪

8 项全勾 = 写作课毕业。带着你的错题本出发，下一站见。

## 常见误区

| ❌ 错误示范 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 篇一天写完，写完不对答案 | 每天 1~2 篇，写完立刻对范文、过清单、记错题 | 写作能力长在"改"的过程里，不在"写"的数量里。只写不改 = 同一错误重复八遍 |
| 检查 5 分钟只通读一遍找手感 | 按清单 10~15 项逐条扫描，专项专查 | 通读找不出错，因为你自己写的句子在你眼里永远是对的。清单是让"另一个你"来审稿 |
| 对范文时只看句型和生词 | 逐段问三个问题：它的论点怎么摆的？论证用了哪招？结论怎么收的？ | 范文的价值 70% 在结构，30% 在语言。只抄好词好句是捡了芝麻 |
| 考场先写提纲又嫌浪费 5 分钟，直接开写 | 提纲 5 分钟是投资不是消费：没有提纲，17 分钟写作期会变成 17 分钟纠结期 | 数据说话：有提纲的考生平均字数更足、结构分更高、还能省出检查时间 |
| 自由话题写成流水账日记 | 依然立骨架：一个习惯 = 一条清晰的"是什么→怎么来→改变了什么"叙事线 | "自由"指内容自由，不是结构自由。没骨架的自由发挥就是跑题的近义词 |

## 实战练习

**A. 全流程 8 篇（第 1~8 题）**：全部按"审题 3 分 → 提纲 5 分 → 写作 17 分 → 检查 5 分"的节奏执行。书信类限 25 分钟，议论文与图表类限 30 分钟。

1. （建议信）完成第 1 篇：给图书馆长的改进建议信（约 120 词）。
2. （感谢信）完成第 2 篇：给寄宿家庭 Mrs. Smith 的感谢信（约 120 词）。
3. （观点对比）完成第 3 篇：独自旅行还是结伴旅行（约 180 词，含一个让步段）。
4. （现象评论）完成第 4 篇：年轻人想当网红现象之我见（约 180 词）。
5. （问题解决）完成第 5 篇：手机依赖的原因与对策（约 180 词，原因和方案都要写）。
6. （柱状图）完成第 6 篇：Green City 通勤方式变化报告（约 150 词，含反超点）。
7. （线图）完成第 7 篇：咖啡与茶消费量变化报告（约 150 词，含交叉点）。
8. （自由话题）完成第 8 篇：改变了我的一个小习惯（约 150 词，自拟标题，至少两稿）。

**B. 清单实操（第 9~10 题）**

9. 用 15 项修改清单逐条给你自己的第 3 篇（或第 5 篇）打钩，统计硬伤数量并列出 top 3 错误类型，写进错题本。
10. 交换互评：找一位同学（或对照本课范文）互相用清单体检，给对方的作文写出两条具体的表扬 + 一条最优先要改的建议。

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看第 1 篇参考范文 + 点评（建议信）</summary>

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> I am writing as a frequent visitor to our library to suggest a few improvements that might benefit all students.
>
> First, I suggest that the reading rooms stay open until midnight during exam weeks, because seats are always full after 9 p.m. at those times. Second, it would be beneficial if a small cafe corner were opened on the ground floor, so that students could refresh themselves without leaving the building. Finally, you might also consider setting up an online system for reserving seats, which would put an end to the early-morning queue.
>
> I hope you will find these suggestions helpful. Thank you for your time and consideration.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> Li Ming

点评：三条建议一条一个句型（I suggest that / It would be beneficial if / you might also consider），正好把第 8 课的建议信句型库全部落地；每条建议都跟一个 because/so that 理由句；落款 Yours faithfully 配 Dear Sir or Madam，正式度同档。写齐这些，这篇就是满配模板分。

</details>

<details><summary>点击查看第 2 篇参考范文 + 点评（感谢信）</summary>

> Dear Mrs. Smith,
>
> I arrived home safely last week, and I am writing to express my heartfelt gratitude for everything you did during my stay in London.
>
> I still remember my first evening, when you cooked a warm welcome dinner and patiently repeated every word until I could follow the conversation. Thanks to your daily corrections, my English improved more in two weeks than in two semesters. What touched me most was the day you took me to the countryside market and taught me the names of every fruit and flower.
>
> You made a stranger feel at home, and I will never forget that. If you ever visit China, my family and I would be more than delighted to host you.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming

点评：三个细节（欢迎晚餐、日常纠音、乡间集市）层层递进，比 "you were very kind to me" 有力一百倍；"more in two weeks than in two semesters" 用对比量化感激；结尾发出邀请是感谢信的黄金收尾。报平安开头的细节让信有了体温。

</details>

<details><summary>点击查看第 3 篇参考范文 + 点评（观点对比：独自 vs 结伴旅行）</summary>

> Travel has never been easier, yet one question remains personal: alone or with companions? As far as I am concerned, traveling alone offers what no group trip can.
>
> First, solo travel forces real independence. When every train, meal, and decision is yours alone, problems become puzzles instead of quarrels. Take my first solo trip for instance: a missed bus left me stranded in a small town, and finding my own way back taught me more about the country than any guidebook did.
>
> Second, traveling alone connects you with strangers. This is because a lone traveler looks approachable, while a closed group rarely welcomes newcomers. As a result, I shared tables with locals on that trip, something that never happened when I traveled with friends.
>
> Admittedly, companions share costs and double the joy of beautiful views. However, shared joy can be enjoyed after the trip through photos and stories, while the growth of handling a journey alone cannot be outsourced at all.
>
> In conclusion, a solo journey is a short course in independence, and its lessons travel home with you.

点评：引言一句设问直接亮立场；主体 1 举例（missed bus 画面具体），主体 2 因果（落单者更易被搭话——道理真实）；让步段承认对方最大卖点（分摊费用、快乐加倍）再用"照片可分享、成长不可代劳"夺回；结论 "a short course in independence" 呼应升华。四法用了三法，链条完整。

</details>

<details><summary>点击查看第 4 篇参考范文 + 点评（现象评论：想当网红）</summary>

> Open any short-video app and you will meet a teenager announcing, "My dream is to become an influencer." Surveys show that for many students, this dream now outranks doctor or engineer. In my view, this trend is understandable but needs a cold shower of realism.
>
> The attraction has real causes. This is because influencer life looks free, colorful, and richly paid, especially compared with the grey routine of traditional jobs. For a generation raised on screens, the stage of an app feels closer than any office.
>
> However, the visible top of the pyramid hides its enormous base. It is widely believed that only a tiny fraction of creators earn a living wage, while the majority burn out chasing algorithms. A career built on followers is also a career built on sand: one scandal or one platform change can erase years of work overnight.
>
> Admittedly, the dream itself is not a sin, and creating content does build real skills. However, young people should treat it as a side dish rather than the whole meal, keeping their studies as the main course.
>
> In conclusion, wanting to be seen is human, but building a life on likes alone is a risky bet. The steadiest fame, in the end, is skill.

点评：引言用画面（少年宣布梦想）代替空话；主体 1 站在年轻人一边解释成因，先懂再评；主体 2 用"金字塔藏底座""沙上筑楼"两个比喻把风险讲透；让步段态度辩证不骑墙（side dish / main course 的比喻是全文记忆点）；结论 "The steadiest fame is skill" 一句升华。现象评论题的教科书打法：成因 + 风险 + 辩证立场。

</details>

<details><summary>点击查看第 5 篇参考范文 + 点评（问题解决：手机依赖）</summary>

> Look around any library and you will see heads bent not over books, but over glowing screens. Smartphone addiction has quietly become the number one enemy of study, and it deserves both an honest diagnosis and a practical cure.
>
> The causes are no accident. First, apps are engineered to trap attention: endless feeds, autoplay, and notifications are designed by teams whose job is to keep thumbs moving. Second, willpower fails because phones sit within reach; a bright screen one meter away beats a good intention every time.
>
> The good news is that solutions exist, and the best ones change the environment rather than the person. Leaving the phone in the dorm while studying, for example, removes the battle itself, for one cannot yield to a temptation that is not there. You might also consider replacing the habit instead of merely forbidding it: a paper notebook or a short walk fills the idle seconds that usually trigger scrolling. Finally, studying with a partner adds gentle supervision, since broken promises are harder to make face to face.
>
> In short, phones win by design, so we must win by design too. Move the phone, fill the gap, bring a friend, and concentration will slowly come home.

点评：主体 1 两层原因（产品设计 + 环境便利），第二层为后文"改环境式方案"埋下伏笔——前后勾连是结构分亮点；主体 3 三条方案每条带一句原理（物理隔离 / 替代习惯 / 同伴监督），全部具体可执行；结论四个短句排比收束，"concentration will slowly come home" 收得温暖有力。问题解决类"一问不少、方案落地"的满分示范。

</details>

<details><summary>点击查看第 6 篇参考范文 + 点评（柱状图：通勤方式）</summary>

> The bar chart compares the shares of three main means of commuting in Green City in 2015 and 2025.
>
> In 2015, the private car clearly dominated, used by 55% of commuters, while buses carried 30% and bicycles only 15%. Over the following decade, the picture changed sharply. Car commuting declined steadily to 38%, and the bus share slipped slightly to 26%. Cycling, by contrast, witnessed a remarkable surge, more than doubling its share to 36%. As a result, bicycles came from far behind to nearly overtake cars, trailing by only two percentage points in 2025.
>
> Overall, the chart reveals a clear shift toward green commuting: the car lost its comfortable lead within a single decade, and the bicycle rose from the smallest choice to a strong second.

点评：主体段按"变化幅度"排序（车 -17、自行车 +21 是主角，公交 -4 一句带过），重点数据全部带数字；"came from far behind to nearly overtake" 把"差 2 个百分点未反超"这个最微妙的信息点写成了全文亮点；结论 green commuting 只概括趋势、不提建议，零观点红线守住了。

</details>

<details><summary>点击查看第 7 篇参考范文 + 点评（线图：咖啡与茶）</summary>

> The line graph illustrates changes in annual coffee and tea consumption per person in one country between 2010 and 2020.
>
> At the start of the period, tea was the unquestioned national drink, with average consumption standing at 8 kg per person, four times the figure for coffee, at only 2 kg. The two lines then moved steadily in opposite directions: coffee climbed persistently throughout the decade, while tea declined gradually year by year. The gap narrowed from 6 kg in 2010 to almost nothing by 2016, and around 2017 coffee overtook tea for the first time. From then on, the gap grew again in reverse, and by 2020 coffee consumption had tripled to 6 kg, while tea had fallen to 5 kg.
>
> Overall, the decade saw a complete reversal of drinking habits, with coffee replacing tea as the country's preferred drink.

点评：两个满分动作——①交叉拐点单独立句（around 2017 coffee overtook tea for the first time），这是线图作文最值钱的一句；②"gap narrowed... gap grew again in reverse" 把两线关系写成一段追逐叙事。起点对比 four times the figure for coffee 顺手用了倍数表达。结尾 a complete reversal 概括全篇，干净收工。

</details>

<details><summary>点击查看第 8 篇参考范文 + 点评（自由话题：小习惯）</summary>

**Three Lines Before Sleep**

> Every night, before turning off the lamp, I write exactly three lines in a small notebook: one thing I did, one thing I learned, and one thing I am grateful for. The habit is so small that it takes less than two minutes, yet it has quietly rearranged my days.
>
> It began in my darkest semester, when failed exams and sleepless nights followed each other in a loop. A teacher suggested ending each day with "a few honest words to yourself." I started with three lines, mostly complaints at first.
>
> Slowly, the complaints ran out, and the grateful line grew longer. Falling asleep after writing felt different: the day had been received, recorded, and closed, like a book returned to its shelf. My sleep improved, and so did my mornings. That is the quiet power of a small habit: it does not change your life in a day, but it changes every day of your life.

点评：标题"Three Lines Before Sleep"具体到可直接想象；第一段末尾 "rearranged my days" 悬念钩子；第二段交代起点（最黑暗的学期），让习惯有了来历；第三段 "like a book returned to its shelf" 的比喻和结尾对仗（not in a day / every day）是语言分亮点。自由话题的取胜之道：小事写细，细节见真。

</details>

<details><summary>点击查看第 9~10 题参考做法 + 点评（清单实操）</summary>

**第 9 题**：参考做法——用清单逐项打钩后，把每处硬伤归类（如"语言-11 主谓一致 ×2、语言-13 搭配 ×1、结构-8 衔接 ×1"），统计出 top 3：主谓一致、动词搭配、段间衔接。下一步：下次写作检查期专扫这三行，错题本记一句"写完先找每个动词的主语"。
点评：清单的意义不在打钩，在于让错误从"感觉写得还行"变成"可统计、可追踪、可消灭的具体对象"。

**第 10 题**：参考做法——互评时先说结构（论点找得到吗？论证哪招？），再说语言（哪一句最出彩？哪一句最别扭？）。具体的表扬如"你的让步段反驳有力"，而不是笼统的"写得不错"；优先建议一次只提一条，贪多则失。
点评：会评别人，才会改自己。互评是把自己的眼睛练成阅卷人的眼睛，这是修改清单之外最便宜的提分手段。

</details>
