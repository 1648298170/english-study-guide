> **预计学习时间**：4~6 小时，建议拆 3 天（第 1 天学格式与邀请信、感谢信，第 2 天学建议信、投诉信，第 3 天做真题实战） | **前置**：[写作课 · 第 7 课](./unit-07.md) | **难度**：B1+（写作课）

## 本课目标

- [ ] 我能按"称呼 + 三段正文 + 落款"的标准格式写一封完整的英文书信
- [ ] 我知道四种关系（陌生/认识/熟悉/亲密）分别该用哪个称呼和落款
- [ ] 我能套用邀请信、感谢信、建议信、投诉信四大模板各写出一封信
- [ ] 我能区分邮件和正式书信的语域差异，不再对导师写 Hi there
- [ ] 我能在 30 分钟内完成一道四六级风格的书信题

## 核心内容

### 一、格式规范：三件套

英文书信的骨架只有三件套：**称呼、正文三段、落款**。四六级和考研的写信题，这个格式一分都不能丢。

```text
Dear Mr. Smith,                    ← 称呼（逗号，不是冒号；冒号是超正式美式用法，考试用逗号）
                                   ← 空一行
    第一段：我是谁 + 我为什么写（1~2 句）
    第二段：具体内容（细节 3~5 句）
    第三段：期待回复 / 表达祝愿（1~2 句）
                                   ← 空一行
Yours sincerely,                   ← 落款（每字母下移，缩进式或左对齐皆可，全文统一）
Li Ming                            ← 署名（考题让你写谁就写谁，别写真名）
```

**落款按关系选词表**（考试保命表）：

| 关系 | 称呼 | 落款 | 场景举例 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 不认识的人/机构 | Dear Sir or Madam, | Yours faithfully, | 投诉给客服、申请给委员会 |
| 认识但正式（老师、上级） | Dear Mr. Smith, / Dear Professor Lee, | Yours sincerely, | 给老师写信、联系导师 |
| 认识且半正式（同事、客户） | Dear Sarah, | Kind regards, / Best regards, | 职场邮件、合作往来 |
| 熟人朋友 | Dear Tom, / Hi Tom, | Best wishes, / Yours, | 朋友邀请、私人感谢 |

记忆窍门：**S Faithfully 配 Stranger，S Sincerely 配 Sir 有名有姓**。考试 90% 的情况用 Yours sincerely 就对了。

### 二、四大场景模板

#### 场景一：邀请信（Letter of Invitation）

**结构模板**：

```text
第一段：自我介绍 + 点明邀请（我要办什么活动，邀你来）
第二段：活动细节：时间、地点、内容、为什么邀请你
第三段：请求答复 + 期待
```

**句型库**（直接套）：

- I am writing to invite you to... （我写信是想邀请你……）
- It would be our great honor if you could... （如果您能……将是我们极大的荣幸）
- The event is scheduled for [时间] at [地点]. （活动定于……）
- Please let me know by [日期] whether you will be able to come. （请在……前告知能否出席）
- I am looking forward to your reply. （期待回复）

**完整范文**（邀请外教参加班级晚会）：

> Dear Professor Smith,
>
> I am writing on behalf of Class 3 to invite you to our English Evening, which will be held in the school hall at 7 p.m. on Friday, June 20th.
>
> The evening will feature English songs, short plays, and a poetry reading, all prepared by the students. As our speaking coach this term, you have helped us improve more than any textbook could. It would be our great honor if you could join us and give a few closing remarks.
>
> Please let me know by next Monday whether you will be able to come. We are all looking forward to seeing you there.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming

#### 场景二：感谢信（Letter of Thanks）

**结构模板**：

```text
第一段：点明感谢什么（对方为你做过的一件事）
第二段：具体细节：当时发生了什么 + 对方的帮助带来什么改变
第三段：再次感谢 + 愿意回报
```

**句型库**：

- I would like to express my sincere gratitude for... （我想对……表达真诚的感谢）
- It was incredibly kind of you to... （您真是太好了，……）
- Thanks to your help, I was able to... （多亏您的帮助，我才能……）
- If there is ever anything I can do for you, please do not hesitate to let me know. （若有机会回报，请一定告诉我）

**完整范文**（感谢房东在你生病时的照顾）：

> Dear Mrs. Brown,
>
> I would like to express my sincere gratitude for the care you gave me during my illness last week.
>
> When I came down with a bad fever, I was alone and did not know what to do. It was incredibly kind of you to bring me hot soup, buy my medicine, and even call the doctor for me. Thanks to your help, I recovered quickly and did not miss my exams. What moved me most was that you checked on me every evening after work.
>
> I feel truly lucky to have such a warm-hearted landlady. If there is ever anything I can do for you, please do not hesitate to let me know.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming

#### 场景三：建议信（Letter of Suggestions / Advice）

**结构模板**：

```text
第一段：说明写信缘由（针对什么问题/收到什么请求提建议）
第二段：建议 2~3 条，每条"建议句 + 理由句"
第三段：希望建议有用 + 欢迎讨论
```

**句型库**：

- I am writing to offer a few suggestions regarding... （我想就……提出几条建议）
- I suggest that... （我建议……，注意 suggest that + 动词原形）
- It would be beneficial if... （如果……会很有益）
- You might also consider... （你也可以考虑……）
- I hope you will find these suggestions helpful. （希望这些建议对你有帮助）

**完整范文**（同学要来你的城市旅游，给出行建议）：

> Dear Jack,
>
> I am so glad to hear that you are coming to Chengdu next month. As a local, I would like to offer a few suggestions to make your trip better.
>
> First, I suggest that you take the metro instead of taxis, as the traffic downtown is often heavy. Second, it would be beneficial if you booked your hotel near Chunxi Road, because most attractions are within walking distance from there. Finally, you might also consider visiting the panda base early in the morning, when the pandas are most active.
>
> I hope you will find these suggestions helpful. If you have any questions, just write to me anytime.
>
> Best wishes,
> Li Ming

#### 场景四：投诉信（Letter of Complaint）

**结构模板**：

```text
第一段：说明投诉对象 + 问题是什么（克制、就事论事）
第二段：问题细节：何时发生 + 造成什么不便 + 你期望的解决方案
第三段：表达期待解决 + 设定回复期限
```

**句型库**：

- I am writing to complain about... （我写信是为了投诉……）
- I was disappointed to find that... （我失望地发现……）
- This caused me considerable inconvenience, as... （这给我造成很大不便，因为……）
- I would appreciate it if you could... （如果您能……我将不胜感激）
- I look forward to your prompt reply. （期待您的尽快答复）

**完整范文**（投诉网购手机质量问题）：

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> I am writing to complain about a mobile phone I purchased from your online store on May 3rd (Order No. 20260503).
>
> When the phone arrived, I was disappointed to find that the screen had a small crack and that the battery drained within three hours. This caused me considerable inconvenience, as I rely on my phone for work. According to your return policy, I am entitled to a full refund or a replacement.
>
> I would appreciate it if you could deal with this matter within one week. I look forward to your prompt reply.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> Li Ming

### 三、邮件 vs 书信：正式度差异

邮件和纸质书信骨架相同，但**语域（register）按对象切换**。判断标准只有一个：对方是谁，你们什么关系。

| 维度 | 正式邮件/书信 | 半正式邮件 | 非正式邮件 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 称呼 | Dear Mr. Smith, | Dear Sarah, / Hello Sarah, | Hi team, / Hey Ben, |
| 开头 | I am writing to... / I hope this email finds you well. | I hope you had a good weekend. | Just a quick note to say... |
| 收尾 | I look forward to hearing from you. | Let me know what you think. | Cheers! / Talk soon! |
| 落款 | Yours sincerely, / Kind regards, | Best regards, | Best, / Thanks, |
| 缩写 | 避免（use "do not"） | 少量（I'm, it's） | 随意（gonna 也别写，仍要拼写完整） |

对比感受一下：

> **正式**：Dear Mr. Smith, I am writing to request an extension for the essay deadline. ...
> **同事群发**：Hi team, Just a quick note to share tomorrow's meeting agenda. ...

同一个人，换了对象，整套语言皮肤都要换。**对导师写 Hi there 是事故，对室友写 Yours sincerely 是喜剧**。写之前先问自己：这封信落在这张表的哪一格？

## 常见误区

| ❌ 错误示范 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dear Mr. Smith: Yours, Tom（格式混乱，称呼冒号配亲密落款） | Dear Mr. Smith, ... Yours sincerely, Li Ming | 称呼和落款的正式度必须同一档。对 Smith 先生用 Yours 就像穿西装配拖鞋 |
| I suggest that you **should** take the metro. | I suggest that you **take** the metro. | suggest that 后接虚拟语气动词原形。加 should 不是错（英式可接受），但考试标准答案用原形，最稳 |
| 开头直接进入细节：The party is on June 20th... | 先亮身份和来意：I am writing on behalf of... to invite you... | 英文书信第一功能句是"我为什么写"。没有开头句，读者像半路拦下一辆已经开动的公交车 |
| 投诉信情绪上头：Your product is garbage and your service is a joke! | I was disappointed to find that the product did not work properly. | 愤怒降低可信度。克制的描述 + 明确的诉求，才是最有力的投诉。骂人解决不了问题，证据和条款才解决 |
| 落款写自己的真名 Li Huaixin | 按题目指令署名（通常是 Li Ming / Zhang Wei） | 考试署名错误可能扣分。题目让你演谁你就演谁 |

## 实战练习

**A. 格式与语域判断（第 1~4 题）**

1. 给从未谋面的酒店经理写投诉信，称呼和落款分别用什么？
2. 下面这封给导师的邮件有 3 处语域错误，找出来并改正：

> Hey Prof. Lee,
> I wanna ask if I can hand in my paper later. Thx!
> Cheers,
> Ming

3. "Yours faithfully" 和 "Yours sincerely" 的分工是什么？一句话说清。
4. 判断对错：给好朋友写感谢便条，用 "I am writing to express my sincere gratitude" 开头很得体。

**B. 句型填空与改写（第 5~8 题）**

5. 填空：I am writing ______ you to our graduation ceremony at 3 p.m. on July 1st.（邀请信）
6. 用 It would be beneficial if... 改写：You should book tickets early.（建议信）
7. 用 I would appreciate it if... 改写：Please reply within three days.（投诉信）
8. 填空：______ your help, I passed the interview smoothly.（感谢信，填一个双词短语）

**C. 段落写作（第 9 题）**

9. 写一段建议信的第二段（主体段）：朋友 Lucy 想提高英语口语，请你提 3 条建议，每条 = 建议句 + 理由句（5~7 句）。

**D. 真题实战（第 10~11 题）**：四六级风格，完整写一封信（15~25 分钟计时）。

10. **（邀请信）** 假设你是学生会的李明，你们大学将于 6 月 20 日举办"国际文化节"。请给你的外国朋友 Chris 写一封邮件，邀请他参加并介绍活动安排（各国美食展、文艺表演、语言交换角），并告知他回复截止时间。
11. **（感谢信）** 假设你是李明，上个月你参加了一个社区志愿者项目，项目负责人王女士在你受伤时开车送你就医并垫付了药费。给她写一封感谢信，说明事情经过并表达谢意。

**E. 自由写作（第 12 题）**

12. 选一个真实场景给你真实生活中的某个人写一封英文邮件（外教、客服、国外网友均可），用上本课格式。发出去之前用正式度表格自检一遍。

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看参考答案与点评</summary>

**第 1 题**：Dear Sir or Madam, 开头，Yours faithfully, 落款。
点评：从未谋面 → 最正式格。注意题目如果给了经理姓名（如 Mr. Brown），则用 Dear Mr. Brown, + Yours sincerely。

**第 2 题**：三处错误——① Hey Prof. Lee → Dear Professor Lee,（对导师不能用 Hey）；② I wanna... Thx! → I would like to ask whether I could submit my paper a few days later. Thank you for your consideration.（wanna/Thx 是聊天体）；③ Cheers, Ming → Yours sincerely, / Kind regards, Li Ming（Cheers 太随意，且署名要写全名）。
点评：改完后再读一遍，语气从"哥们"变成"学生"，这就是语域的力量。

**第 3 题**：称呼收信人姓名时（Dear Mr. Smith）用 Yours sincerely；不知道对方姓名时（Dear Sir or Madam）用 Yours faithfully。
点评：一句话口诀：知道名字 sincere，不知道名字 faithful。

**第 4 题**：错。对亲密朋友用如此隆重的句型显得疏远做作，像用法律文书感谢室友带饭。朋友之间一句 "Thank you so much for..." 加具体细节就足够真诚。

**第 5 题**：I am writing **to invite** you to our graduation ceremony at 3 p.m. on July 1st.
点评：invite 是动词。对比拓展：感谢信用 to express my gratitude，建议信用 to offer suggestions，投诉信用 to complain about。

**第 6 题**：**It would be beneficial if you booked** tickets early.
点评：It would be + 形容词 + if 句型中，if 从句用过去式 booked 表委婉建议，不是指过去时间。这是建议信最显功力的句型。

**第 7 题**：**I would appreciate it if you could reply** within three days.
点评：注意 appreciate 后面的 it 不能丢（appreciate it if 是固定搭配）。这句比 Please reply 语气正式且不失礼貌，投诉信标配结尾。

**第 8 题**：**Thanks to** your help, I passed the interview smoothly.
点评：Thanks to = 多亏，注意大写 T 和复数 s。对比：Thank you for your help 是普通感谢句，Thanks to 强调因果关系。

**第 9 题**（参考段）：
> First, I suggest that you join the English Corner held in the central park every Saturday morning, because speaking with real people beats talking to an app. Second, it would be beneficial if you watched English vlogs with subtitles turned off, as training your ears is the first step to opening your mouth. Finally, you might also consider recording yourself for two minutes every day, which helps you notice mistakes you never hear yourself making.
点评：三条建议用 First/Second/Finally 排开，每条都由"建议句 + as/because/which 理由句"组成，还顺手用上了第 7 课的定语从句合并。

**第 10 题**（参考范文）：
> Dear Chris,
>
> I am writing to invite you to the International Culture Festival of our university, which will be held in the main square from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. on June 20th.
>
> The festival will feature three main events. Food stalls run by international students will offer dishes from over twenty countries. In the afternoon, there will be performances of folk music and dance on the open-air stage. My favorite part is the Language Exchange Corner, where you can teach visitors a few words of French and pick up some Chinese in return. As a native speaker of French, you would be a perfect guest of honor.
>
> Please let me know by June 15th whether you can come, so that I can reserve a seat for you. I am looking forward to your reply.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming
点评：第一段亮明来意 + 时间地点；第二段三个活动各一句，最后一句"为什么是你"是邀请信的加分细节；第三段给出答复期限。结构完全是模板三段。

**第 11 题**（参考范文）：
> Dear Ms. Wang,
>
> I would like to express my sincere gratitude for your kindness during my stay in the volunteer program last month.
>
> When I cut my hand badly while cleaning the river bank, you drove me to the hospital without a second thought and even paid for my medicine before I could contact my family. What touched me most was that you visited me the next day with fruit and a get-well card signed by all the volunteers. Thanks to your quick reaction, my hand healed within two weeks without any infection.
>
> I feel truly grateful to have worked under such a caring leader. If there is ever anything I can do for you or the program, please do not hesitate to let me know.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming
点评：第二段"送医 + 垫付 + 探望"三层细节层层递进，比只说"你对我很好"具体一百倍。感谢信的说服力全在细节里。

**第 12 题**：开放题。自检要点：称呼落款是否同档、开头是否有功能句、缩写是否与正式度匹配。发出去的那一刻，本课就毕业了。
