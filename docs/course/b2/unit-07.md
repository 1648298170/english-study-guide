## 本课目标

- [ ] 我能准确区分五对"意义有别"的动词：remember / forget / stop / try / regret 接 to do 还是 doing
- [ ] 我能用动名词（doing）作主语造句（Reading in bed is her hobby.）
- [ ] 我能熟练使用 it is + adj + to do 句型
- [ ] 我能用 prefer A to B 和 prefer to do rather than do 表达偏好
- [ ] 我能分清 would like to do 与 feel like doing，并掌握 look forward to doing 等高频搭配

## 生词表

| try | /traɪ/ | v. | 尝试；努力 | Try pressing the red button; that usually works. |

## 语法讲解

**不定式**（infinitive）就是 to + 动词原形（to do）；**动名词**（gerund）就是动词的 -ing 形式作名词用（doing）。有些动词后面接 to do，有些接 doing，还有一些两者都能接但**意思不同**。这一课的精髓就在最后这类词上：选 to do 还是 doing，直接决定你想表达什么。

### 一、意义有别五对重点：接 to do 还是 doing，意思完全不同

| 动词 | + to do（多指"未来的事"） | + doing（多指"已发生的事"） |
|---|---|---|
| remember | 记得要去做（事还没做） | 记得做过（事已发生） |
| forget | 忘记要去做（事还没做） | 忘记做过（做过但忘了） |
| stop | 停下来去做另一件事 | 停止正在做的事 |
| try | 努力做（克服困难） | 尝试换方法做 |
| regret | 遗憾地要（说/告知） | 后悔做过 |

逐对体会（每组 2~3 个中英对照）：

**remember / forget**：

- 出门时记得关灯。（Remember to turn off the lights when you leave.）→ 灯还没关，提醒去做。
- 我记得去年见过他一次。（I remember meeting him once last year.）→ 见面已发生，现在还记得。
- 别忘了明天给我打电话。（Don't forget to call me tomorrow.）
- 我永远不会忘记第一次看到大海。（I'll never forget seeing the ocean for the first time.）

**stop**：

- 他在路边停下来买了个汉堡。（He stopped to buy a burger on the way.）→ 停下（车/脚步）是为了买汉堡。
- 五年前他戒烟了。（He stopped smoking five years ago.）→ 把"抽烟"这件事停掉了。
- 老师停下来喝了口水，然后继续讲课。（The teacher stopped to drink some water and then went on.）

**try**：

- 我整晚都想睡着，但就是睡不着。（I tried to fall asleep all night, but I just couldn't.）→ 努力做难做成的事。
- 门打不开？试试用另一把钥匙。（The door won't open? Try using the other key.）→ 换个方法试试。
- 她努力不笑出来，但没忍住。（She tried not to laugh, but she couldn't help it.）

**regret**：

- 我很遗憾地通知你，你的申请被拒绝了。（I regret to tell you that your application was rejected.）→ 即将说的话令人遗憾，固定搭配 regret to say / to tell / to inform。
- 我很后悔说了那些话。（I regret saying those things.）→ 说过的话已发生，现在后悔。

记忆锚点：**to do 面向未来（还没做），doing 回望过去（已发生）**。stop 和 try 稍特殊（stop to do = 停下来去做；try doing = 换招），单独记。

### 二、动名词作主语：把一件事当名词用

动词短语想当主语，必须把动词变成 doing：

- 躺在床上看书是她最大的爱好。（**Reading in bed** is her favorite hobby.）
- 早睡早起有益健康。（**Going to bed early** is good for your health.）
- 跟他讲道理没用。（**Talking to him** is useless. / It's no use talking to him.）

对比错误直感：中文说"看书很好"，初学者容易直译成 *Read in bed is her hobby* 或 *To reading...*，都不对。主语位置上要么是动名词 doing，要么是不定式 to do（泛指习惯性的事优先 doing）。

| ✅ 正确 | ❌ 错误 |
|---|---|
| Swimming is fun. | Swim is fun. / To swimming is fun. |
| Learning a language takes time. | Learn a language takes time. |

### 三、it is + 形容词 + to do：把长主语后移

当 to do 那串太长、头重脚轻时，英语习惯用 it 占位，把真主语放句尾（这个 it 叫"形式主语"）：

- 睡个好觉很重要。（**It is important to sleep** well.）
- 在高峰期开车穿过这座城市很难。（**It is difficult to drive** across this city during rush hour.）
- 跟她说实话可能不太容易。（**It may not be easy to tell** her the truth.）

| 结构 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| It is + adj + to do | It is dangerous to swim here at night. |
| It takes + 时间 + to do | It takes two hours to finish the report. |
| It is + adj + for sb + to do | It is hard for me to get up early. |

### 四、prefer 的两个句型：A 与 B 比偏好

**句型一：prefer A to B**（A、B 都是名词或动名词，注意用 to 不用 than）：

- 咖啡和茶比，我更喜欢咖啡。（I prefer **coffee to** tea.）
- 我喜欢走路胜过开车。（I prefer **walking to driving**.）

**句型二：prefer to do A rather than do B**（接动词时用这个）：

- 我宁愿自己做饭，也不想天天点外卖。（I **prefer to cook** at home **rather than order** takeout every day.）

两句话型不能杂交：说 *prefer tea than coffee* 或 *prefer to walk to driving* 都是错的。参考对照：

| ✅ 正确 | ❌ 错误 |
|---|---|
| I prefer tea to coffee. | I prefer tea than coffee. |
| I prefer to stay home rather than go out. | I prefer to stay home rather than going out. |

### 五、would like to do vs feel like doing + 高频固定搭配

两个都表示"想要"，但结构完全不同：

| 表达 | 结构 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| would like to do | + to do | I **would like to order** a pizza.（我想点个披萨。礼貌客气） |
| feel like doing | + doing | I **feel like eating** something sweet.（我想吃点甜的。口语随性） |

两者都接"要做的事"，一个带 to，一个不带，恰好是本章主题的缩影。

其他高频固定搭配，直接整组记：

| + to do | + doing |
|---|---|
| can't afford to do（负担不起） | look forward to doing（期待） |
| manage to do（设法做成） | avoid doing（避免） |
| decide to do（决定） | suggest doing（建议） |
| pretend to do（假装） | risk doing（冒险） |
| would like to do（想要） | feel like doing（想要） |
| hope to do（希望） | can't help doing（忍不住） |

注意 look forward to 里的 to 是**介词**，后面必须接 doing，这是中国学习者错误率最高的一处（详见下节）。

## 中文母语者常见错误

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| I look forward to see you next week. | I look forward to **seeing** you next week. | look forward to 的 to 是介词，后面接 doing。高频错误，面试邮件里踩雷格外尴尬。 |
| My father stopped to smoke for his health last year.（想表达戒烟） | My father **stopped smoking** for his health last year. | stop to smoke 是"停下来去抽烟"，意思正好说反了。戒烟是 stop smoking。 |
| I suggest to go by train. | I suggest **going** by train. | suggest 后面只能接 doing，不能接 to do。 |
| Read in bed is my favorite hobby. | **Reading in bed** is my favorite hobby. | 动词不能直接作主语，必须变成动名词。 |
| I prefer tea more than coffee. | I prefer tea **to** coffee. | prefer A to B 是固定结构，介词用 to，不加 more than。 |
| I feel like to watch a movie tonight. | I feel like **watching** a movie tonight. | feel like 后面接 doing，不是 to do。would like 才接 to do。 |
| I can't afford buying a new laptop. | I can't afford **to buy** a new laptop. | afford 接 to do，不接 doing。 |

## 练习

**A. 选择题：选出正确形式。**（1~8）

1. Please remember ______ the window before you leave.
   A. closing　B. to close　C. close　D. closed
2. I'll never forget ______ Paris for the first time.
   A. to visit　B. visit　C. visiting　D. visited
3. He stopped ______ a coffee because he felt sleepy.
   A. drinking　B. to drink　C. drink　D. drank
4. The remote doesn't work. Try ______ the batteries.
   A. to change　B. changing　C. change　D. changed
5. I regret ______ you that your flight has been canceled.
   A. telling　B. to tell　C. tell　D. told
6. We look forward to ______ from you soon.
   A. hear　B. hearing　C. to hear　D. heard
7. She suggested ______ the meeting until Friday.
   A. to put off　B. putting off　C. put off　D. puts off
8. It is dangerous ______ the river here.
   A. swimming　B. to swimming　C. to swim　D. swim

**B. 填空：用括号内动词的正确形式（to do 或 doing）填空。**（9~18）

9. My sister can't afford ______ (go) on vacation this year.
10. He avoided ______ (answer) my question directly.
11. ______ (read) in poor light hurts your eyes.
12. It takes me forty minutes ______ (walk) to the station.
13. I prefer ______ (take) the subway rather than ______ (drive) during rush hour.
14. Do you feel like ______ (watch) a movie tonight?
15. The little boy pretended ______ (be) asleep when his mom walked in.
16. We managed ______ (finish) everything before the deadline.
17. Don't risk ______ (lose) your passport; keep it in the hotel safe.
18. I regret ______ (not study) harder when I was in school.

**C. 改错：每句有一处错误，写出正确句子。**（19~22）

19. Swim in the sea is my favorite summer activity.
20. I would like inviting you to my birthday party.
21. He tried passing the exam three times and finally made it.（语境：他很努力地想通过考试，考了三次）
22. I prefer coffee more than tea.

**D. 翻译。**（23~26）

23. 记得明天把书带来。（还没带）
24. 我忘不了在机场见到她的那一幕。
25. 我期待着收到你的回复。
26. 我遗憾地告诉你，我们不得不取消这次旅行。
