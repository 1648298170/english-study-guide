## 本课目标

同事问你"老板刚才说什么了"，朋友问"她跟你说了什么"，你每天都要转述别人的话。中文转述很简单，把原话重复一遍就行；英语要动三样东西：时态、语序、时间地点词。这一课就是把这些"机关"一个个拆开装好。

学完这一课，你应该能做到：

- [ ] 把直接引语改成间接引语，正确完成时态后移（will→would，am/is→was 等）
- [ ] say 和 tell 的用法不再出错：tell 后面必须跟人，say 后面不直接跟人
- [ ] 转述一般疑问句（if/whether + 陈述语序）和特殊疑问句（疑问词 + 陈述语序）
- [ ] 知道 tomorrow、here、now 这类词转述时要变成 the next day、there、then
- [ ] 掌握 16 个常用转述动词，让转述不再只有 said 和 told

## 语法讲解

### 第 1 步：直接引语 vs 间接引语

- 直接引语：原话照搬，加引号。**"I am tired," she said.**
- 间接引语：用自己的话转述，不用引号。**She said (that) she was tired.**

转述时通常要动三样东西：

1. 人称：I 变 she/he，you 变 me/him/her（跟着说话对象变）
2. 时态：往后退一步（下一节讲）
3. 时间地点词：tomorrow 变 the next day，here 变 there（第 6 步讲）

that 可以省略，口语里几乎都说 She said she was tired.

### 第 2 步：时态后移：把时态往回退一格

主句动词是过去时（said / told / asked）时，从句时态通常要"后移"（backshift，即往后退一步）：

| 直接引语里的时态 | 后移成 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般现在时 → 一般过去时 | "I want coffee." → She said she **wanted** coffee. |
| 现在进行时 → 过去进行时 | "I'm reading." → He said he **was reading**. |
| 一般过去时 / 现在完成时 → 过去完成时 | "I saw him." / "I have seen him." → She said she **had seen** him. |
| will → would | "I'll call you." → He said he **would** call me. |
| can → could | "I can come." → She said she **could** come. |
| must → had to | "I must finish it." → He said he **had to** finish it. |

例外：转述的内容到现在仍然成立，可以不后移：

- He said the earth **goes** around the sun.（真理不会变）
- She said she **lives** in Beijing.（她现在仍住北京）

第 1 课的过去完成时在这里派上大用场：原话里的一般过去时和现在完成时，后移后统统变成 had + 过去分词。

### 第 3 步：say vs tell：中文母语者的头号错误

铁律一句话：**tell 后面必须跟"人"，say 后面不能直接跟"人"。**

| 动词 | 搭配 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| tell | tell + 人 + 话 | She **told me** (that) she was busy. |
| tell | tell + 人 + to do（转述命令） | He **told me to** wait outside. |
| say | say + 话（不加人） | She **said** (that) she was busy. |
| say | say to + 人 + 话（语法正确，但日常更常用 told） | She **said to me** that she was busy. |

❌ She said me that she was busy. 这是中文母语者最常见的错误，考试必考。

记忆法：tell 是"告诉某个人"，必须有人接收；say 只是"说出内容"。中文的"她说我……"容易让人把 said 和 me 直接连起来，一定要警惕。

### 第 4 步：转述一般疑问句：if / whether + 陈述语序

原话：**"Are you tired?"**

转述：**She asked if I was tired.**（她问我累不累。）

规则两条：

1. 加 if 或 whether（意思是"是否"）
2. 改成陈述语序（主语在动词前），不再倒装，也不要助动词 do/did

更多例子：

- "Do you live here?" → He asked **if I lived there**.
- "Have you finished the report?" → She asked **if I had finished the report**.
- "Will you come tomorrow?" → He asked **whether I would come the next day**.

❌ She asked was I tired. ❌ He asked did I live there.

### 第 5 步：转述特殊疑问句：疑问词 + 陈述语序

原话：**"Where do you live?"**

转述：**She asked where I lived.**（她问我住在哪里。）

疑问词（what / where / when / why / how 等）保留，后面接陈述语序：

- "What time does the meeting start?" → He asked **what time the meeting started**.
- "Why are you late?" → She asked **why I was late**.
- "How did you get here?" → He asked **how I had gotten there**.

❌ She asked where did I live.（考试高频错误：疑问词后不能再倒装）

### 第 6 步：时间、地点、指示词的变化

如果转述时时间和地点已经变了，这些词也要跟着变：

| 直接引语 | 间接引语 |
|------|------|
| today | that day |
| tonight | that night |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| next week | the next week / the following week |
| last night | the night before |
| ago | before |
| now | then |
| here | there |
| this | that |

例：He said, "I'll call you tomorrow." → He said he would call me **the next day**.

补充一条：转述祈使句（叫别人做或不做某事），用 tell/ask + 人 + (not) to do：

- "Please close the door." → She **asked me to close** the door.
- "Don't be late." → He **told me not to be** late.

## 中文母语者常见错误

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 为什么 |
|------|------|------|
| She said me that she was tired. | She **told me** (that) she was tired. 或 She **said** (that) she was tired. | say 后面不能直接跟人；tell 后面必须跟人 |
| He asked me where did I work. | He asked me **where I worked**. | 间接引语用陈述语序，疑问句的倒装和 did 都要去掉 |
| She asked was I happy with the result. | She asked **if/whether I was** happy with the result. | 一般疑问句转述要加 if 或 whether，再加陈述语序 |
| He said he will come tomorrow. | He said he **would** come **the next day**. | 时态后移 will→would；时间已过，tomorrow 变 the next day |
| Tom told that he had finished the report. | Tom **said** that he had finished the report. 或 Tom **told me** that... | tell 后面必须有人，不能直接接 that 从句 |
| She told me don't be late. | She told me **not to be** late. | 转述祈使句用 not to do，不用 don't |

## 练习

### 一、填空：完成时态后移和词汇变化

1. "I am very tired." → She said that she ______ very tired.
2. "I will help you tomorrow." → He said that he ______ help me ______.
3. "I saw that movie last week." → She said that she ______ that movie ______.
4. "I can't come to the party." → He told me that he ______ come to the party.
5. "We are living in Beijing now." → They said that they ______ living in Beijing ______.
6. "I have finished my homework." → She said that she ______ her homework.

### 二、改写：把直接引语改成间接引语

7. "Where do you work?" → She asked me where ______.
8. "Do you like Chinese food?" → He asked me if ______.
9. "Don't touch the wet paint." → She told me ______.
10. "I'll call you tonight," Lily said to me. → Lily told me ______.

### 三、改错：每句有一处错，找出并改正

11. She said me that the meeting was on Friday.
12. He asked me what time was it.
13. Tom told that he had finished the report.

### 四、中译英：翻译下面的句子

14. 她问我住在哪里。
15. 他告诉我他第二天会很忙。
16. 老师叫我们不要忘记交作业。

### 五、情景应用

17. 情景：早上同事 Jack 对你说："I'm not feeling well. I have to leave early today. Please tell the boss." 中午老板问 Jack 去哪了。请用两三句间接引语向老板转述 Jack 的话。
18. 写 3 组"直接引语 + 间接引语"对照句：先写下家人或朋友昨天对你说的一句原话（加引号），再把它改成间接引语。
