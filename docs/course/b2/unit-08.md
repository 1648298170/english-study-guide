## 语法讲解

### 一、B2 七课语法总复习大表

先总览，再逐条自问"我能不看书各举一例吗"。答不上来的，回对应课重学那一节。

| 课 | 语法主题 | 核心规则 | 一句话例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 第 1 课 | 过去完成时（had + 过去分词） | 表示"在过去某个时间点之前已完成"的动作 | When I got to the station, the train **had** already **left**. |
| 第 2 课 | 第三条件句（过去虚拟） | if + had done，主句 would/could/might have done；谈过去的假设，与现实相反 | **If I had known**, I **would have called** you. |
| 第 3 课 | 间接引语（转述） | 时态后移一步、人称和时空词相应变化；tell sb that（必须有宾语）、say that | She **said (that) she was** tired **that day**. |
| 第 4 课 | 被动语态 | be + 过去分词；强调承受者或让表述更客观 | This bridge **was built** in 1990. |
| 第 5 课 | 推测情态 | must be（一定）/ might be（可能）/ can't be（不可能）；推测过去用 must / might / can't have done | The lights are off. They **must be** out. |
| 第 6 课 | 冠词系统 | a/an 首次提及看发音；the 特指/独一无二/最高级；泛指复数与不可数、三餐、球类、专有名词零冠词 | **The** moon is bright. / **Dogs** are loyal. |
| 第 7 课 | 高级不定式与动名词 | remember to do（记得要做）vs remember doing（记得做过）等五对；动名词作主语；It is adj to do；prefer A to B | I look forward to **seeing** you. |

### 二、高频"踩雷点"串讲

改错题的全部陷阱都来自下面这张表，先自己过一遍：

| 雷点 | 错误示范 | 正确 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 否定推测误用 mustn't | He mustn't be home. | He **can't be** home. | 第 5 课 |
| 推测过去漏 have | He can't passed. | He can't **have passed**. | 第 5 课 |
| 专有名词加 the | the China、the Shanghai | **China** / **Shanghai** | 第 6 课 |
| 泛指复数加 the | I love the dogs.（泛指） | I love **dogs**. | 第 6 课 |
| 独一无二漏 the | Moon is bright. | **The** moon is bright. | 第 6 课 |
| tell 缺宾语 | She told that... | She **told me** that... / She **said** that... | 第 3 课 |
| 间接引语时态不后移 | He said he is tired. | He said he **was** tired. | 第 3 课 |
| 被动语态用过去式 | The cake was ate. | The cake was **eaten**. | 第 4 课 |
| 虚拟条件句用 would | If I would have known... | If I **had known**... | 第 2 课 |
| 过去完成漏 had | The movie already started.（到站前） | The movie **had** already started. | 第 1 课 |
| stop to do / stop doing 混淆 | 戒烟说成 stop to smoke | stop **smoking** | 第 7 课 |
| 介词 to 后接原形 | look forward to see | look forward to **seeing** | 第 7 课 |

## 中文母语者常见错误

本课的错误表直接从笔试改错题里提炼，先睹为快（细节见练习解析）：

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| He mustn't be at work now; it's 10 p.m. | He **can't be** at work now; it's 10 p.m. | mustn't 是"禁止"，否定推测只能用 can't。 |
| The China has a long history. | **China** has a long history. | 国家名零冠词。 |
| She told that she would come later. | She **told me** that... / She **said** that... | tell 必须带宾语，say 不带。 |
| My dad stopped to smoke last year and feels much better. | My dad **stopped smoking** last year... | stop to smoke 是"停下来去抽烟"，戒烟是 stop smoking。 |
| If I would have known you were in town, I would have visited you. | If I **had known** you were in town... | 第三条件句 if 从句用 had done，不用 would have done。 |
| When we got to the cinema, the movie already started. | ...the movie **had already started**. | 到达之前的动作要用过去完成时。 |

## 练习

### 第一关 · 改错闯关（12 题）

每句恰有一处语法错误，找出来并写出正确句子。

1. He mustn't be at work now; it's 10 p.m.
2. The China has a history of thousands of years.
3. She told that she would come to the party later.
4. My dad stopped to smoke last year, and now he feels much better.
5. If I would have known you were in town, I would have visited you.
6. The whole cake was ate by the kids in five minutes.
7. When we got to the cinema, the movie already started.
8. I look forward to see you at the conference next week.
9. She said me that she was too tired to come.
10. I love the cats; they are such clean animals.（泛指）
11. He can't pass the exam; he didn't even open the book.（语境：考试已经结束，说话人在推测）
12. Remember bringing your umbrella tomorrow; it might rain.

### 第二关 · 综合笔试 35 题（限时 60 分钟）

**一、选择题（第 13~22 题）**

13. When I got to the station, the train ______.
    A. left　B. had left　C. was leaving　D. has left
14. If we ______ earlier, we wouldn't have missed the flight.
    A. left　B. had left　C. would leave　D. have left
15. She said she ______ already finished the report.
    A. has　B. had　C. has had　D. is
16. English ______ in more than fifty countries.
    A. speaks　B. is spoken　C. is speaking　D. spoke
17. The door is locked and the lights are off. They ______ out.
    A. must be　B. can't be　C. must go　D. might going
18. He can't ______ the test; he didn't study at all.
    A. pass　B. have passed　C. passed　D. passing
19. She plays ______ piano beautifully.
    A. a　B. an　C. the　D. \-\-
20. ______ water freezes at 0°C.（泛指）
    A. A　B. An　C. The　D. \-\-
21. Don't forget ______ the lights when you leave.
    A. turning off　B. to turn off　C. turn off　D. turned off
22. The remote control doesn't work. Try ______ the batteries.
    A. change　B. to change　C. changing　D. changed

**二、填空题（第 23~32 题：用括号内提示的正确形式或正确的冠词填空）**

23. By the time we arrived, the movie ______ (already, start).
24. If I ______ (know) it was your birthday, I would have bought a cake.
25. He said he ______ (will) call me the next day.
26. This bridge ______ (build) in 1990.
27. The floor is wet. It ______ (must) be raining outside.
28. She isn't answering her phone. She ______ (might, leave) it at home.
29. I go to work by ______ subway every day.（填冠词）
30. ______ moon goes around ______ earth.
31. I remember ______ (lock) the door, so don't worry; it's locked.
32. He avoided ______ (answer) my question.

**三、中译英（第 33~42 题）**

33. 我到车站的时候，火车已经开走了。
34. 如果我早点起床，就不会迟到了。
35. 她说她那天非常累。
36. 这本书是 2020 年写的。
37. 灯都关着，他们一定外出了。
38. 他不可能听到这个消息，不然他早就打电话给我了。
39. 今晚月亮真亮。
40. 我永远不会忘记第一次见到你。
41. 我期待着收到你的来信。
42. 她建议晚饭后去散步。

**四、句型转换（第 43~47 题，按括号要求改写，一空一词）**

43. "I am very happy," she said. → She said (that) she ______ very happy.
44. Someone stole my bike last week. → My bike ______ ______ last week.（改为被动）
45. He left early, so he caught the train. → If he ______ ______ early, he wouldn't have caught the train.（改为第三条件句）
46. I'm sure he is at home; the lights are on. → The lights are on. He ______ ______ at home.（用 must 改写）
47. It started to rain. We stopped playing soccer. → It started to rain, so we ______ ______ soccer.（合并为一句，用 stop doing）

### 第三关 · 口语自测 5 任务

每项准备 2 分钟，连续说 1~2 分钟并录音。按文末三档量表自评。

- **任务 1（第 1 课）**：讲一次你"到早了"或"到晚了"的经历：你到达时什么事已经发生了？（至少用 2 个过去完成时）
- **任务 2（第 2 课）**：讲一件你后悔的事，用第三条件句说说"如果当初……就会……"。（至少 2 句 If I had..., I would/could/might have...）
- **任务 3（第 3 课）**：转述家人或朋友最近说过的三句话（时态正确后移）。
- **任务 4（第 4 课）**：介绍一项中国发明（造纸术、纸币、高铁……），被动语态至少用 3 处。
- **任务 5（第 5 课）**：隔壁很吵，说说你对邻居在干什么的推测（must / might / can't 各至少一次，过去推测至少一次）。

## 练习答案

### 改错闯关解析

1. **mustn't → can't**。否定推测用 can't：He can't be at work now; it's 10 p.m.
2. **删 the**。国家名零冠词：China has a history of thousands of years.
3. **told 后加宾语（或改 said）**。She told me that she would come... / She said that she would come...
4. **stopped to smoke → stopped smoking**。戒烟是"停止抽烟"：My dad stopped smoking last year...
5. **would have known → had known**。第三条件句 if 从句用 had done：If I had known you were in town...
6. **ate → eaten**。被动语态用过去分词：The whole cake was eaten by the kids...
7. **already started → had already started**。到达电影院之前电影已开始，过去完成时：...the movie had already started.
8. **see → seeing**。look forward to 的 to 是介词：I look forward to seeing you...
9. **said me → said to me（或 told me）**。say 不直接接人：She said to me that... / She told me that...
10. **删 the**。泛指一类用复数零冠词：I love cats...
11. **can't pass → can't have passed**。推测已结束的考试用 can't have done：He can't have passed the exam...
12. **bringing → bring**。明天还没发生，"记得要带"用 remember to do：Remember to bring your umbrella tomorrow...

### 综合笔试解析

**一、选择题**

13. **B**（火车在"我到站"之前已离开 → 过去完成时）
14. **B**（第三条件句 if + had left）
15. **B**（间接引语时态后移：had already finished）
16. **B**（被动：is spoken）
17. **A**（锁门+关灯，强证据推测 → must be）
18. **B**（推测过去 → can't have passed）
19. **C**（西洋乐器 the piano）
20. **D**（泛指不可数零冠词：Water freezes...）
21. **B**（离开时"记得要关"，事没做 → to turn off）
22. **C**（换方法试 → try changing）

**二、填空题**

23. **had already started**（by the time + 过去时，主句用过去完成）
24. **had known**（第三条件句）
25. **would**（will → would，时态后移）
26. **was built**（被动 + 过去时间）
27. **must**（地面湿 → must be raining，推测进行中）
28. **might have left**（推测过去）
29. **零冠词（不填）**（by subway 交通方式）
30. **The; the**（独一无二）
31. **locking**（门已锁，"记得锁过" → remember doing）
32. **answering**（avoid + doing）

**三、中译英（评分点：语法 2 分 + 关键结构 1 分，每句 3 分；只要评分点达成，用词不同不扣分）**

33. When I got to the station, the train **had already left（过去完成时 had + left）**.
34. **If I had gotten up earlier（if + had done）**, I **wouldn't have been late（would not have done）**.
35. She said (that) she **was（时态后移）** very tired **that day（that day 替换 today）**.
36. This book **was written（被动 was + written）** in 2020.
37. The lights are off; they **must be out（must + 原形）**.
38. He **can't have heard（can't have + 过去分词）** the news; otherwise he **would have called（虚拟结果）** me.
39. **The moon（独一无二的 the）** is bright tonight.
40. I'll never forget **meeting（忘记/记得做过 → doing）** you for the first time.
41. I look forward to **hearing（介词 to + doing）** from you.
42. She suggested **going（suggest + doing）** for a walk after dinner.

**四、句型转换**

43. **was**（am → was 时态后移）
44. **was stolen**（一般过去被动）
45. **hadn't left**（反事实：正因为他早走了才赶上，反过来 If he hadn't left early, he wouldn't have caught the train）
46. **must be**（推测）
47. **stopped playing**（stop doing = 停止正在做的事）
