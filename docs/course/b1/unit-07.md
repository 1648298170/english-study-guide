## 本课目标

学完这一课，你可以做到：

- [ ] 用 because 和 so 正确表达因果，不再说出 Because...so... 这种"中式成对"句子
- [ ] 用 although / though 表达"虽然"，并记住它永远不跟 but 连用
- [ ] 用 however 书面地表达转折，逗号位置不出错
- [ ] 用 and / or / but 连接并列的词、短语和句子
- [ ] 分清 for example 和 such as 两种"例如"，会用 in fact / actually 补充和修正观点

## 语法讲解

这一课的主角是连词（conjunction，把词、短语或句子连接起来的词）。中文里"因为……所以……""虽然……但是……"总是成对出现，而英语的规则正好相反：**一对关系，只用一个词**。这是中文母语者的头号连词错误来源，这一课就把它彻底解决。

### 第一步：because 和 so，一个说因，一个说果

because 引导"原因"那一半，so 引导"结果"那一半。同一个句子只能选一个。

| 连词 | 意思 | 引导哪一半 | 位置 |
| ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| because | 因为 | 原因 | 可前可后 |
| so | 所以 | 结果 | 通常在后半句 |

同一个意思，英语有两种说法：

- **I went to bed early because I was tired.** 我早睡了，因为我累了。（because 说因）
- **I was tired, so I went to bed early.** 我累了，所以我早睡了。（so 说果）

再看两组：

- **She didn't come because she was sick.** 她没来，因为她病了。
- **She was sick, so she didn't come.** 她病了，所以没来。
- **We stayed home because it was raining.** 因为下雨，我们待在家里。
- **It was raining, so we stayed home.** 下雨了，所以我们待在家里。

注意 because 后面跟完整句子，"因为名词"要说 **because of**：

- **We stayed home because of the rain.** 因为雨，我们待在家里。（rain 是名词，用 because of）
- **We stayed home because it rained.** 因为下雨，我们待在家里。（it rained 是句子，用 because）

### 第二步：although 和 though，"虽然"不配 but

although 表示"虽然、尽管"，引导让步的那一半。中文说"虽然……但是……"，英语里 although 和 but 也是**只能用一个**。

- **Although it was cold, we swam in the sea.** 虽然天气很冷，我们还是在海里游泳了。
- **We swam in the sea although it was cold.** 我们在海里游泳了，虽然天气很冷。
- **Although the test was hard, everyone passed.** 虽然考试难，大家都通过了。
- **He kept working although he was tired.** 虽然很累，他还在继续工作。

though 和 although 意思几乎一样，though 更口语，though 还可以放在句尾：

- **It's hard work. I like it, though.** 这活儿不轻松，不过我喜欢。

### 第三步：however，书面的"然而"，必须带逗号

however 比 but 更正式，常见于书面语。它不直接连在句子里，而是**另起一句，后面加逗号**：

- **The hotel was expensive. However, the food was great.** 酒店很贵，然而食物很棒。
- **I studied hard. However, I failed the test.** 我学习很努力，然而考试没及格。
- **The phone looks old. However, it works perfectly.** 这部手机看着旧，然而运行完美。

but 和 however 对比：

| | but | however |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| 语体 | 口语、书面都常用 | 偏正式、书面 |
| 位置 | 句中，前面加逗号 | 句首（或插在句中），后面必须加逗号 |
| 例句 | It's cheap, **but** it's slow. | It's cheap. **However,** it's slow. |

### 第四步：and、or、but，并列三兄弟

| 连词 | 关系 | 例句 |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| and | 顺接"和、并且" | I finished my homework **and** watched a movie. 我写完作业，看了部电影。 |
| or | 选择"或者" | You can take the bus, **or** you can walk with us. 你可以坐公交，或者跟我们一起走。 |
| but | 转折"但是" | The bag is expensive, **but** it is well made. 这个包贵，但做工好。 |

三者连接的东西必须"规格一致"：名词连名词，句子连句子。

- **I like tea and coffee.**（名词连名词）
- **I called him, but he didn't answer.**（句子连句子）

### 第五步：for example 和 such as，两种"例如"

| | for example | such as |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| 后面接什么 | 逗号 + 例子（可以是完整句，也可以是名词） | 直接跟名词，不跟完整句 |
| 逗号 | 例子前有逗号 | 前面不加逗号 |
| 例句 | I love fruit. **For example,** I eat an apple every day. | I love fruit **such as** apples and bananas. |

- **Big cities, such as Shanghai and Beijing, are often noisy.** 上海、北京这样的大城市常常很吵。
- **You should eat more vegetables. For example, you can add a salad to lunch.** 你该多吃蔬菜，比如午饭加一份沙拉。

### 第六步：in fact 和 actually，补充与修正

- **in fact**（事实上）：把前面的话再往前推一步，补充更强的信息。
- **actually**（其实）：修正别人（或自己原来）的想法。

- **Everyone thinks he is serious. In fact, he is very funny.** 大家觉得他严肃，事实上他很有趣。（补充真相）
- **I thought the class was boring, but actually it was fun.** 我原以为这课无聊，其实挺有意思。（修正预期）
- **It's not cheap. In fact, it's the most expensive one here.** 它不便宜，事实上是这里最贵的。（更进一步）

## 中文母语者常见错误

| ❌ 错误说法 | ✅ 正确说法 | 为什么 |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| **Because** I was hungry, **so** I ate two sandwiches. | I was hungry, **so** I ate two sandwiches. 或 **Because** I was hungry, I ate two sandwiches. | 中文"因为……所以……"成对出现，英语一句只能用一个：because 说因，so 说果。两个都用是头号中式错误。 |
| **Although** she was tired, **but** she kept working. | **Although** she was tired, she kept working. | "虽然"和"但是"在英语里同样只能选一个。although 出场，but 就退场。 |
| It was cold. **However** we still went out. | It was cold. **However,** we still went out. | however 后面必须加逗号，这是它的"出场费"，书面语里漏掉最扎眼。 |
| I like sweet food, **such as** I like chocolate. | I like sweet food, **such as** chocolate. | such as 后面直接跟名词（巧克力），不能跟一整句话。想跟句子用 for example：For example, I love chocolate. |
| We stayed home **because** the rain. | We stayed home **because of** the rain. | because 后面要跟句子（because it rained）；后面是名词时要用 because of。 |

## 练习

### A. 选词填空

从 because / so / although / though / but / however / and / or 中选择最合适的词填空（每个词只用一次）。

1. It was raining, ______ we still enjoyed our walk.
2. ______ the test was hard, everyone passed.
3. I was tired, ______ I went to bed early.
4. She didn't come to class ______ she was sick.
5. You can take the bus, ______ you can walk with us.
6. The hotel was expensive. ______, the food was great.
7. I finished my homework ______ watched a movie.
8. ______ he is only six, he can cook noodles.

### B. 改错

每句有一处错误，找出并改正。

9. Because I was hungry, so I ate two sandwiches.
10. Although she was tired, but she kept working.
11. It was cold. However we still went out.
12. I like sweet food, such as I like chocolate.

### C. 合并句子

用括号里的连词把两句话合并成一句。

13. The bus is cheap. The bus is slow. (but)
14. I study English every day. I want to work in another country. (because)
15. It was late. We kept talking. (although)
16. He didn't study. He failed the test. (so)

## 练习答案

<details><summary>A. 选词填空 答案</summary>

1. but（转折：下雨了，但我们散步仍很开心）
2. Although（虽然考试难，大家都通过了）
3. so（我累了，所以我早睡了）
4. because（她没来，因为她病了）
5. or（选择：坐公交，或者一起走路）
6. However（然而，注意后面有逗号）
7. and（顺接：写完作业并看了电影）
8. Though（虽然他只有六岁；用 Although 也对）

</details>

<details><summary>B. 改错 答案</summary>

9. 去掉 **so**（或去掉 Because）：I was hungry, so I ate two sandwiches. / Because I was hungry, I ate two sandwiches.
10. 去掉 **but**：Although she was tired, she kept working.
11. However 后加逗号：It was cold. **However,** we still went out.
12. such as 后直接跟名词：I like sweet food, **such as chocolate**.

</details>

<details><summary>C. 合并句子 答案</summary>

13. The bus is cheap, but it is slow.（也可写成 The bus is cheap but slow.）
14. I study English every day because I want to work in another country.
15. Although it was late, we kept talking.
16. He didn't study, so he failed the test.

</details>
