- [ ] 我能认出词义题（The word "X" most likely means...）和指代题（The word "it" refers to...）

- [ ] 我能在 8 分钟内完成一篇 200 词短文 + 5 道词义/指代混合题

## 核心内容

### 8.1 词义题：不考背诵，考语境

词义题的标准长相：

- The word "compound" (Line 3, Para. 2) most likely means ___
- The phrase "a level head" is closest in meaning to ___
- What does the word "witnesses" refer to in the last paragraph?

很多同学一见被考的词就心跳加速："这词我没背过！"先冷静。词义题的**本质是语境猜词**，和[第 3 课](./unit-03.md)的猜词五线索（定义、举例、因果、对比、常识）是同一套功夫，只是考场版。出题人挑的词经常满足两条之一：要么是生词（逼你用线索），要么是熟词（但考偏义，逼你别只认得它最常见的那张脸）。

### 8.2 词义题三原则

**原则一：答案常是熟词的偏义。**
book 不只是"书"，还是"预订"；fine 不只是"好"，还是"罚款"；stand 不只是"站"，还是"承受"。被考的词你越眼熟，越要问自己：**它在这个句子里干这份工作吗？**

**原则二：不选字面义。**
如果选项把这个词最常用、最字典的意思原样搬来，九成是坑。出题人知道不读语境的人会闭眼选它。字面义是"给不读上下文的人准备的答案"。

**原则三：代入验证。**
把四个选项轮流放回原句替换被考的词，读三样东西：句子通不通、和上下文方向合不合、和整段话题搭不搭。三个都过关的才是答案。

### 8.3 词义题两例精讲

**例 1（熟词偏义）**

> Raising prices during a shortage will only **compound** the problem.
> A. solve　B. worsen　C. notice　D. repeat

拆解：compound 你背过的意思是"化合物"（名词），字面代入完全不通，划掉一切"化合物"相关联想。看语境线索：**因果链**——短缺时涨价 → 结果会 ___ the problem。"only（只会）"暗示结果是负面的：涨价让问题更糟。B 项 worsen 代入："涨价只会使问题恶化"，通。A 项 solve 与"only + 负面语境"打架。答案 **B**：compound 作动词 = 使加重。

**例 2（习语中的熟词）**

> Firefighters need a **level** head in dangerous situations.
> A. 处于高位的　B. 冷静的　C. 快速的　D. 强壮的

拆解：level 常见义"水平、层面、平坦的"，单独看哪个都塞不进"___ head"。线索一：**场景**是危险现场；线索二：**常识**——消防员最需要的品质是什么？不是身高不是力气，是慌乱中不乱。B 项"冷静的"代入："危险中需要冷静的头脑"，通且合场景。答案 **B**。这就是"熟词的偏义"：level head = 冷静的头脑。

### 8.4 指代题：反向搜索法三步

指代题的标准长相：

- The word "it" in Line 4 refers to ___
- What does "they" / "this" / "such" refer to?

代词是回头的箭头，永远指向前文（极少数指后文，考试中可忽略）。反向搜索法三步：

1. **往前找最近的名词**（含名词短语），圈出所有候选。
2. **单复数对齐**：it / this / one 配单数；they / these / those / ones 配复数。对不上的直接划掉。
3. **代入验证**：把候选名词放回代词的位置重读整句，句义通顺、逻辑成立的那一个才是答案。

### 8.5 指代题的陷阱：最近的名词不一定是它

出题人深谙"距离产生诱惑"：他会故意把一个**语法上单复数一致、但句义不通**的名词放在代词旁边。看例：

> Some birds migrate to warmer regions. **They** return in spring when food becomes plentiful again.

反向搜索：they 前最近的复数名词是 **regions**（更暖的地区），单复数也一致（都是复数）。代入验证："地区在春天回来"——地区怎么会回来？回来的只能是**鸟**。所以 they = **some birds**。

再狠一点的：

> The committee rejected the plan. **This** surprised many members.

最近名词是 the plan（单数，this 单数，一致）。代入："这个计划让很多成员吃惊"？可原句强调的动作是**拒绝**这个决定本身让人吃惊。这里 this 指前面**整件事**（委员会否决了计划）。这类"指整句"的指代题，答案常长成 "The fact that the committee rejected the plan"。

结论：**单复数一致只是入场券，句义通顺才是裁判。**

### 8.6 指代题两例精讲

**例 1**

> Lisa tried three recipes before the cake finally turned out right. **It** was worth the effort.

三步：①候选名词往前找：the cake（单数）、three recipes（复数，与 it 不符，划掉）、the effort（单数）。②剩下 the cake 和 the effort。③代入："___ 值得这份努力"——"蛋糕值得这份努力"通顺；"努力值得这份努力"原地打转。答案：**the cake**。

**例 2**

> Schools are introducing coding classes, and many parents welcome **them**.

①候选：coding classes（复数）、schools（复数）、many parents（复数）。②三个都过单复数关。③代入验证："家长欢迎 ___"——欢迎"编程课"通顺自然；欢迎"学校"？语义勉强；欢迎"家长自己"？荒谬。答案：**coding classes**。看到没有：**复数名词可能有好几个**，单复数筛完还得靠句义拍板。

### 8.7 示范短文：一篇 165 词短文 + 3 题

先自己做（限时 4 分钟），再看解析。

> In many neighborhoods, small wooden boxes on posts have begun to appear in front yards. They look like birdhouses for books: glass doors, a shelf or two, and a simple sign that reads "Take a book, leave a book." These little free libraries belong to everyone and no one.
>
> The idea is simple. Anyone may open the door, borrow a book, and return it later—or keep it and leave another in its place. There are no cards, no fines, and no staff. The system runs entirely on trust.
>
> Some people doubted that such an honor system could work. Yet in most neighborhoods, the boxes remain full. Children's picture books disappear quickly, which delights the volunteers who maintain the boxes. Occasionally a box goes empty for a few days, but neighbors usually refill it before the weekend.
>
> Researchers who study community life say the boxes do more than share books. They give strangers a reason to stop, talk, and feel that the street belongs to them.

**Q1. The word "fines" most likely means ___**

- A. cards shown at a front desk
- B. small amounts of money paid as punishment
- C. official letters from a library
- D. prizes for frequent readers

解析：fine 的熟义"好的"是字面陷阱。语境：no cards, no **fines**, no staff——排比的全是真实图书馆会有的"规矩负担"：不办卡、不罚款、没管理员。B 项"罚金"代入："不罚款"，和"免卡、免人员"完美并列。答案 **B**。

**Q2. The word "delights" is closest in meaning to ___**

- A. worries
- B. pleases
- C. surprises
- D. slows down

解析：定位句：Children's picture books disappear quickly, which **delights** the volunteers。因果线索：童书被快速借走 → 志愿者 ___。志愿者维护书箱的目的就是让人拿书看，书被拿走他们高兴。B 项 pleases（使高兴）代入通顺。答案 **B**。

**Q3. In the last paragraph, "They" refers to ___**

- A. the researchers
- B. the boxes
- C. the books
- D. the strangers

解析：三步走。①候选：researchers（复数）、the boxes（复数）、books（复数）。②单复数全过。③代入："___ 给陌生人一个停下脚步、聊天的理由"——最近的是 books（陷阱：share **books** 紧挨着 They），但"书给陌生人聊天的理由"勉强；再往上找，本句主语是 the boxes（the boxes do more than share books），"书箱给陌生人驻足的理由"完全通顺，且与 "do **more** than share books" 的"不止于书"呼应——比书箱更主动的主体是箱子。答案 **B**。主语延续 + 句义双重验证。

## 常见误区

| ❌ 错误做法 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 词义题考词汇量，不认识这个单词就只能蒙 | 用第 3 课的猜词线索现场推，认识反而要警惕 | 生词题人人平等，线索全在句子里；熟词题才最阴险，它考你不知道的偏义 |
| 选那个"最像词典释义"的选项 | 优先怀疑字面义，用代入验证拍板 | 字面义是出题人给"不读语境的人"备的诱饵。语境义（本句里它干的活）才是考点 |
| 指代题找到代词前面最近的名词就选 | 单复数筛完必须代入整句验证句义 | 出题人专门在代词旁边放"单复数一致但语义不通"的陷阱名词 |
| 验证时只读代词那半句 | 连着前后两句读，看逻辑是否成立 | 指代是否正确，取决于整句甚至前后句的意思是否顺畅，半句话看不出破绽 |

## 实战练习

**Passage A（约 170 词）**

> No one knows exactly when humans first drank tea, but a Chinese story from almost five thousand years ago offers an answer. According to the story, Emperor Shennong was boiling water under a tree when some leaves fell into his pot. He tasted the result, found it refreshing, and tea was born.
>
> Historians doubt the story, yet they agree on one point: tea spread from China to the rest of the world. Buddhist monks drank it to stay awake during long nights of meditation. By the 1600s, Dutch traders were shipping it to Europe, where it quickly became fashionable among the wealthy.
>
> In Britain, tea drinking turned into a national habit. Afternoon tea, with small sandwiches and cakes, is said to have been invented by a hungry duchess in the 1840s. Workers in factories received short breaks that became known as "tea breaks," a custom that survives in many British offices today.
>
> Whatever its true origin, tea has quietly become the world's second most popular drink, after water.

**1. The word "refreshing" (Para. 1) most likely means ___.**

- A. making you feel less tired
- B. sweet enough to drink
- C. strange in flavor
- D. safe to taste

**2. The word "fashionable" (Para. 2) is closest in meaning to ___.**

- A. expensive to buy
- B. popular at a particular time
- C. difficult to obtain
- D. carefully produced

**3. The word "survives" (Para. 3) most likely means ___.**

- A. dies out slowly
- B. continues to exist
- C. moves to another country
- D. changes its form completely

**4. In "where it quickly became fashionable among the wealthy", "it" refers to ___.**

- A. Europe
- B. the Dutch trading business
- C. tea
- D. water

**5. In "He tasted the result", "the result" refers to ___.**

- A. the fallen leaves
- B. the water with the leaves in it
- C. the old story
- D. the pot under the tree

**Passage B（约 175 词）**

> Every commercial airplane carries two devices that few passengers ever think about. People call them "black boxes," although the color is wrong: they are painted bright orange so that search teams can spot them easily. One device records the pilots' voices and engine sounds; the other keeps a running record of the plane's speed, height, and direction. Together, they tell the story of every flight.
>
> The boxes are built to survive disasters. Each one must stand fire, deep-sea pressure, and shocks as violent as a high-speed car crash. Every box also carries a small beacon that starts sending underwater signals on impact. Thanks to this signal, searchers can find the box even at the bottom of an ocean.
>
> After an accident, investigators listen to the recordings and study the data. The boxes do not judge who was wrong, but without them, the truth about many accidents would remain a mystery forever. Flying has grown far safer partly because every crash teaches lessons that these silent orange witnesses remember perfectly.

**6. The word "stand" (Para. 2) most likely means ___.**

- A. remain upright
- B. endure without damage
- C. escape from
- D. measure accurately

**7. The phrase "on impact" (Para. 2) most likely means ___.**

- A. at the moment of hitting
- B. with a powerful effect on people
- C. after a long delay
- D. in a planned order

**8. The word "witnesses" (Para. 3) most likely refers to ___.**

- A. people who watch an event happen
- B. judges in a court of law
- C. the boxes that record evidence
- D. survivors of air accidents

**9. In "Together, they tell the story of every flight", "they" refers to ___.**

- A. the pilots and the engines
- B. the two devices
- C. speed and direction
- D. the passengers

**10. In "Each one must stand fire", "one" refers to ___.**

- A. each flight
- B. each box
- C. each passenger
- D. each search team

## 练习答案

**Passage A：茶的故事**

1. **A**。定位句：He tasted the result, found it **refreshing**, and tea was born。线索：茶的诞生靠这个"refreshing"的好体验。代入验证：A"让你不觉疲惫"——与下文 monks 喝茶 stay awake 呼应；B 甜（没提糖）；C 味道怪（怪味不会诞生一种饮品）；D 安全可尝（与"于是茶诞生了"的正面因果不接）。答案 A。

2. **B**。定位句：became **fashionable** among the wealthy（在富人中变得 fashionable）。代入：B"在某段时间流行"——通；A 昂贵（贵 ≠ 时髦，方向偏）；C 难获得（正在贸易运输，说明买得到）；D 精心生产（对象是人群不是商品）。答案 B。

3. **B**。定位句：a custom that **survives** in many British offices today。主语是 custom（习俗），today 是关键词：习俗"今天还在" = B"继续存在"。A 消亡（与 today 矛盾）；C 搬去别国；D 彻底改形。答案 B。

4. **C**。三步：候选单数名词往前找——shipping **it** to Europe 里 it 已经是 tea（ships tea）；where **it** quickly became fashionable 中最近的单数名词是 **Europe**（陷阱！）。代入验证："欧洲在富人中流行起来"？不通，流行的是饮品不是大洲；"茶流行起来"通顺。且整段主话题就是 tea。答案 C。

5. **B**。"the result"字面义"结果"，此处指"过程的产物"：树叶掉进沸水，尝的是**那锅泡了叶子的水**。A 只是叶子（主角是饮品）；C 故事；D 锅本身。代入："他尝了那锅水的味道"通。答案 B。

**Passage B：黑匣子**

6. **B**。stand 熟义"站立"是字面陷阱。定位句：Each one must **stand** fire, deep-sea pressure, and shocks（要 ___ 火焰、深海压力和撞击）。宾语全是灾难，能"站"在这些灾难面前 = B"承受而不损坏"。A 站立（宾语是火不是地板）；C 逃离（后文说要扛住）；D 测量。答案 B。

7. **A**。on impact 熟联想"影响"是陷阱（B 项 powerful effect on people 就是拿"impact=影响"钓你）。定位句：beacon starts sending underwater signals **on impact**。语境：事故坠落 → 落水瞬间启动发信。A"撞击的那一刻"代入完美。答案 A。

8. **C**。witness 熟义"目击者"（A 项字面陷阱）。但注意限定词：**these silent orange witnesses**——橙色的、无声的，人不会是橙色的，会的是黑匣子。此处是比喻：匣子"目击并记住"事故 = C"记录证据的匣子"。原则二"不选字面义"的经典案例。答案 C。

9. **B**。候选复数名词：the pilots and the engines、speed and direction（最近，陷阱）、the two devices。代入："___ 一起讲述每次飞行的故事"——"速度和方向讲故事"勉强但被动；"两台设备一起讲述"完整覆盖声音+数据，且与主语延续（One device... the other...）一致。答案 B。

10. **B**。定位句：The boxes are built to survive disasters. **Each one** must stand fire...。one 指代上文刚出场的复数名词 the boxes 中的"每一个"。代入："每个匣子必须扛住火焰"通。答案 B。

**归因自查**：词义题错了 → 是没找到线索句，还是被字面义钓走了？指代题错了 → 是单复数没对齐，还是没做代入验证？把错的那道题的"陷阱设计"用一句话写进笔记，你会开始享受看穿出题人的乐趣。
