## 本课目标

- [ ] 我能画出说明文、议论文、新闻、广告通知四类文体的骨架图
- [ ] 我知道"结构 = 出题地图"：每类文体的高频题型和出题位置
- [ ] 我会用首末段定律：首段常出主旨题，末段常出结论或态度题
- [ ] 我能用三步法在 30 秒内给文章定型并调整阅读策略
- [ ] 给我任意一篇 B1 难度文章的首段，我能说出它大概率是哪类文体

## 核心内容

### 10.1 为什么先定型：结构就是出题地图

考场文章不是随机的散文，是**按固定骨架搭出来的**。骨架只有四套，骨架决定了出题人在哪儿埋题：

- 说明文把"机制"写在第二三段 → 细节题埋在那儿
- 议论文把"立场"写在首段和末段 → 主旨题、态度题埋在那儿
- 新闻把"全部要点"压缩进首段 → 细节题埋在那儿
- 广告把"信息点"撒满全篇 → 匹配题让你回头扫

所以高手的读法不是"从头均匀读到尾"，而是先定型，再把火力对准那篇文体的高价值地段。**定型只要 30 秒，却能省下两分钟盲读。**

### 10.2 四类文体骨架图

```text
说明文（Expository）
现象 / 话题 → 是什么 → 怎么运作（机制） → 应用 / 延伸
· 语气客观，作者隐身
· 没有敌人，没有立场，只有解释
· 常见信号：this is because... / the process works like... / for example
```

```text
议论文（Argumentative）
亮出观点 → 论证一 → 论证二 → 让步与反驳 → 重申观点
· 有立场，有敌人（反方观点）
· 常见信号：should / in my opinion / first... second... / however / admittedly
```

```text
新闻报道（News Report）
导语（首段 = 5W：谁、何事、何时、何地、为何/如何）
→ 细节展开 → 背景补充 → 引语
· 倒金字塔：信息按重要性递减
· 首段读完，主旨和大半细节题已有答案
```

```text
广告 / 通知（Advertisement / Notice）
卖点罗列：时间 → 地点 → 价格 → 亮点清单 → 行动召唤（怎么报名）
· 没有论证，没有态度，只有信息点
· 先看题干，再回来扫数字、日期、黑体词
```

### 10.3 结构 = 出题地图：题型分布规律表

| 文体 | 高频题型 | 出题重灾区 | 阅读策略 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 说明文 | 细节题、词义题、例证题 | 机制段（通常第二、三段） | 首段快读定性，机制段放慢精读 |
| 议论文 | 主旨题、态度题、推理题 | 首段（观点）+ 末段（重申） | 首末段各读两遍，论证段扫读 |
| 新闻报道 | 细节题、主旨题 | **首段（导语）** | 精读首段，其余扫读找对应 |
| 广告通知 | 细节匹配题、排除题 | 时间、价格、地点、条件 | 先读题干再扫关键词，别通读 |

**首末段定律**（全文体通用）：首段常出主旨题——作者开篇就要交代"这篇文章讲什么"；末段常出结论题或态度题——作者收尾要表态。新闻是唯一例外：它的"结论"就是首段导语，所以更要精读首段。

### 10.4 快速定型三步（30 秒）

1. **扫首段**：有没有亮观点的句子（should / must / in my opinion）？有 → 议论文倾向；只有事实交代 → 继续判断。
2. **看结构信号词**：because / process / for example 成串 → 说明文；first / second / however / 让步反驳 → 议论文；5W 齐全一句话交代大事 + 引语 → 新闻；日期价格感叹号清单 → 广告通知。
3. **定文体，调策略**：对照 10.3 的表格，立刻决定哪里精读、哪里扫读、哪里先跳过。

三步的产出是一句话，比如："议论文，态度题必考，首末段读两遍。"说出这句话，才许开始做题。

### 10.5 四类文体迷你样本（每篇约 120 词 + 结构点评）

**样本一：说明文**

> Light from the sun looks white, but it is really a mixture of colors. Each color travels as a wave, and the waves have different lengths. Red light has the longest waves; blue light has some of the shortest.
>
> When sunlight enters the atmosphere, it bumps into countless tiny gas molecules. Long waves, like red, slide past these molecules easily. Short waves, like blue, hit them and scatter in every direction. The scattered blue light reaches our eyes from all parts of the sky, and so the sky above us looks blue.
>
> The same effect also explains sunsets. When the sun sits low, its light travels a longer path through the air, the blue is scattered away entirely, and the reds and oranges are what remain.

结构点评：现象（天为什么蓝）→ 机制（散射原理）→ 应用（顺手解释日落），全程无观点无人称立场，教科书级说明文。出题人会盯住第二段机制段出细节题。

**样本二：议论文**

> Every evening, millions of students copy words they have already learned and finish exercises they could do in their sleep. This is not learning; this is paperwork. Schools should cut repetitive homework sharply and replace it with reading, discussion, and rest.
>
> First, research repeatedly shows that after about an hour, extra homework adds almost nothing to learning. Second, hours of copying kill the curiosity that education is supposed to protect. A tired student who hates books learns less than a rested one who picks them up by choice.
>
> Some parents fear that less homework means less learning. In fact, the opposite often proves true: time spent on real reading builds skills that no worksheet can. Schools exist to light fires, not to fill forms.

结构点评：观点（砍掉重复作业）→ 论证一（研究）→ 论证二（好奇心）→ 让步反驳（家长担心？恰恰相反）→ 金句重申（点火不是填表）。主旨题答案在第一段末句，态度题答案在末段。

**样本三：新闻报道**

> The Old Mill Bridge reopened to traffic on Tuesday morning, six weeks earlier than planned, giving 12,000 daily commuters back their shortest route across the river.
>
> The 60-year-old bridge was closed in March after inspectors found cracks in two support beams. The repair, which cost 3.4 million dollars, replaced the damaged beams and added a new bicycle lane.
>
> City engineer Maria Chen said the early finish resulted from good weather and a second construction crew that worked weekends. "We promised September, and we meant it," she told reporters at the site.
>
> Mayor James Wood, who visited the bridge on Monday, thanked residents for their patience during the detour. A small opening ceremony is planned for Saturday at 10 a.m., with free boat rides under the bridge.

结构点评：首段一句话装下何事（复通）、何时（周二）、何果（提前六周、1.2 万通勤者受益）——倒金字塔塔尖；后面全是对首段的展开（背景：为何封闭；细节：花钱、引语、后续活动）。主旨题答案就在第一句。

**样本四：广告通知**

> **Summer Science Camp — Register Now!**
>
> Dates: July 10–28 (weekdays only)  |  Place: Northfield University Science Building
> Ages: 10–14  |  Fee: $180 (lunch included; 10% off before June 1)
>
> Join three weeks of hands-on science:
>
> - Build and launch your own water rocket
> - Cook food with sunlight in a solar oven you design
> - Sleep over at the campus observatory and view Saturn through a real telescope
>
> No experience needed—only curiosity. Our teachers are university students who love science and love teaching.
>
> Places are limited to 40 campers and fill quickly every year. Visit the front office or call 555-0192 to register. See you in July!

结构点评：卖点清单（日期、地点、价格、折扣条件、三大亮点、名额、报名方式）+ 行动召唤结尾。考试只问信息点（几岁能去？6 月 1 日前多少钱？），先看题干再回来扫数字，永远别通读广告。

## 常见误区

| ❌ 错误做法 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 拿到文章从头逐字读到尾，不分文体 | 先花 30 秒定型，再按文体分配注意力 | 四类文体的出题位置完全不同；均匀用力等于把宝贵时间花在不出题的段落上 |
| 新闻报道认认真真读完每一段 | 精读首段导语，其余扫读 | 新闻是倒金字塔：首段 5W 浓缩了主旨和大半细节题的答案，后面全是展开和引语 |
| 对广告通知也找"作者态度" | 广告只有信息点，考的是匹配 | 广告的目的是让你行动（报名、参加），没有论证也没有态度；时间、价格、地点、条件才是考点 |
| 首段没读懂也硬着头皮往下读 | 首段值得回头再读一遍 | 首末段定律：出题密度最高的是首段（主旨）和末段（结论/态度），首段读丢，后面全在雾里开车 |

## 实战练习

**Part A：定型快练（读首段，判断文体）**

**1.** Honey never spoils. Jars found in ancient tombs, some three thousand years old, still contain honey that is safe to eat. The secret lies in the honey's chemistry: very little water, high acidity, and natural chemicals that kill bacteria.

- A. 说明文
- B. 议论文
- C. 新闻报道
- D. 广告通知

**2.** School uniforms should stay. However old-fashioned they look, they remove a daily competition that many students silently lose. When everyone wears the same clothes, attention moves from brands to books, and mornings get shorter for parents too.

- A. 说明文
- B. 议论文
- C. 新闻报道
- D. 广告通知

**3.** A fire broke out at the Riverside Market on Sunday night, destroying six shops before firefighters brought it under control. No one was injured, and the market is expected to reopen within a month, the city said.

- A. 说明文
- B. 议论文
- C. 新闻报道
- D. 广告通知

**4.** Weekend English Corner — Free! Every Saturday, 3–5 p.m., City Library, Room 204. Talk with native speakers, play word games, and enjoy free tea. All levels welcome. No sign-up needed—just bring yourself (and maybe a friend)!

- A. 说明文
- B. 议论文
- C. 新闻报道
- D. 广告通知

**5.** Cities should plant more trees, and not just because they are pretty. Trees cool streets by several degrees in summer, absorb floodwater after storms, and even slow traffic, because drivers unconsciously ease off the pedal on green streets.

- A. 说明文
- B. 议论文
- C. 新闻报道
- D. 广告通知

**Part B：完整说明文实战（先 30 秒定型，再限时 5 分钟做题）**

> **Spicy Is a Feeling, Not a Taste**
>
> Many people describe chili peppers as "hot," but strictly speaking, spiciness is not a taste. Your tongue recognizes five basic tastes: sweet, sour, salty, bitter, and umami. Spiciness does not belong to this list, because it is not detected by the tongue's taste buds at all.
>
> The real hero, or villain, of the chili is a chemical called capsaicin. When capsaicin touches the mouth, it binds to special receptors whose actual job is to sense heat and physical danger. These receptors normally warn the brain when food is too hot in temperature or when the skin is being rubbed or cut. Capsaicin sets off the same alarm without any real heat or injury. In other words, when you eat a chili, your brain is not tasting something; it is feeling pain.
>
> This explains several familiar experiences. Milk relieves the burning better than water because capsaicin dissolves in fat, and milk contains fat, while water merely spreads the chemical around. It also explains why regular chili eaters suffer less: repeated alarms dull the receptors, in the same way that constant noise dulls hearing for a moment.
>
> So the "heat" of a chili lives not in the pepper but in the alarm system of the person eating it.

**6. Which of the following best states the main idea of the passage?**

- A. The tongue can only recognize five basic tastes.
- B. Spiciness is a pain signal from heat receptors, not a true taste.
- C. Milk is the healthiest drink to have with spicy food.
- D. Regular chili eaters enjoy food more than other people.

**7. What do the special receptors in the mouth normally do?**

- A. They detect the five basic tastes.
- B. They warn the brain of heat and physical injury.
- C. They dissolve fat and kill bacteria.
- D. They produce capsaicin in the chili.

**8. The phrase "binds to" (Para. 2) most likely means ___.**

- A. attaches to
- B. escapes from
- C. destroys
- D. confuses

**9. What can we infer about plain water?**

- A. It removes capsaicin from the mouth quickly.
- B. It cannot dissolve capsaicin and may spread it around.
- C. It makes the mouth hotter than before.
- D. It dulls the receptors after repeated use.

**10. What can we learn about regular chili eaters?**

- A. They have fewer taste buds than other people.
- B. Their receptors respond less after repeated alarms.
- C. They drink more milk than other people.
- D. They can no longer feel real heat at all.

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**Part A：定型快练**

1. **A 说明文**。现象（蜂蜜三千年不腐）+ 机制预告（The secret lies in...：化学原理）。无立场、无行动召唤、无 5W 时事——标准说明文开头。
2. **B 议论文**。should stay 直接亮观点，正文围绕"为什么校服该留"展开论证。should / must 开篇 = 议论文第一信号。
3. **C 新闻报道**。一句话装下 5W：何事（火灾烧毁六店铺）、何时（周日晚）、何果（无人伤亡、一月内复业）、消息源（the city said）。倒金字塔塔尖。
4. **D 广告通知**。时间地点、"Free!"感叹、卖点清单（native speakers / word games / free tea）、"No sign-up needed"条件说明。信息点罗列 + 招人。
5. **B 议论文**。Cities **should** plant more trees 立场 + "not just because..."预告论证。首段已在摆证据（降温、吸水、减速），不是介绍机制。

**Part B：辣味说明文**

定型检查：标题就是话题（辣不是味觉），首段无立场，第二段 The real hero... is... 讲机制——说明文。按地图：主旨看首段，细节盯机制段（第二、三段）。

6. **B**。主旨题（首末段定律：首段末句 + 末段总结句）。首段说辣不是味觉，第二段说它是受体的疼痛警报，末句总结 the "heat"... lives... in the alarm system。B 是全文合体。A 只是第一段的铺垫事实；C、D 是第三段的枝节例证。

7. **B**。细节题，定位机制段：receptors whose actual job is to **sense heat and physical danger** + normally **warn the brain** when food is too hot... B 是近义改写。A 张冠李戴（五种味觉靠 taste buds，不是 receptors）；C 是牛奶干的事（dissolves fat 的是奶）；D 颠倒（capsaicin 在辣椒里，不由受体生产）。

8. **A**。词义题（[第 8 课](./unit-08.md)功夫）。capsaicin 触到口腔 → ___ 受体 → 触发警报。代入 A"附着于"：化学物质附上受体、按响门铃，通；B 逃离（逃离了还怎么报警）；C 摧毁（摧毁了警报器，后文无法成立）；D 迷惑。答案 A。

9. **B**。推理题（[第 7 课](./unit-07.md)功夫）。定位句：water **merely spreads** the chemical around。一步推断：水不能溶解辣椒素，只会把它抹开——这正是"喝水越喝越辣"的原因。A 相反；C 无据；D 张冠李戴（dull receptors 的是 repeated alarms）。

10. **B**。细节题。定位句：repeated alarms **dull the receptors**（反复报警让受体变钝）。B 项 "respond less after repeated alarms" 是近义改写。A 无据（没比较味蕾数量）；C 无据；D 极端词 no longer... at all——原文只说钝了一刻（dull for a moment 的类比），没说永远失灵。

**归因自查**：Part A 错了 → 回 10.2 骨架图重背信号词；Part B 错了 → 你是没定型就开读，还是定型了却没按地图走？把"机制段"三个字写在错题旁边提醒自己。
