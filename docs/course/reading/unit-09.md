## 本课目标

- [ ] 我能认出态度题：题干出现 attitude / tone，四个选项全是态度形容词
- [ ] 我能背出三大类信号词各 5 个以上：正面、负面、中性
- [ ] 我知道文中找态度证据的三处：修饰词的选择、转折词之后、观点动词的隐含色彩
- [ ] 遇到没有态度词的说明文，我敢直接选 objective，不慌不硬找
- [ ] 面对双面讨论文，我会直奔末段找作者的最后立场

## 核心内容

### 9.1 题型识别：选项暴露了一切

态度题两副面孔：

- What is the author's attitude toward remote work?
- The tone of the passage is ___

认它不用看题干，看选项就行：**四个选项清一色是态度形容词**——supportive、critical、neutral、doubtful……出题人把"这是什么题型"直接写在了选项里。

态度题只有一个黄金原则：**不凭感觉，只找证据。**每个判断都要能在原文圈出词。作者从不说"我很生气"，他只会在形容词、副词、动词的选择里泄露情绪。

### 9.2 情感信号词三大类（必背大表）

**正面信号（作者点赞）**

| 信号词 | 中文 | 信号词 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| supportive | 支持的 | enthusiastic | 热情的 |
| positive | 积极肯定的 | sympathetic | 赞同的、有共鸣的 |
| approving | 赞许的 | appreciative | 欣赏的 |
| favorable | 有利的、赞同的 | admiring | 钦佩的 |
| optimistic | 乐观的 | confident | 有信心的 |

**负面信号（作者差评）**

| 信号词 | 中文 | 信号词 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| critical | 批评的 | pessimistic | 悲观的 |
| negative | 否定的 | concerned | 担忧的 |
| disapproving | 不赞许的 | worried | 忧虑的 |
| doubtful | 怀疑的 | ironic | 讽刺的 |
| skeptical | 挑剔怀疑的 | contemptuous | 鄙夷的 |

**中性信号（作者隐身）**

| 信号词 | 中文 | 信号词 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| neutral | 中立的 | detached | 超然旁观的 |
| objective | 客观的 | impartial | 不偏不倚的 |
| indifferent | 冷漠的 | factual | 只陈述事实的 |
| uninterested | 不感兴趣的 | informative | 纯提供信息的 |
| ambivalent | 矛盾摇摆的 | descriptive | 纯描述性的 |

易混预警：indifferent 是"冷漠不关心"（作者懒得评价），neutral 是"中立不站队"（作者权衡过但不偏袒），objective 是"客观无感情"（说明文默认状态）。三者中文都像"没态度"，方向完全不同。

### 9.3 文中找证据的三处

**第一处：形容词、副词的选择。**
同一件事，作者用哪个修饰词，立场就露了：

- **Fortunately**, the rain stopped.（幸好——作者松了口气，正面）
- **Unfortunately**, the rain stopped.（不巧——作者盼着下雨，负面）
- a **bold** plan（大胆的——褒）
- a **reckless** plan（鲁莽的——贬）

事实不变，形容词变了，态度就变了。做题时把原文的形容词副词全圈出来，问一句：**换成中性的词行不行？**不行，说明这里有立场。

**第二处：转折词后的立场。**
英语写作的铁律是"让步—转折"：although / while / despite 先退一步，**but / yet / however 后面才是真心话**。

> The app is admittedly cheap. **But** price is the only thing it has to offer.

前半句夸便宜，but 一出，前功尽弃。转折词前的夸奖都是铺垫，转折词后的内容才是作者本尊。

**第三处：观点动词的隐含色彩。**
作者转述别人观点时用的动词，自带态度滤镜：

| 动词 | 表面动作 | 隐含色彩 |
|---|---|---|
| argue | 主张 | 认真提出观点，中性偏正式 |
| claim | 声称 | 悄悄提示"未必是真的" |
| admit | 承认 | 不情愿地认下对自己不利的事 |
| concede | 让步 | 勉强承认对方有点道理 |
| insist | 坚称 | 带抵触情绪的坚持 |
| boast | 夸口 | 贬义，吹牛 |

比较：The company **claims** its product is safe.（作者：呵呵，我可不背书）vs. The company **reports** its product is safe.（作者：只转述，不下场）。

### 9.4 没有态度词怎么办：说明文 = objective

讲蝴蝶如何迁徙、面包怎么发酵、黑匣子如何制造的文章——通篇没有 fortunate，没有 but 后的重锤，没有 claim 的阴阳怪气。这不是作者忘了表态，是**说明文作者本来就该隐身**。此时态度题答案直接选 **objective / neutral / informative** 一族，别慌，也别硬从石头里挤出态度来。

一条判断捷径：**文章在"介绍东西"还是"说服你"？**介绍 = 客观；说服 = 必有立场。

### 9.5 双面讨论文：末段定态度

最狡猾的文体是双面讨论文：正面一段、反面一段，看起来公平得像辩论赛。这种文**态度题的答案几乎总在末段**，因为写作惯例是：铺垫完两方观点，作者最后总要自己说句话（"the truth is..." "perhaps the wisest course is..."）。末段三个标志：

1. 出现总结性词：in short / the truth / as usual / the honest answer
2. 语气降温，给出折中或温和站队：may / seems / depends on
3. 不再转述他人，开始说作者自己的判断

操作口诀：**双面文，跳末段，找作者那句话。**

### 9.6 示范短文两篇 + 态度题全流程

**篇 1：立场鲜明的批评文（约 175 词）**

> City officials love bike-sharing programs, and the numbers explain why: in our city, shared bikes now carry more than two million rides a month. Unfortunately, the love story has an ugly chapter. Sidewalks near subway stations have turned into parking lots of tangled bicycles, and blind residents say the fallen bikes have made their usual paths dangerous.
>
> To be fair, the companies behind the bikes have hired teams to move them, and the situation has improved slightly. But voluntary cleanup is not a plan; it is a promise made after each complaint. What cities need are firm rules: fixed parking areas, fines that bite, and limits on how many bikes each company may drop on the streets.
>
> Some argue that strict rules will slow a green revolution. This is nonsense. A revolution that blocks wheelchairs is not green, and a service that annoys the neighbors will eventually lose them. Bike-sharing deserves a future, but only the kind that knows where to park.

**Q. What is the author's attitude toward the current management of bike-sharing?**

- A. supportive
- B. critical
- C. indifferent
- D. uncertain

全流程还原：

| 步骤 | 原文证据 | 判定 |
|---|---|---|
| 圈修饰词 | **Unfortunately**, the love story has an **ugly** chapter | 负面开场 |
| 圈转折 | **But** voluntary cleanup is **not a plan**; it is a promise made after each complaint | 让步后反手一锤，负面加重 |
| 圈态度句 | This is **nonsense**（一派胡言） | 直接差评，实锤 |
| 结论 | 三处负面证据，零处正面证据（"deserves a future"也只是"值得有规矩的未来"） | 答案 **B critical** |

**篇 2：双面讨论文（约 190 词）**

> Should young children use tablets? Ask two experts and you may get three opinions.
>
> Supporters point to real benefits. Good educational apps adapt to each child's level, offering practice that a busy parent cannot always provide. For children in remote areas, tablets can open doors to libraries, museums, and language partners that no bus could ever reach. Some studies also report improved early reading skills among children who used well-designed apps with adults nearby.
>
> Critics worry about the costs. They note that time on a screen is time not spent climbing, talking, or arguing with a brother—activities that build bodies and social skills in ways no app can copy. Eye doctors add that early heavy screen use is linked to rising rates of short-sightedness.
>
> Recent research offers a boring but honest answer: the device itself matters far less than the adults around it. A tablet with a parent asking questions can teach; the same tablet used as an electronic babysitter at dinner probably harms. Until more long-term studies arrive, the wisest course seems to be moderation—limited time, chosen content, and a grown-up in the chair.

**Q. The author's attitude toward young children using tablets is ___.**

- A. strongly opposed
- B. fully supportive
- C. cautious and balanced
- D. uninterested

全流程还原：正文两段各执一词（supporters / critics），证据互相抵消，**跳末段**。末段钥匙词：the device itself matters far less than the adults around it（不看机器看大人）+ the **wisest** course seems to be **moderation**（最明智的做法是适度）。作者不站队、不开绿灯也不拉闸，给出"适度 + 成人陪伴"的谨慎折中。答案 **C cautious and balanced**。注意 D 无据：作者花一整段给建议，怎么会 uninterested？

## 常见误区

| ❌ 错误做法 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 凭整体印象选态度："读下来感觉作者人还挺好的" | 每个态度判断圈出至少两处原文信号词 | 态度题选项个个自带感情滤镜，感觉最容易骗人；信号词是白纸黑字的证据，可复查可辩论 |
| 文章正反两面都提了，就选 neutral / objective | 双面讨论文直奔末段，看作者最后站哪边 | 列两面是论证手法，不是态度；作者常在结尾温和站队或给出折中——那才是态度 |
| 把 but 前面的夸奖当作者立场 | 转折词之前全是铺垫，之后才是真心话 | 英语议论文的"让步—转折"结构：先承认对方优点，再用 but 亮出真观点 |
| 说明文找不到态度词，反复纠结不敢选 | 说明文作者隐身，直接选 objective 一族 | 没有态度词本身就是证据：作者在介绍，不在说服 |

## 实战练习

**Part A：信号词快判（判断每句中作者的态度：正面 / 负面 / 中性）**

**1.** Fortunately, the fire was discovered before it spread to the warehouse.

**2.** The plan, however, turned out to be yet another promise that nobody kept.

**3.** The report describes the four stages of the experiment in the order they occurred.

**4.** Admittedly, the app works, but calling it "revolutionary" is a joke.

**5.** To our relief, the missing hikers were found on the third morning.

**Part B：短文实战**

**Passage C（约 175 词）**

> Supermarkets love self-checkout machines, and it is easy to see why: one employee can now watch six tills instead of operating one. Shoppers, we are told, enjoy the speed and independence.
>
> Do we? A recent survey found that most customers still prefer a human cashier when given the choice. The machines misread barcodes, refuse slightly bent coins, and freeze at the first puff of an unplanned bag. Then the famous words flash on the screen: "Unexpected item in the bagging area." Anyone who has stood waiting, arms full, while a light blinks for help knows exactly how "independent" this experience feels.
>
> The stores respond that customers will adjust. Perhaps. But queues at the human tills keep growing longer on Saturday afternoons, which suggests that customers have adjusted already—to walking away from the machines.
>
> Self-checkout is not a service. It is a transfer of unpaid work from employees to customers, dressed up as convenience.

**6. What is the author's attitude toward self-checkout machines?**

- A. supportive
- B. neutral
- C. critical
- D. uncertain

**7. Why does the author put "independent" in quotation marks?**

- A. To quote exactly what shoppers say.
- B. To question whether the word is true.
- C. To praise the machines for freeing shoppers.
- D. To introduce a technical term.

**8. What can we infer from the last paragraph?**

- A. Self-checkout gives some of the work to customers without pay.
- B. Stores will soon remove all the machines.
- C. Employees receive higher pay under the new system.
- D. Customers get discounts for using the machines.

**Passage D（约 185 词）**

> When companies sent employees home to work in 2020, many predicted the end of the office. Four years on, the picture is more complicated.
>
> Fans of remote work point to productivity studies, several of which report equal or better output among home workers. They also mention the two hours of commuting saved each day, money that stays in employees' pockets, and jobs that suddenly open up to parents and carers who cannot easily travel.
>
> The other side is equally loud. Managers describe virtual teams that struggle to share ideas, young employees who miss the quiet training of watching seniors work, and friendships at work that never quite form over video calls. Some companies also report that it became harder to turn work off at night when the desk sits two meters from the dinner table.
>
> The truth, as usual, refuses to take sides. Remote work is neither a miracle nor a trap; it is a tool whose value depends on the job, the person, and the rules around it. Hybrid schedules, done thoughtfully, may simply be the honest answer.

**9. What is the author's attitude toward remote work?**

- A. Enthusiastic promotion.
- B. Total rejection.
- C. Balanced acceptance.
- D. Amused indifference.

**10. What can be inferred about the author's view on hybrid schedules?**

- A. They may be a realistic compromise.
- B. They will replace all offices within a year.
- C. They mainly benefit managers.
- D. They reduce employees' pay.

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**Part A：信号词快判**

1. **正面**。Fortunately（幸好）一锤定音：火没烧到仓库是好事，作者松了口气。
2. **负面**。however 转折 + yet another promise that nobody kept（又一个没人兑现的承诺）："yet another"的厌倦感 + 定语从句补刀。
3. **中性**。describes... in the order they occurred：只陈述实验步骤，无修饰立场，纯描述。这类句子出现在说明文里，作者隐身。
4. **负面**。Admittedly 让步承认 app works，but 转折后 calling it "revolutionary" is a joke（说它"革命性"是个笑话）：虚假引号 + joke，双重嘲讽。
5. **正面**。To our relief（让我们松了口气）：情绪词直接报立场，找到失联登山者是好消息。

**Passage C：自助结账机**

6. **C critical**。证据链三连：①第二段连拍 misread / refuse / freeze（三连差评动词）；②"independent"打虚假引号（见第 7 题）；③末段 Self-checkout is **not a service**. It is a transfer of unpaid work... **dressed up as convenience**（把无偿劳动包装成便利）——揭穿式总结句。全文无一处正面证据（开头 supermarkets love 是转述超市立场，"we are told"暗含"我可没说"）。B 不可选：作者态度鲜明，绝不中立。

7. **B**。引号的两用法：真引用 vs 虚假引用。此处的"independent"不是谁的原话，是作者借超市宣传语反讽：机器号称给你"独立"，实际让你举着东西等灯闪。引号 = 我不认这个词。A 错在无出处；C 与文意相反；D 不是术语。

8. **A**。定位末段：It is a **transfer of unpaid work** from employees to customers（把无酬劳动从员工转给顾客）。A 项 "gives some of the work to customers without pay" 是近义改写，一步推断（[第 7 课](./unit-07.md)功夫）。B 二步脑补（批判 ≠ 预言拆除）；C、D 无据。

**Passage D：远程办公**

9. **C Balanced acceptance**。正文两方证据对冲（fans / the other side），按 9.5 直奔末段：The truth **refuses to take sides** + it is a **tool whose value depends on** the job, the person, and the rules。作者不吹不黑，承认工具价值但强调条件 = 平衡接受。A、B 与"不站队"矛盾；D 无据（作者认真分析了一整篇，不是觉得好笑）。

10. **A**。定位末句：Hybrid schedules, done thoughtfully, **may simply be the honest answer**（用心安排的混合办公，可能就是诚实的答案）。may be a realistic compromise = 近义改写，一步推断。B 极端词 will replace all + 时间脑补；C 无据（文中 managers 恰是抱怨方）；D 无据。

**归因自查**：态度题错选 neutral / objective 的同学，回去找证据链——你是不是漏了末段？错选极端态度（strongly opposed / enthusiastic）的同学，检查你圈到的证据够不够两处：单凭一个形容词定案，证据不足。
