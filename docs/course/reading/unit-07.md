## 核心内容

### 7.1 题型识别：这些词一出现，就是推理题

推理题的题干非常好认，看到下面这些信号词，立刻切换到"推理模式"：

| 题干信号词 | 常见句式 | 中文意思 |
|---|---|---|
| infer | What can we infer from Paragraph 2? | 从第二段能推断出什么 |
| imply | The author implies that ___ | 作者暗示…… |
| suggest | The last sentence suggests that ___ | 最后一句暗示…… |
| learn from | What can we learn from the passage? | 从文中能得知什么 |
| conclude | It can be concluded that ___ | 可以得出结论…… |

记住：**这些词一出现，答案就不会直接印在原文里**。但也别慌，它离原文非常近，近到只隔一层"换说法"的距离。

### 7.2 黄金律：推理题不需要"推"

这是本课最重要的一句话，请读三遍：

> **推理题的答案 = 原文某句话的近义改写，不是你的知识。**

很多同学一看 "infer" 就开始开脑洞："文章说蜜蜂多了，那蜜蜂多了是不是说明环境好了？环境好了是不是说明这个学校是环保名校？……"停！你已经在编故事了。

出题人造推理题的方式很简单：他从原文挑一句话，把它**换个说法**写成正确答案。换法无非三件套：

1. **同义词替换**：原文 didn't sleep well → 答案 was tired
2. **句式变换**：原文主动句 → 答案被动句；原文否定 → 答案双重否定
3. **正话反说**：原文 flowers never had a chance to bloom（花从没机会开）→ 答案 the mowing prevented flowering（割草阻止了开花）

所以推理题的动作不是"推"，而是"翻译"：把原文那句翻译成另一种说法。你脑子里那个"我读过很多相关文章"的库，全程不参与。**过度推断是推理题的头号坑**，掉坑的人比读不懂的人还多。

### 7.3 推断的两级：一步安全，二步危险

把推断想成走路：从原文出发，走一步能到的，是答案；要走两步的，是陷阱。

**一步推断（✅ 答案的住所）**：原文 A → 答案是 A 的直接含义。

原文：Tom didn't sleep well; his eyes were red.
- ✅ 一步推断：Tom was probably tired.（"没睡好"的直接含义，换说法而已）

**二步推断（❌ 陷阱的住所）**：原文 A → 中间结论 B → 答案 C。

原文还是那句 Tom didn't sleep well。
- ❌ 二步推断：Tom will fail the exam.（A 没睡好 → B 状态差 → C 考试挂科，走了两步）
- ❌ 二步推断：Tom played video games all night.（A → B 他熬夜了 → C 熬夜在打游戏，全是脑补）

判断口诀：**从原文到选项，如果需要"所以……所以……"两个箭头，直接划掉。**

### 7.4 过度推断的三大表现

掉进二步推断坑的人，通常有这三种姿势。做题时拿这张清单扫描每个选项：

**表现一：用自己的常识补**
文章说"A 城市去年冬天特别冷"，选项说" A 城市的人冬天都穿羽绒服"。常识上没毛病，但原文一个字没提穿着。你的生活经验再合理，也不是作者写的。

**表现二：用极端词 must / only / never / all**
原文通常是留有余地的陈述（often, many, probably, tend to）。选项一旦出现 must、only、never、all、completely 这类绝对化用词，九成是把"有时"放大成了"永远"。例外只有一种：原文自己就这么绝对。

**表现三：范围放大或缩小**
原文说 some students（部分学生）→ 选项说 students in general（学生普遍）
原文说 in recent years（近几年）→ 选项说 since 1990（从 1990 年起）
数字、人群、时间范围、频率，这四个维度最容易被偷偷拉伸。看到选项先问：**范围和原文一样大吗？**

### 7.5 推理题和细节题的唯一区别

| | 细节题 | 推理题 |
|---|---|---|
| 定位方式 | 题干关键词回原文找 | 同样回原文找 |
| 答案形态 | 原文原句的近义改写 | 原文原句的近义改写 |
| 区别 | 答案**写在**原文里 | 答案**不直接写**在原文里 |

看出门道了吗？两类题的做法几乎一样：定位 → 找到那句话 → 比对选项。唯一区别是推理题的正确答案需要你"换个说法"才能和原文对上号。

但有一条铁的底线：**正确答案永远在原文"一句话射程内"**。如果一个选项离开原文任何一句话都能独立成立，它多半是靠常识或脑补站着的，划掉。

### 7.6 整篇示范：200 词短文 + 2 道推理题逐项分析

先自己限时 5 分钟做一遍，再看分析。

**示范短文（约 200 词）**

> Last spring, Riverside School did something unusual: it stopped cutting most of its grass. Instead of a smooth green lawn, the field behind the science building was allowed to grow wild. Within months, tall grass and wildflowers covered the area, and students began to notice changes they had not expected.
>
> The most obvious visitors were insects. Bees and butterflies appeared in large numbers, attracted by the flowers that had never had a chance to bloom under the weekly mowing schedule. Teachers reported that birds followed, feeding on the seeds and the insects.
>
> Then came the surprising part. The school had planned the project to save money on maintenance. Cutting a lawn every week costs a surprising amount, and the school's budget had been tight for years. But the wild field turned out to do more than save money. Science classes now use it as an outdoor laboratory, and a recent survey showed that students felt calmer during breaks when they sat near the wild plants.
>
> Not everyone is happy. Two students complained in the school newspaper that the field "looks messy," and one parent called it "a bad example." The head teacher, however, has no plans to bring back the mowers. "Nature," she said last week, "does not need to be tidy."

**Q1. What can we infer about the weekly mowing schedule before last spring?**

- A. It was introduced to attract birds.
- B. It prevented wildflowers from blooming.
- C. It was popular among students.
- D. It cost almost nothing.

逐项分析（标注每个选项的推断级别）：

| 选项 | 判定 | 推断级别分析 |
|---|---|---|
| A | ❌ 无据 | 原文说割草是常规维护，从没说它是为了吸引鸟。属于"目的脑补" |
| B | ✅ 答案 | 定位句：flowers that **had never had a chance to bloom** under the weekly mowing schedule。改写：mowing **prevented** flowering。教科书级一步推断：prevent = not give a chance |
| C | ❌ 无据 | 学生是否喜欢旧草坪，原文只字未提。常识补位 |
| D | ❌ 相反 | 原文说 Cutting a lawn every week **costs a surprising amount**（花费惊人），D 说"几乎不花钱"，方向就反了 |

**Q2. What can be learned from the last paragraph?**

- A. The head teacher will soon change her mind.
- B. All parents are against the wild field.
- C. The wild field remains despite the disagreement.
- D. The school newspaper decides school policy.

逐项分析：

| 选项 | 判定 | 推断级别分析 |
|---|---|---|
| A | ❌ 无据 | 原文说 **no plans** to bring back the mowers，"很快改变主意"是脑补未来 |
| B | ❌ 范围放大 | 原文只说 **one parent**（一位家长）反对，B 用 All parents 把"一个"放大成"全部"。典型范围放大 |
| C | ✅ 答案 | 末段两件事：有人抱怨 + 校长不打算恢复割草。合起来 = despite the disagreement, the field remains。一步推断，且贴着原文最后两句话 |
| D | ❌ 二步 | 报纸登了抱怨 → 学校报决定政策 → ? 原文没有任何"报纸有决定权"的痕迹 |

做完这两题你应该有感觉了：正确答案一点也不"聪明"，它笨笨地贴着原文；错误选项才个个"聪明"得像你自己写的。

## 常见误区

| ❌ 错误做法 | ✅ 正确做法 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 看到 infer 就开脑洞，推得越远越显得"有推理能力" | 想得越近越好，答案贴着原文一句话 | 出题人只能保证一个答案可被原文支撑。你多走一步，就多一分掉进无据选项的风险 |
| 选项带 must / only / never，读起来很确定，就选它 | 见到极端词先降半格怀疑，回原文核对范围 | 原文陈述大多留有余地（often、many、probably），绝对化用词多半是范围放大 |
| 这个话题我熟，用我的专业知识帮文章"补充" | 知识只用来帮助理解，绝不用来选答案 | 考试考的是"读没读懂这篇文章"，不是"懂不懂这个领域"。用知识补出来的选项恰恰是出题人埋的雷 |
| 推理题答案都很绕，要拐好几个弯才对 | 多数推理题只是把原文换个说法 | "绕好几个弯"的选项通常就是二步推断。你觉得绕，正说明它离原文远 |

## 实战练习

**Passage A（约 150 词）**

> Every morning, Mr. Ferrante climbs the narrow stairs to his small workshop above a bakery. Inside, hundreds of clocks cover the walls, and the room never falls completely silent. Some clocks have been in his family for three generations; others were brought in by strangers who could not find anyone else willing to fix them.
>
> His hands move slowly now. A simple repair that once took an hour may take him two, and he refuses to rush. "A clock keeps time for a hundred years," he tells young visitors. "It deserves an hour of yours."
>
> Few of his neighbors know that the trade of clock repair is dying. Training programs have closed, and young workers prefer jobs with faster money. When Mr. Ferrante finally retires, the clocks on his walls will still tick, but the hands that know how to stop them will be gone.

**1. What can we infer about Mr. Ferrante's attitude toward his work?**

- A. He finds his work boring and repetitive.
- B. He believes good repair work cannot be hurried.
- C. He wants to move to a bigger workshop.
- D. He dislikes the young visitors who come to watch.

**2. What can be inferred from the last paragraph?**

- A. Young workers earn less money than clock repairers.
- B. There will be few people left who can repair old clocks.
- C. The clocks on his walls will stop working when he retires.
- D. Training programs will reopen after he retires.

**3. What do we learn about the clocks brought in by strangers?**

- A. They are usually the oldest ones in the workshop.
- B. Other repairers had refused or been unable to fix them.
- C. They originally belonged to his family.
- D. They never tick at all.

**4. The sentence "the room never falls completely silent" suggests that ___**

- A. the bakery downstairs is always noisy
- B. at any given moment, some clock in the room is sounding
- C. Mr. Ferrante talks to the clocks while working
- D. most of the clocks are broken

**Passage B（约 145 词）**

> Many people notice a special, earthy smell when rain falls after a dry period. That smell has a name: petrichor. It was identified in the 1960s by two Australian scientists, who showed that it comes mainly from a substance produced by soil bacteria.
>
> The process works like this. During dry weather, certain bacteria in the soil release tiny oily drops. When raindrops hit the ground, they throw these drops into the air as a fine mist, much smaller than the eye can see. The mist carries the smell up to our noses.
>
> Interestingly, the smell is strongest after a light rain, not a heavy storm. A violent downpour washes the material away too quickly, while a gentle rain lifts it slowly into the air. This is why a small shower on dry soil often smells stronger than a day of heavy rain.

**5. What can be inferred about petrichor?**

- A. It can be seen in the air as a dark mist.
- B. It exists in the soil even before the rain begins.
- C. It is produced by the raindrops themselves.
- D. It was first noticed in Australia in the 1960s.

**6. What can we learn about heavy storms?**

- A. They produce the strongest petrichor of all.
- B. They wash the oily material out of the soil.
- C. They kill most of the soil bacteria.
- D. They create mist large enough to see.

**7. What does the passage suggest about light rain?**

- A. It lifts the smell into the air little by little.
- B. It falls mostly in dry countries.
- C. It produces no smell unless it lasts all day.
- D. It is less common than heavy rain.

**Passage C（约 120 词）**

> **Notice: Changes to the School Cafeteria**
>
> Starting next month, the school cafeteria will stop selling bottled water and sugary drinks. Instead, six new water stations will be installed near the dining hall and the gym. Students are encouraged to bring their own bottles, which can be filled for free.
>
> The change follows a survey completed by 800 students last term, in which 71 percent said they wanted healthier drink choices. It also follows a decision by the school board to cut plastic waste on campus.
>
> Bottled water will still be sold in the shop next to the library, which is run by the students' union and is not managed by the school.

**8. What can be inferred about the shop next to the library?**

- A. It will soon stop selling bottled water too.
- B. It operates outside the school's direct control.
- C. It refused to take part in the student survey.
- D. It sells the healthiest food on campus.

**9. What can be inferred about the reasons for the change?**

- A. Students complained about the price of bottled water.
- B. The school wants healthier options and less plastic waste.
- C. The water stations are cheaper to run than the old machines.
- D. The school board has a dispute with drink companies.

**10. What can we learn about the survey?**

- A. Most students who answered it wanted healthier drinks.
- B. All students supported removing sugary drinks.
- C. It was completed by the school board.
- D. It mainly asked teachers for their opinions.

**11. What does the notice suggest students should do?**

- A. Buy new bottles from the cafeteria.
- B. Bring a bottle from home to fill at the stations.
- C. Stop drinking water during school hours.
- D. Sign up for next term's survey.

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**Passage A：修钟人**

1. **B**。定位句：he **refuses to rush** + 引语 "It deserves an hour of yours"（它值得你的一小时）。B 项 "good repair work cannot be hurried" 是原文"拒绝赶工"的近义改写，一步推断。A 无据（他说钟"值得"花时间，恰显珍视）；C 无据（没提换工作室）；D 曲解（他给年轻人讲道理，不是讨厌他们）。

2. **B**。定位句：the trade of clock repair is **dying** + training programs have closed + the hands that know how to **stop them will be gone**。三处信息合成：会修钟的人即将寥寥。一步推断。A 反向脑补（原文说年轻人 prefer **faster money**，说明修钟来钱慢，不是年轻人挣得少）；C 字面误读（钟还在响，走的是修钟的手艺）；D 无据（没有任何"重开"的痕迹）。

3. **B**。定位句：strangers who **could not find anyone else willing to fix them**。"找不到别人愿意修"的直接含义：别的修理者拒绝或修不了。一步推断。A 无据（没说外来的钟更老）；C 相反（家里传的是另一批）；D 无据（满墙钟在响，与末段 will still tick 呼应）。

4. **B**。满墙几百座钟 + "从不完全安静"，直接含义：任何时刻总有钟在出声。一步推断。A 常识补位（归因给楼下面包房）；C 纯脑补；D 无据（在响说明走着，不是坏了）。

**Passage B：雨后泥土香（petrichor）**

5. **B**。定位句：During **dry weather**（晴天）certain bacteria in the soil **release tiny oily drops**。晴天就在释放，说明下雨前它已存在于土里。一步推断。A 相反（much smaller than the eye can see）；C 曲解（雨滴只是搬运工，生产者是细菌）；D 二步脑补（两位澳大利亚科学家**命名**了它 ≠ 气味最早在澳大利亚被注意到——看清楚选项偷偷换的动词）。

6. **B**。定位句：A violent downpour **washes the material away** too quickly。B 项 "wash the oily material out of the soil" 是近义改写。一步推断。A 相反（最强的是小雨后）；C 无据；D 曲解（看得见的是雨，不是 petrichor 雾）。

7. **A**。定位句：a **gentle** rain lifts it **slowly** into the air。A 项 "little by little" = slowly。一步推断。B、C、D 全部无据，其中 C 还和原文相反（小雨恰恰是气味最强的场景）。

**Passage C：食堂通知**

8. **B**。定位句：run by the students' union and **not managed by the school**。"不由学校管理"的直接含义就是 B"不在学校直接控制之下"。一步推断。A 二步脑补（学校减少塑料垃圾 ≠ 学生会商店也会跟进）；C、D 无据。

9. **B**。定位句：71 percent said they wanted **healthier** drink choices + a decision by the school board to **cut plastic waste**。两条理由各取一半，合成 B。一步推断。A 无据（没提价格）；C 无据（没比成本）；D 二步脑补（减少塑料垃圾推不出和公司有纠纷）。

10. **A**。定位句：**71 percent** said they wanted healthier choices。71% 是"大多数"，A 项 Most 是忠实改写。B 范围放大（All 替换 71%——数字维度被拉伸）；C 相反（800 名**学生**完成）；D 无据。

11. **B**。定位句：Students are **encouraged to bring their own bottles**。B 项 "bring a bottle from home to fill at the stations" 是近义改写。一步推断。A 无据（没让买）；C 荒谬（装水站就是让人喝水）；D 时序错乱（调查是上学期的事）。

**归因自查**：错了哪道题，就回到对应定位句重读一遍。问自己：我是没找到那句话（定位问题），还是找到了却没认出改写（替换问题），还是选了那个"我觉得很合理"的脑补选项（过度推断）？
