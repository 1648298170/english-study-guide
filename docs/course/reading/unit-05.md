---

> ⏱ 预计 4~6 小时 · 建议拆 3 天完成 | 前置：[第 4 课 · 长难句阅读](./unit-04.md) | 难度：B1+（阅读课）

从这一课开始进入题型线。主旨题是阅读题里的"大分值小题型"：数量不多（每篇至多一题），但它考的是你对全文的掌控力，而且**主旨抓得准，细节题、推断题全都跟着受益**。把文章想成一条鱼，主旨就是脊椎骨：脊椎在手，全身的肉往哪长一目了然。

## 本课目标

- [ ] 能识别主旨题的三种问法：main idea / best title / purpose
- [ ] 掌握主题句定位三处：段首、段末、段中，记住各自的信号词
- [ ] 没有主题句时，会用"高频词重复法"自己归纳主旨
- [ ] 用三条标准（覆盖全文、不过宽、有信息量）选出好标题
- [ ] 识破干扰项三大坑：细节扩大化、范围过宽、无中生有

## 核心内容

### 5.1 先认脸：主旨题的三种问法

| 问法 | 典型题干 | 在问什么 |
|------|---------|---------|
| main idea 型 | What is the passage mainly about? / What is the main idea of the passage? | 文章的中心意思 |
| best title 型 | Which of the following is the best title for the passage? | 用一个短语概括全文（标题 = 浓缩的主旨） |
| purpose 型 | What is the author's main purpose in writing the passage? | 作者写作目的（介绍 / 说服 / 警告 / 娱乐） |

三种问法一个内核：**主旨 = 话题（文章讲什么）+ 态度（作者怎么看）**。只有话题没有态度，或者只有态度没有话题，都算没抓全。

### 5.2 主题句定位三处

主题句（topic sentence）是直接表达段落大意的句子，英语说明文和议论文的段落写法高度模式化，主题句只爱待在三个地方：

**第一处：段首（命中率最高，约七成）。**
英文写作的黄金结构是"总起 + 展开"。信号特征：段首是概括性陈述句，后面跟着解释、举例、数据。

> 主题句示例：Homework has value beyond grades.（作业的价值不止于分数。）后面三句全在展开：它培养自律、暴露薄弱点、教会独立解决问题。

**第二处：段末（次之，约两成）。**
作者先摆现象、举例子，最后收拢出结论。信号词：therefore / thus / in short / in conclusion / so。

> 段落示例：三行实验数据之后，末句 "In short, practice matters more than talent."（总之，练习比天赋更重要。）这句才是脊椎。

**第三处：段中（最少，但最阴险）。**
先用让步或背景做铺垫，转折后才亮观点。信号词：but / however / yet / in fact。**转折词后面才是主人的座位。**

> 段落示例：许多人以为左手更灵活的人更聪明，研究也一度这样认为。**However**, the largest study so far found no difference at all.（然而，迄今最大规模的研究发现毫无差异。）这段的主旨是 however 之后那句。

实操流程：读每段时，按"段首 → 段末 → 转折词后"的顺序找主题句，三处都没有，才动用 5.3 的归纳法。

### 5.3 没有主题句怎么办：高频词重复法

有些文章（尤其记叙文和夹叙夹议文）没有明示的主题句，作者把主旨藏进了"反复出现"里。方法：

1. 逐段扫名词，标出重复 3 次以上的实义词（动词名词都算，介词冠词不算）
2. 高频词群 = 文章话题
3. 再看作者对这些词做了什么判断（夸？骂？担心？）= 态度
4. 话题 + 态度，拼出主旨一句话

示范：假设一篇文章里 cities、parks、rooftops、green spaces 反复出现，作者的态度词是 recovering、healthier、worth the investment，那么主旨大致是"城市绿地正在恢复，值得投入"。首段和末段是高频词最密集的地方，扫这两段性价比最高。

### 5.4 标题三标准

best title 题是主旨题的变装。好标题三条标准：

1. **覆盖全文，不片面**：只概括了第二段的标题不行
2. **范围不过宽**：把文章抽一步空的标题不行（讲"秋天叶子变色"的文章，标题"自然"就太宽）
3. **有信息量**：好标题自带"话题 + 看点"，不只是一个干巴巴的话题词

用三条标准现场筛一遍。假设文章讲秋天叶子变色的原理，四个标题：

| 候选标题 | 判定 | 理由 |
|---------|------|------|
| Leaves | 太宽 | 所有关于叶子的文章都能用，零信息量 |
| Why Autumn Leaves Change Color | ✓ | 覆盖全文 + 自带看点（原理之问） |
| How Chlorophyll Breaks Down in October | 太窄 | 只是第二段一个环节的细节 |
| Beautiful Colors of Fall | 太浅 | 只提到现象，漏掉"解释原理"这层主旨 |

### 5.5 干扰项三大坑

主旨题的错误选项是精心设计的，只有三种坑，认出坑型就能免疫：

**坑一：细节扩大化。** 拿文中一个真实存在的例子或分论点，包装成全文主旨。特征：内容正确，但只覆盖一两个段落。这是最常见的坑，因为选项"看起来没毛病"。

**坑二：范围过宽。** 把文章话题往上抽一层，大而无当。特征：正确但空洞，任何相关文章都能套。比如一篇讲"某市公交时刻表改革"的文章，选项说"现代城市交通的重要性"。

**坑三：无中生有。** 文中根本没讨论的比较、结论或态度。特征：说法本身可能符合常识，甚至很"合理"，但文章一个字没提。你的常识不能替作者发言。

### 5.6 整篇示范：250 词议论文 + 逐项排除全过程

**示范短文：Should Handwriting Survive?（约 240 词）**

> When was the last time you wrote more than a shopping list by hand? For most people, the honest answer is "months ago." Keyboards and screens have taken over almost every writing task, from love letters to lecture notes. Some schools have responded by reducing handwriting instruction, arguing that class time is better spent on typing. This trend, however, deserves a second look, because research keeps uncovering advantages of the slow, old-fashioned pen.
>
> The strongest evidence concerns learning. In several well-known experiments, students who took notes by hand remembered and understood more than those who typed, even though the typists wrote down more words. Handwriting is slower, and that slowness forces the brain to select, summarize, and rephrase. In other words, the very weakness of the pen is its hidden strength.
>
> Handwriting also connects us to memory and identity. A letter from a grandparent carries warmth that no text message can copy; doctors increasingly suggest writing by hand to patients who struggle with memory, because forming letters appears to engage the brain more deeply than tapping keys.
>
> Supporters of keyboards are right about one thing: children must learn to type. But this is not an either-or choice. A sensible school can teach both skills, giving typing its due without deleting handwriting from the timetable. Until someone invents a screen that feels like paper, the pen still has a job to do.

**第一步：略读抓结构（30 秒）。** 首段末句亮观点（This trend, however, deserves a second look：削减书法教学值得重新考虑），第二段讲学习证据，第三段讲记忆与情感，第四段让步 + 结论（教两种技能，别删书法）。文章脊椎：**手写有打字替代不了的好处，学校应保留手写教学**。

**第二步：主旨题。**
> What is the main idea of the passage?
> A. Typing is faster than handwriting, so schools should stop teaching handwriting.
> B. Handwriting offers unique benefits, so schools should keep teaching it alongside typing.
> C. Students who take notes by hand remember and understand more than those who type.
> D. Keyboards and screens have taken over almost every writing task in modern life.

**逐项排除：**

- **A**：方向反了。作者立场是保手写，A 说该停掉手写，与首段末句和全文立场相反。而且"typing is faster"文中只是背景承认。判定：**无中生有 + 反向**。杀。
- **B**：话题 handwriting ✓；态度"有独特好处、应保留"✓；"alongside typing" 对应末段"teach both skills"✓。覆盖四段：证据段、记忆段、让步结论段全部兼容。判定：**正确**。
- **C**：这句是真的，第二段原话。但它只是"学习证据"这一个分论点，第三段（记忆与情感）没覆盖。典型的"内容正确、覆盖不全"。判定：**细节扩大化**。杀。
- **D**：这句也是真的，首段原话。但它只是引出话题的背景（现状描述），不是作者的观点主张，后文三段都没在讲"接管了哪些任务"。判定：**细节（背景）扩大化**。杀。

选 B。整个过程不到 90 秒，其中排除 C 和 D 只需要各问一句："它能罩住后面几段吗？"

## 常见误区

| ❌ 错误做法 | ✅ 正确姿势 | 为什么 |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| 主旨题只看第一段第一句就选 | 按"段首 → 段末 → 转折词后"三处找主题句，必要时归纳 | 主题句也可能在段末或 however 之后；首句常常只是引子 |
| 感觉对的选项直接选，凭印象 | 拿选项逐段"罩"一遍，能覆盖所有段落才算数 | 印象来自最后读的内容（末段），细节扩大化的干扰项最爱藏在"熟悉感"里 |
| 高频词 = 主旨，抄下来就完事 | 高频词只给话题，还要加上作者的态度 | 主旨 = 话题 + 态度；只写"文章在谈城市绿地"会漏掉"作者认为值得投入" |
| 标题选最文艺、最吸睛的 | 选覆盖全文 + 范围合适 + 有信息量的 | 出题人喜欢用"漂亮但空洞"的标题钓人；标题题考概括力，不考文采 |

## 实战练习

### 第一档 · 小段落找主题句（第 1~3 题）

指出每段的主题句位置（段首 / 段末 / 段中），并抄出主题句。

**1.** Homework has value beyond grades. It forces students to review the day's lessons without a teacher watching. It also exposes weak points early, when fixing them is still cheap. Finally, homework builds the habit of working alone, a skill that every job demands sooner or later.

**2.** For years, coaches trained young swimmers six days a week, adding kilometers every month. Many believed that more training always produced faster swimmers. However, a growing body of research shows the opposite: young athletes who rest enough improve more quickly and get injured far less.

**3.** City planners planted a thousand trees along the new avenue. Shop owners reported more customers staying longer on the street. Apartment prices nearby rose by eight percent. Summer temperatures on the avenue measured two degrees lower than on treeless roads nearby. Therefore, even a single green street can change a neighborhood's economy and comfort.

### 第二档 · 干扰项坑型判断（第 4~6 题）

先读这个迷你段落：
> Greenfield's city buses used to run half-empty, and people blamed "laziness." Then the city rewrote the timetable around real working hours, adding evening routes. Within a year, passenger numbers had doubled. The lesson is clear: public transport fails not because people dislike buses, but because schedules ignore how people actually live.

这段的最佳主旨是：**Better timetables can bring riders back to public transport.**（时刻表改好，乘客就会回来。）

判断下面三个错误选项分别踩了三大坑中的哪一个：

**4.** "Greenfield added evening bus routes, and passenger numbers doubled within a year."

**5.** "The importance of public transport in modern cities."

**6.** "People no longer dislike taking buses to work."

### 第三档 · 整篇实战（第 7~10 题）

**练习短文：Should Students Grade Their Teachers?（约 185 词）**

> For decades, evaluation has flowed in only one direction: teachers grade students, and students keep quiet. Recently, however, some schools have begun to ask a brave question: should students grade their teachers?
>
> Supporters argue that students are the best witnesses. They sit in the classroom every day; they notice which teacher explains twice without losing patience and which one simply reads slides aloud. In cities such as Chicago, where student surveys have been tested, principals say the feedback helps them decide which teachers need coaching.
>
> Critics see a popularity contest. A strict teacher who assigns heavy reading, they warn, may receive terrible reviews from lazy students, while an easy grader collects five stars. Teachers might soften standards to win approval, and the students would pay the real price years later.
>
> Both sides, interestingly, agree that surveys should never be the only measure. Test scores, classroom visits, and experienced judgments still matter. But as one principal put it, "If a restaurant wants to know whether the food is good, it should ask the people eating it." Schools exist for the students. Refusing to hear their voice, it seems, protects everyone except the learners.

**7.** What is the main idea of the passage?
A. Students in Chicago have begun grading their teachers.
B. Student feedback on teachers is valuable but should remain one of several measures.
C. Strict teachers always receive worse reviews than easy graders.
D. Schools should replace test scores with student surveys.

**8.** Which of the following would be the best title for the passage?
A. The Brave Schools of Chicago
B. A Popularity Contest in the Classroom
C. Should Students Grade Their Teachers?
D. How to Design a Teaching Survey

**9.** What is the author's main purpose in writing the passage?
A. To report a new school policy in full detail
B. To argue that teacher grading by students deserves a careful try
C. To warn schools against using any student feedback
D. To entertain readers with funny classroom stories

**10.** The restaurant quotation ("If a restaurant wants to know whether the food is good...") is mainly used to ___.
A. support the idea that students' opinions about teaching deserve to be heard
B. prove that schools and restaurants face exactly the same problems
C. show that the principal dislikes the survey system
D. suggest that schools should also serve better food

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**1. 段首。** 主题句：Homework has value beyond grades.（作业的价值不止于分数。）后面三个 It.../It also.../Finally... 全是展开句。

**2. 段中（转折词 however 之后）。** 主题句：a growing body of research shows the opposite: young athletes who rest enough improve more quickly and get injured far less.（足够休息的年轻运动员进步更快、受伤更少。）前面两句是背景铺垫，However 一响，主人登场。

**3. 段末。** 主题句：Therefore, even a single green street can change a neighborhood's economy and comfort.（哪怕一条绿街也能改变街区的经济与舒适度。）信号词 Therefore 引出结论，前面全是证据（树、客流、房价、温度）。

**4. 细节扩大化。** 选项内容真实（第二句原话），但只是"加晚班车 + 客流翻倍"这一个证据，罩不住结论句"时刻表该跟着真实作息走"这层主旨。

**5. 范围过宽。** "现代城市公共交通的重要性"能套住一切公交话题文章，本文只谈"时刻表与乘客"这一个切面。太大 = 不选。

**6. 无中生有。** 原文说客流翻倍的原因是时刻表改了，没有说"人们从此爱坐公交了"（people no longer dislike taking buses）。原文甚至明说问题从来不在 dislike。选项自身听着合理，但文章没说，这就是这个坑的钓饵。

**7. B。** 定位：首段末句提出问题，二段正方、三段反方，四段 Both sides, interestingly, agree that surveys should never be the only measure + 末句"拒绝听学生的声音保护了所有人，除了学习者"。主旨 = 学生反馈有价值，但只能是标准之一。A 是二段细节（芝加哥）扩大化；C 是三段反方担忧（they warn 是引用的担忧，且 always 太绝对，原文无此说）属于细节 + 无中生有；D 方向偏了，原文明确说 surveys should never be the only measure，D 说"取代"考试成绩，反向。

**8. C。** 标题三标准逐条过：C 是问句，正好覆盖"正方 + 反方 + 结论"的全文结构，自带看点（一个有争议的好问题）；A 只罩住第二段的例子（细节扩大化）；B 只代表反方立场（细节 + 立场偏颇）；D 无中生有（全文没讲怎么设计问卷）。

**9. B。** purpose 题：作者摆出双方观点后，用 "But as one principal put it..." 和结尾句明确偏向"该听听学生的声音"，但又不激进（承认不能只靠问卷），所以是"论证这件事值得一试"而非单纯报道（A）、警告（C）或娱乐（D）。

**10. A。** 餐厅之喻的功能定位：学校为学生而存在（Schools exist for the students），就像菜好不好该问吃饭的人。引语紧跟在"双方都同意问卷不该是唯一标准"之后，是给"学生声音值得听"这一面补的最后一锤，不是给反方。B 把类比当成了等同（exactly the same problems，类比永远只是相似）；C 与引语的拥护语气相反；D 字面化陷阱，谈"食物"就真选食物。
