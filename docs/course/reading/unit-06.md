> ⏱ 预计 4~6 小时 · 建议拆 3 天完成 | 前置：[第 5 课 · 主旨题](./unit-05.md) | 难度：B1+（阅读课）

细节题是阅读题库里的大头：四六级一篇阅读五道题，通常三道以上是细节题。好消息是，它是所有题型里**最像工程活**的一题：不玄学、不靠语感，靠两样硬技术：**定位**（找到原文那句话）和**同义替换**（认出选项是原句的变装）。这一课把两样技术拆到手上。

## 本课目标

- [ ] 会用定位三步法：题干关键词 → 扫描定位句 → 读定位句前后各一句
- [ ] 能按优先级挑关键词：数字、专有名词 > 独特名词 > 普通实词
- [ ] 认出四种同义替换：同义词、词性转换、句式转换、正话反说
- [ ] 掌握 NOT TRUE 题型的逆向操作：先找三个对的，剩下就是错的
- [ ] 能破解定位陷阱：关键词多处出现时，用第二关键词锁定

## 核心内容

### 6.1 细节题的本质：一场"变装辨认"游戏

细节题的公式只有一行：

> **细节题 = 回原文定位 + 认出选项的同义替换**

命题人的出题流程是固定的：从文章里挑一句话 → 把它改写成选项（换词、换词性、换句式）→ 再造三个错误选项（偷换、扩大、无中生有）。所以正确选项长什么样？**它一定是你定位到的那句话的"变装版"**，很少原样照抄。

这个规律反过来用威力更大：**错误选项最爱原词照抄**。把原文的词原封不动搬进选项，制造"眼熟 = 正确"的错觉，这是命题人的经典钓鱼手法。记住口诀：照抄多留心，变装才是真。

### 6.2 定位三步法

**第一步：从题干里挑关键词。**

关键词不是随便划的，有优先级：

| 优先级 | 关键词类型 | 为什么好用 | 例 |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-----|
| ★★★ | 数字（年份、百分比、金额） | 视觉上极好扫，原文里独一无二 | 2023、23 percent |
| ★★★ | 专有名词（人名、地名、机构名） | 首字母大写，扫描目标大写锁定 | Dr. Fischer、Chicago |
| ★★ | 独特名词 / 动词（题干里最"扎眼"的实词） | 出现次数少，容易定位 | safety brake、surplus |
| ★ | 普通实词 | 全文到处都是，只能当第二关键词 | study、problem、students |

挑 1~2 个就够，挑多了反而互相干扰。

**第二步：回原文扫描，找到定位句。**

带着关键词回文章，眼睛做纵向扫描（大写字母和数字在竖向扫视里特别显眼），锁定关键词所在的句子。注意：定位句未必包含题干全部关键词，可能只含其中一个。

**第三步：读定位句 + 前后各一句。**

正确答案经常不是定位句本身，而是它的上下文（比如定位句是个例子，答案在例子前后的观点句里）。**前后各一句**是安全边界，这是无数考生用分数换来的经验值。

### 6.3 同义替换四件套

认出"变装"是细节题的核心功力。变装只有四种：

| 替换类型 | 原文 | 正确选项 |
|---------|------|---------|
| 同义词替换 | extend their feeding season | make the feeding season **longer** |
| 词性转换 | the **discovery** of oil | after oil **was discovered** |
| 句式转换 | A city is not a concrete desert（陈述句） | Cities **can actually be** good habitats（换句式重述） |
| 正话反说 | Fewer than one in a thousand is ever found（很少被找到） | **Most bottles are never** found（绝大多数找不到） |

训练方法：以后每次做完细节题，不管对错，都把"定位句 → 正确选项"抄成一对，标上属于哪种替换。抄上二十对，你的眼睛就建立了自动识别。

### 6.4 NOT TRUE 题型：逆向操作

题干长这样：Which of the following statements is NOT true? / All of the following are true EXCEPT ___.

新手做法：逐个选项验证，四个都查一遍，时间哗哗流走。

逆向操作：**反向找三个"对的"**。因为"对的选项"往往直接来自原文，验证起来飞快（关键词定位 + 原词/替换确认，各 10 秒）；三个"对的"划掉之后，剩下那个验证不了的，就是答案。

流程：A 定位成功且同义 → 划掉；B 定位成功且同义 → 划掉；C 定位成功且同义 → 划掉；D 找不到原文支撑或与原文冲突 → 选 D。你找"对的"的速度，永远快于证明"错的"。

### 6.5 定位陷阱与破解

**陷阱一：关键词多处出现。**
"students" 这种词在一篇教育文章里出现 8 次，从哪开始找？破解：**换用或追加第二关键词**。题干问"学生对新规定的反应"，就别拿 students 定位，拿"新规定"（the new rule / the policy）去扫。

**陷阱二：数字题被"数字海"淹没。**
涉及年份、百分比的题目，别顺着读，**直接竖向扫数字**：先扫出所有数字的位置，再逐个核对是谁的数字。一篇文章里 2023、120,000、23%、12%、80% 各有归属，扫错了主语，答案就错位。

**陷阱三：定位句≠答案句。**
关键词所在的句子只负责"报到"，答案可能在它前一句（观点）或后一句（结果）。所以 6.2 第三步的前后各一句不是仪式，是保命。

### 6.6 整篇示范：3 道细节题全流程

**示范短文：City Bees（约 215 词）**

> Scientists have some surprising news for gardeners: city bees may be doing better than their country cousins. A ten-year study, led by researchers at Holloway University and published last year, followed 120,000 bees across fourteen cities in Europe. The results upset many people's expectations.
>
> In the countryside, modern farming often replaces wild flowers with a single crop. Bees, which need different flowers during different weeks of the season, struggle in these green deserts. Urban areas, by contrast, offer parks, rooftop gardens, balconies, and abandoned land where wild plants grow freely. City bees also enjoy warmer temperatures, which extend their feeding season by up to three weeks.
>
> The numbers tell the story. In the study, city bees produced 23 percent more honey per hive than rural bees, and their winter survival rate was 12 percent higher. "A city is not a concrete desert," said Dr. Lena Fischer, the lead researcher. "It is a huge, unplanned garden."
>
> There is, however, a catch. Not every city is friendly. Areas where buildings cover more than 80 percent of the ground still showed poor bee health, and bees from the busiest streets carried more heavy metals on their bodies. The researchers therefore suggest that city planners leave small untidy corners, plant local flowers rather than expensive imported ones, and stop cutting grass so often. Perfection, they note, is the enemy of pollination.

**第 1 题（数字细节）：**
> According to the study, how much more honey did city bees produce per hive than rural bees?
> A. 12 percent　B. 23 percent　C. 80 percent　D. 3 weeks

流程演示：①关键词：honey（独特名词）+ 数字。②竖向扫数字：120,000（第一段，规模）、three weeks（第二段，季节）、23 percent / 12 percent（第三段）、80 percent（第四段）。③核对归属："23 percent more honey per hive"，honey 挂在 23% 上。12% 是越冬存活率，80% 是建筑覆盖率，3 weeks 是觅食季延长。**答案 B**。全程 20 秒，这就是"数字直接扫"的威力。

**第 2 题（同义替换）：**
> By calling a city "a huge, unplanned garden," Dr. Fischer means that ___.
> A. cities should build more public gardens for bees
> B. cities accidentally provide bees with rich and varied flowers
> C. city gardens are usually poorly designed and need repair
> D. bees prefer gardens to parks in urban areas

流程演示：①关键词：Dr. Fischer / unplanned garden（引语原词，专名 + 独特短语双保险）。②定位：第三段末两句。③读前后：引语前一句讲"城市蜜蜂产蜜多 23%、存活率高 12%"，第二段讲"城市有公园、屋顶花园、阳台、荒地，野花自由生长"。④比对选项：**unplanned（无计划的）对应 accidentally（偶然地）；a huge ... garden 对应 parks, rooftop gardens, balconies ... wild plants grow freely（丰富多样的花源）**。B 是两段的正话重述。A 把"无心的巧合"偷换成"该去 deliberate 建设"（should build），反向；C 把 unplanned 曲解成"设计差需要修"，字面陷阱；D 无中生有（原文没比较花园和公园）。**答案 B**。

**第 3 题（NOT TRUE）：**
> Which of the following is NOT true according to the passage?
> A. Warmer city temperatures lengthen the feeding season of city bees.
> B. Imported flowers are better for bees than local ones.
> C. Bees from the busiest streets carried more heavy metals on their bodies.
> D. Modern farming in the countryside often plants a single crop.

流程演示（逆向操作）：A 定位第二段末句 "warmer temperatures, which extend their feeding season"，extend = lengthen 同义替换 → 对的，划掉。C 定位第四段 "bees from the busiest streets carried more heavy metals" 原词级一致 → 对的，划掉。D 定位第二段首句 "modern farming often replaces wild flowers with a single crop" → 对的，划掉。B 找定位：第四段建议 "plant local flowers **rather than** expensive imported ones"（种本地花而不是引进花），可见本地更好，B 说反了 → **答案 B**。三个"对的"各花了 10 秒，比正面证明 B 错误快得多。

## 常见误区

| ❌ 错误做法 | ✅ 正确姿势 | 为什么 |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| 凭刚读完的印象直接选选项 | 每道细节题必须回文定位，找到定位句再比对 | 印象是模糊的，选项是精心设计来钓印象的；定位是唯一硬证据 |
| 选项里出现原文原词，觉得"眼熟"就选 | 原词照抄恰恰要警觉，先确认逻辑是否被偷换 | 正确项通常被同义改写；错误项最爱拿原词当鱼饵 |
| 定位到关键词所在句就直接选答案 | 再读定位句的前一句和后一句 | 答案常在例子前后的观点句里；只读定位句会漏掉上下文逻辑 |
| NOT TRUE 题四个选项挨个正面验证 | 逆向操作：快速找三个"对的"划掉，剩下即答案 | 验证"对的"只需定位 + 替换确认，10 秒一个；证明"错的"要读全段，性价比极低 |

## 实战练习

### 第一档 · 圈定位词（第 1~3 题）

下面的题干（针对 6.6 示范短文 City Bees），你会优先圈哪 1~2 个关键词？说出理由。

**1.** According to Dr. Fischer, what is a city compared to?

**2.** How long did the study mentioned in the passage last?

**3.** What do the researchers suggest city planners do with grass?

### 第二档 · 同义替换辨认（第 4~6 题）

把下面的原文句和选项配对，并写出替换类型（同义词 / 词性转换 / 句式转换 / 正话反说）。

**4.** 原文：Urban bees also enjoy warmer temperatures, which extend their feeding season by up to three weeks.

**5.** 原文：The results upset many people's expectations.

**6.** 原文：There is, however, a catch.

候选选项（对应上面三句的正确项，乱序）：
a. However, a hidden problem remains.
b. Warmer cities make the feeding season longer for bees.
c. Many people were surprised by the findings.

### 第三档 · 整篇实战（第 7~11 题）

**练习短文：A Four-Day School Week?（约 190 词）**

> In 2022, a small town in Iowa made a decision that surprised the whole country: its public schools would move to a four-day week. Three years later, more than 150 schools in rural America have copied the idea, and the results deserve careful reading.
>
> Supporters point to money and morale. Cutting one school day saves each district tens of thousands of dollars a year on buses, electricity, and substitute teachers. Teachers report fewer sick days, and parents in farming communities say children help more at home.
>
> Critics, however, point to the youngest students. A study of 40 schools by Crestline University found that students in four-day programs learned about 15 percent less mathematics per year than similar students on five-day schedules, though reading scores stayed nearly the same. Working parents face another problem: finding care for the extra day off costs them, on average, 90 dollars a week.
>
> Both sides agree on one thing: schools that added longer hours to the remaining four days protected their scores best. In other words, the calendar matters less than the total learning time. Districts now considering the change would be wise to do the math first.

**7.**（数字细节）According to the Crestline University study, students in four-day programs learned about ___ less mathematics per year.
A. 40 percent　B. 15 percent　C. 90 percent　D. 150 percent

**8.**（同义替换）What extra cost do working parents face under the new schedule?
A. Higher bus fees charged by the district
B. Paying for someone to look after their children on the extra day off
C. Buying more books for longer school days
D. Paying higher electricity bills in winter

**9.**（NOT TRUE）Which of the following is NOT true according to the passage?
A. Reading scores of students in four-day programs stayed nearly the same.
B. Cutting one school day saves money on buses and electricity.
C. All the schools that moved to a four-day week successfully protected their scores.
D. Teachers in four-day districts report fewer sick days.

**10.**（定位陷阱）Which students does the passage say were most affected by the shorter week?
A. Students in farming communities
B. The youngest students
C. Students who ride school buses
D. Students at Crestline University

**11.**（方法反思）第 9 题你用了逆向操作吗？写出你的"三个对的"分别定位在哪一句。

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**1.** 优先圈 **Dr. Fischer**（专有名词，全文只出现一次，大写字母扫描即中）。辅以引语关键成分 "a city compared to"。定位到第三段末两句。

**2.** 优先圈 **how long / last（持续时间）**，然后用数字扫第一段：A **ten-year** study ... followed 120,000 bees。答案藏在数词短语 ten-year 里。这道题的教训：数字不一定是百分比，ten-year 这种"数字 + 名词"结构也要进扫描清单。

**3.** 优先圈 **grass**（独特名词，全文只在最后一段出现一次，比 planners 好定位得多）。定位到末段 "stop cutting grass so often"，答案就在这一小句里。这条题演示了"独特名词优于普通名词"的选词原则。

**4 → b（句式转换 + 同义词）。** 原文 "warmer temperatures ... extend their feeding season"，选项 b "Warmer cities make the feeding season longer"：extend → make ... longer（同义），定语从句 which extend... → make 宾补结构（句式转换）。

**5 → c（句式转换）。** 原文主语是 The results，谓语 upset（使失望/意外），宾语 expectations；选项 c 换了视角：Many people were surprised by the findings。upset expectations ≈ were surprised，findings ≈ results（同义成分），主被动句式翻转。

**6 → a（同义词替换）。** however → However（原样保留），There is a catch → a hidden problem remains：catch（暗藏的问题）→ hidden problem，there be 存在句 → remain 静态动词。catch 是本题真正考的词，你不需要认识它也能凭配对做对。

**7. B。** 流程：关键词 Crestline University（专名）+ mathematics → 定位第三段第二句 "learned about **15 percent** less mathematics per year"。干扰数字各有归属：40 是被研究的学校数，90 是家长每周托育费（美元），150 是采纳新制度的学校总数。数字题先扫全部数字、再核对主语归属，就不会张冠李戴。

**8. B。** 流程：关键词 extra cost / working parents → 定位第三段末句 "finding care for the extra day off costs them, on average, 90 dollars a week"。替换识别：**finding care（找人照看）→ paying for someone to look after their children**，典型同义扩写。A 的 buses、D 的 electricity 出现在第二段，是"学区省下的钱"，与家长的额外支出无关，属于跨段偷拼；C 无中生有。

**9. C。** 逆向操作：A 对应第三段 "reading scores stayed nearly the same" → 对，划掉。B 对应第二段 "saves ... on buses, electricity" → 对，划掉。D 对应第二段 "Teachers report fewer sick days" → 对，划掉。C 找不到支撑且与末段冲突：原文说 "schools **that added longer hours** ... protected their scores best"，即只有加长课时的那部分学校保住了成绩，不是"所有"学校。all 是绝对化偷换。答案 C。

**10. B。** 定位陷阱破解示范：如果拿 students 定位，全文每段都有，寸步难行；换第二关键词 most affected → 第三段 "Critics, however, point to **the youngest students**"。A 是干扰项：farming communities 出现在第二段，说的是"孩子在家帮忙多"，与"受影响最大"无关（跨段偷拼）；C 的 buses 是费用细节；D 的 Crestline 是研究方，不是受影响学生。

**11.** 开放题，参考：三个"对的"的定位句分别是：A → "though reading scores stayed nearly the same"（第三段）；B → "saves each district ... on buses, electricity, and substitute teachers"（第二段）；D → "Teachers report fewer sick days"（第二段）。如果你是逐项正面验证做的第 9 题，下次刻意练习逆向流：先划三个对的，速度差距做三套题就能感受到。
