## 本课目标

被动的"基本款"（be + 过去分词、各时态被动、by 省略）你在 B2 第 4 课已经拿下。这一课专攻三个深化方向：口语里的 get 被动、完成式被动、以及"哪些动词根本没有被动"。学完之后，你应该能做到：

- [ ] 日常口语中自然使用 get 被动（get paid / get changed / get hurt）
- [ ] 识别并写出情态完成式被动（should have been done）
- [ ] 短语动词变被动时把小品词一个不落地带上
- [ ] 报出被动禁区的四类动词，见招拆招改错
- [ ] 警惕"中文主动表被动"的迁移错误

## 语法详解

### 第 1 步：get 被动——be 被动的口语替身

口语里，get 可以顶替 be，构成"get + 过去分词"的被动。它自带一股"发生变化、遭遇某事"的动感，像镜头里的动作切换：

| 搭配 | 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| get paid | Workers **get paid** every Friday.（工人们每周五发工资。） | 拿到工资 |
| get changed | Give me a minute to **get changed**.（给我一分钟换身衣服。） | 换好衣服 |
| get hurt | Luckily, nobody **got hurt** in the accident.（幸好事故里没人受伤。） | 受伤 |
| get lost | We **got lost** in the old town.（我们在老城区迷路了。） | 迷路 |
| get married | They **got married** last spring.（他们去年春天结的婚。） | 结婚 |
| get finished | I need to **get finished** before noon.（我得在中午前干完。） | 干完活 |

get 被动与 be 被动的分工：

| | be + 过去分词 | get + 过去分词 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 语体 | 中性，书面口语通吃 | 偏口语、偏非正式 |
| 语义 | 状态或动作皆可 | 强调"变化的过程、遭遇"，像镜头切到那一刻 |
| 疑问否定 | 借 be 变化（isn't done） | 借 do 变化（didn't get done） |
| by 短语 | 可以带（was written by Lu Xun） | 基本不搭 by |

对比体会：The window was broken（窗户破了/被打破了，状态或事实）vs The window got broken（窗户给弄破了——隐约有个"出事了"的现场感）。

### 第 2 步：完成式被动——should have been done

情态动词 + 完成 + 被动，三层叠罗汉："本该被做（却没做）""一定已经被做"这类考点的标准包装：

公式：**情态动词 + have + been + 过去分词**

- The report **should have been finished** last week, but it is still on his desk.（报告本该上周完成，现在还躺在他桌上。）
- The lights are off; the office **must have been locked** already.（灯全关了，办公室肯定已经锁了。）
- This temple **can't have been built** in the 1920s; the style is much older.（这座庙不可能是 20 世纪 20 年代建的，风格要古老得多。）

拆解一句看层次：

```text
should have been finished
└─┬─┘  └───┬────┘└───┬────┘
情态   完成助动词    过去分词
（本该）  （已）    （被完成）
```

考试信号：选项里同时出现 should be finished / should have been finished / should have finished，先判断语态（主语 report 是承受者→被动），再判断时间（last week 已过去且没做成→完成式）。

### 第 3 步：短语动词的被动——小品词一个都不能少

短语动词（动词 + 小品词）变被动时，look after 里的 after、turn off 里的 off 必须**原样带上**，不能拆：

- The children **are looked after** well by their grandmother.（孩子们被奶奶照顾得很好。——after 在）
- The lights **were turned off** before we left.（我们走之前灯被关掉了。——off 在）
- The doctor **was sent for** at once.（立刻派人去请医生了。——for 在）
- His idea **was laughed at** at first, but later everyone accepted it.（他的想法起初被嘲笑，后来大家又都接受了。——at 在）

错误画像：❌ The children are looked well by their grandmother.（after 被吞掉后，look 变回"看"，句意崩塌）。记住：小品词是短语动词的"身份证"，被动时随身携带。

### 第 4 步：被动禁区——四类动词没有被动

不是所有动词都能变成"被"。下面四类是禁区，考改错题的高发地：

**① 不及物动词：动作不落在宾语上，无处"被"**

- ❌ The accident was happened last week. → ✅ The accident **happened** last week.
- ❌ The train was arrived at six. → ✅ The train **arrived** at six.
- 同类：happen、arrive、occur、come、go、die、rise

**② 系动词：后面接的是表语不是宾语，谈不上被动**

- ❌ He is seemed tired. → ✅ He **seems** tired.
- ❌ The door was remained open. → ✅ The door **remained** open.
- 同类：seem、remain、become、appear、look（"看起来"义）、feel（"感觉"义）

**③ have 表"拥有"：拥有的关系变不了被动**

- ❌ A new car is had by my uncle. → ✅ My uncle **has** a new car.
- 同类：have（拥有）、own、possess、belong to（❌ This house is belonged to him → ✅ This house belongs to him）

**④ 状态动词：cost、last、fit、suit、hold（容纳）等**

- ❌ Fifty dollars was cost by this shirt. → ✅ This shirt **cost** me fifty dollars.
- ❌ Two hours were lasted by the meeting. → ✅ The meeting **lasted** two hours.
- 同类：cost、last、fit、suit、lack

记忆抓手：**"落不到宾语身上的动作，就没有被动"**。不及物动词没有宾语，系动词和状态动词接的是状态不是动作对象，所以全都在禁区里。

### 第 5 步：中文"主动表被动"的迁移错误——复习与升级

中文常说"这本书很好卖""这支笔很好写"——主语是承受者，动词却用主动形式。英语在这类句子里同样用主动（动词自带"好做"的含义），学了被动反而容易手滑加 be。B2 第 4 课的中文专题（详见[B2 第 4 课](../b2/unit-04.md)）讲过原理，这里放一张速查表复习：

| 中文直觉 | ❌ 迁移错误 | ✅ 正确英语 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 这本书很好卖 | The book is sold well | The book **sells well** |
| 这支笔很好写 | The pen is written smoothly | The pen **writes smoothly** |
| 布料摸起来很软 | The cloth is felt soft | The cloth **feels soft** |
| 水很快烧开了 | The water was boiled quickly | The water **boiled quickly** |

两套逻辑分开记：**"被做成"用被动（The bridge was built in 1998），"好做/做着如何"用主动（The bridge holds 2,000 people）**。

## 易混辨析

| # | ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The accident was happened yesterday. | **happened** | happen 不及物，无被动——禁区① |
| 2 | He didn't got hurt in the game. | **didn't get hurt** | get 被动的否定要借助动词 do（get 当实义动词看待），不是把 get 变否定 |
| 3 | The children are looked well. | **are looked after well** | 短语动词被动不可拆，after 必须带上 |
| 4 | The project should have finished last month.（主语是 project） | **should have been finished** | project 是"被完成"的承受者，光有完成没有被动，语态丢了 |
| 5 | This house is belonged to my grandparents. | **belongs to** | belong to 是状态动词，禁区成员，只有主动 |
| 6 | The shirt was cost me fifty dollars. | **cost me** | cost 无被动，且过去式 cost 同形——双重陷阱 |

再对比一组"be 被动 vs get 被动"的语气差：

| 句子 | 语感 |
| --- | --- |
| He was hurt in the accident. | 平静陈述"他在事故中受了伤"（报告口吻） |
| He got hurt in the accident. | "他出了事故受了伤"——有事发生的现场感，口语首选 |

## 实战练习

### 第一档 · 热身（单点直击）

1. Hurry up and ______ dressed; the guests will arrive in ten minutes.（A. get　B. be get　C. got　D. getting）
2. Luckily, no one ______ when the earthquake happened.（A. get hurt　B. got hurt　C. was hurted　D. hurted）
3. An earthquake ______ in that area last month.（A. was happened　B. happened　C. was happening　D. has been happened）

### 第二档 · 标准（考试风格）

4. The workers ______ twice a month in this factory.（A. get pay　B. get paid　C. are paying　D. paid）
5. The lights ______ before the last person left the office.（A. were turned off　B. turned off　C. were turned　D. were off turned）
6. The old temple ______ in the seventeenth century.（A. can build　B. can't have been built　C. must build　D. can't build）
7. The report ______ to the manager yesterday, but nobody remembers seeing it.（A. should send　B. should be sent　C. should have been sent　D. should have sent）
8. The baby ______ by the neighbor while the mother was at work.（A. was looked after　B. was looked　C. looked after　D. was looking after）
9. — How was your camping trip? — Terrible. Our tent ______ down in the storm.（A. blew　B. was blown　C. was blowing　D. blew up）　提示：帐篷是"被吹倒"的
10. The meeting ______ for three hours and nobody could leave.（A. was lasted　B. lasted　C. was lasting　D. had lasted by）
11. This cloth ______ smooth; it must be expensive.（A. is felt　B. feels　C. is feeling　D. felt by）

### 第三档 · 挑战（语境综合）

12. 改错：The letters were answered and the questions were replied by the secretary.
13. 改错：Her new novel is sold very well in both China and abroad.
14. 翻译（用 get 被动）：他换好衣服就出门了。（提示：get changed）

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看答案</summary>

1. **A**。get dressed 是 get 被动（祈使句原形 get）；B 结构不存在。
2. **B**。got hurt——过去时间的 get 被动；hurt 过去分词与原形同形，没有 hurted。
3. **B**。happen 不及物动词，无被动（禁区①）。
4. **B**。工人们"被发工资"，get paid 口语被动；A 缺过去分词，C 是进行时主动，语态错。
5. **A**。turn off 短语动词被动不可拆，were turned off；C 丢 off，D 语序错乱。
6. **B**。"不可能建于 17 世纪"= 情态完成被动 can't have been built；时间 + 风格推断指向完成推测。
7. **C**。"昨天本该被送到却没送"，should have been sent：完成式 + 被动双考点。
8. **A**。look after 短语动词被动，after 必须带上；D 主动语态逻辑不通（婴儿不能照顾别人）。
9. **B**。帐篷被吹倒：was blown down；blow 与 tent 是被动关系。
10. **B**。last（持续）无被动，禁区④；句意"会议持续了三小时"。
11. **B**。feel 是系动词（摸起来），主动表状态，无被动——中文"被摸起来"迁移错误。
12. were replied → **were replied to**。reply 是短语动词 reply to sth，被动时 to 必须带上：the questions were replied to。
13. is sold very well → **sells very well**。"好卖"是中文主动表被动的迁移场景，英语用主动 sells well。
14. **He got changed and went out.**（换好衣服= get changed，口语被动首选）
