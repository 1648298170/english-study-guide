## 语法讲解

### 一、C1 六课知识地图

| 课次 | 主题 | 一句话规则 | 标志例句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [第 1 课](./unit-01.md) | 倒装 | 否定或限定副词开头，助动词提到主语前 | Never have I heard such a weak excuse. |
| [第 2 课](./unit-02.md) | 分裂句 | It is/was + 强调对象 + that/who + 句子其余 | It was the reform, not luck, that saved the firm. |
| [第 3 课](./unit-03.md) | 省略与替代 | so/neither + 助动词 + 主语；one/that 代名词，do (so) 代动词 | I've ordered the salmon, and so has Ben. |
| [第 4 课](./unit-04.md) | 名词化 | 动词、形容词升格为名词，句子紧凑而客观 | decide → the company's decision to expand |
| [第 5 课](./unit-05.md) | 语域控制 | 按场合切换正式/中性/非正式，全程不混档 | I am writing to inform you... / Just a quick heads-up... |
| [第 6 课](./unit-06.md) | hedging 与话语标记 | 不把话说死；用路标词组织话语与态度 | may...to some extent; Having said that,... |

### 二、逐课一分钟回顾

每个结构配一组"平底句 → 武装句"，读三遍找手感：

- **倒装**：I have never heard such nonsense. → Never have I heard such nonsense.（重点提前，语气陡增）
- **分裂句**：My sister broke the vase. → It was my sister who broke the vase.（矛头直指"就是她"）
- **省略与替代**：He ordered the salmon, and I ordered the salmon too. → He ordered the salmon, and so did I.（干净，不啰嗦）
- **名词化**：The company decided to expand. This surprised everyone. → The company's decision to expand surprised everyone.（两句并一句，正式文体引擎）
- **语域**：We got your message. → We have received your message.（同一件事，正式场合的第二副面孔）
- **hedging**：The tax will reduce inequality. → The tax may somewhat reduce inequality.（留了余地，反而更可信）

### 三、六大工具组合拳

C1 的真功夫不在单用，而在组合。看一个底句的四次升级：

底句（非正式）：The government acted late, and that is why the crisis got worse.

| 版本 | 用了什么 | 升级后 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 强调版 | 分裂句 | It was the delayed response that made the crisis worse. |
| 叙事版 | 倒装 | Not until months later did the government respond, and by then the crisis had worsened. |
| 正式版 | 名词化 + 正式词 | The government's delayed response substantially exacerbated the crisis. |
| 学术版 | hedging + 名词化 + 正式词 | It could be argued that the government's delayed response somewhat exacerbated the crisis. |

组合守则三条：

1. **正式文体黄金三角**：名词化 + 被动 + 正式词（第 4、5 课联手），论文和报告的默认底盘。
2. **强调工具限量使用**：倒装和分裂句是聚光灯，一段各最多一次；句句倒装等于句句没强调。
3. **hedging 配学术语域**：may / somewhat / arguably 出现在正式段落里是严谨，出现在给朋友的短信里是做作。工具跟着语域走。

### 四、这一课怎么用：自测流程与评分

1. **热身**：先做改写大闯关 10 题，不限时，把六件工具逐件上手。
2. **笔试**：综合笔试 30 题，限时 60 分钟，不查词典不查笔记，还原真实考场。
3. **归因**：对答案时给每道错题贴标签，是倒装错了、名词化形式错了，还是语域失手？同类错两道以上，回对应课重学那一节。
4. **写作**：三项写作自测不限时，但必须批改（教师、语伴或 AI 均可），按批注修改一轮才算完成。

## 中文母语者常见错误

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not only he was late, but he also forgot the files. | Not only was he late, but he also forgot the files. | 否定词开头必须倒装，把 was 提到主语前；这是第 1 课的头号考点 |
| It was in 2019 when the company was founded. | It was in 2019 that the company was founded. | 分裂句强调时间、地点状语时一律用 that；考试标准里 when 是硬伤 |
| I like jazz, and so I do. | I like jazz, and so do I. | so 句型是 so + 助动词 + 主语，顺序颠倒就变成了"所以我的确如此" |
| The decide of the committee surprised us. | The committee's decision surprised us. | decide 是动词，名词化形式是 decision；-tion/-sion/-ment 是第 4 课的高频后缀 |
| Hardly the meeting had started when the alarm rang. | Hardly had the meeting started when the alarm rang. | hardly 句首必倒装，且 hardly...when 是固定搭档，时态是过去完成时 |
| 正式邮件通篇 I think it will definitely work. | It would appear that the plan may prove effective. | 正式文体里 I think + definitely 双重踩雷：语域太口语 + 绝对化断言，第 5、6 课一起白学 |

## 练习

### Part A · 改写大闯关（10 题，先热身）

1. 用倒装改写：I have never eaten better dumplings.
2. 用倒装改写：We realized only then how serious it was.
3. 用分裂句改写（强调 the internet）：The internet, not television, changed everything.
4. 用分裂句改写（强调 at a conference in 2019）：I first met her at a conference in 2019.
5. 用 so 改写：Alice passed the interview, and Ben passed the interview too.
6. 用 neither 改写：He doesn't own a car. His wife doesn't own a car.
7. 用名词化改写：The company decided to expand, and this surprised everyone.
8. 用名词化改写：Because prices rose sharply, demand fell.
9. 语域升级为正式文体：We got a lot of complaints, so we ended the service.
10. 加 hedging 改写（正式语域）：Social media is bad for teenagers.

### Part B · 综合笔试（30 题，限时 60 分钟，不查资料）

**B1 选择题（10 题）**

1. Never before ___ such a demand for remote work.（A. there has been B. has there been C. there was D. was there）
2. Not only ___ the deadline, but he also exceeded the budget.（A. he missed B. missed he C. did he miss D. he did miss）
3. It was the intern, ___ the manager, who caught the error.（A. rather than B. instead of C. no D. not）
4. "I've visited Kyoto." "___"（A. So have I. B. So I have. C. So do I. D. Neither have I.）
5. She doesn't drive, and ___ her husband.（A. so doesn't B. neither does C. either does D. does neither）
6. The ___ of the two companies took two years.（A. merger B. merge C. merged D. merging）
7. 哪句适合出现在正式报告中？（A. We got tons of feedback. B. Feedback came in like crazy. C. We received a great deal of feedback. D. We grabbed loads of feedback.）
8. 哪句是合格的学术 hedging？（A. Video games obviously destroy concentration. B. Video games may impair concentration to some extent. C. Video games totally destroy concentration. D. Video games destroyed concentration, no question.）
9. The funding was cut. ___, the project survived.（A. Therefore B. Moreover C. By the way D. Nevertheless）
10. 哪句倒装正确？（A. Hardly had he arrived when the meeting began. B. Hardly he had arrived when the meeting began. C. Hardly did he arrive when the meeting began. D. Hardly he arrived when the meeting began.）

**B2 改写题（10 题）**

11. 用倒装改写：She had hardly sat down when the phone rang.
12. 用倒装改写：We will never forget that lesson.
13. 用分裂句改写（强调 last May）：The regulation took effect last May.
14. 用 so 改写：He can swim, and his daughter can swim too.
15. 用 neither 改写：I don't enjoy horror films. My partner doesn't enjoy them either.
16. 用名词化改写：The government decided to raise taxes, and this angered voters.
17. 用名词化改写：Because the team communicated poorly, the launch failed.
18. 语域升级：We need to get more staff pretty soon.
19. 语域升级：Thanks for telling me about the delay.
20. 加 hedging 改写：This approach always works better.

**B3 中译英（10 题，注意题目要求的结构或语域）**

21. 我从来没见过这么有耐心的老师。（用倒装）
22. 直到考试结束，他才想起自己忘了写名字。（用 not until 倒装）
23. 是不断的练习让她的英语突飞猛进。（用分裂句）
24. 我就是在波士顿第一次尝到正宗的龙虾卷。（用分裂句）
25. 他会说法语，他妹妹也会。（用 so）
26. 公司突然决定裁员，震惊了整个行业。（用名词化）
27. 汇率剧烈波动导致出口下降。（用名词化）
28. 请书面告知我们您能否出席会议。（正式语域）
29. 新政策或许能在一定程度上缓解交通拥堵。（用 hedging）
30. 正式段落翻译（通知信，注意全程正式语域）：
    兹通知：本服务将自下月起终止。感谢各位客户多年来的信任。如需协助，请随时与我们联系。

### Part C · 写作自测（3 任务，不限时，必经批改）

- **任务一（150 词正式邮件）**：给一家酒店的经理写投诉邮件，内容自定（如入住延迟、房间与预订不符）。要求：零缩写、至少 3 个正式词（require / inform / assistance / terminate 类）、至少 1 处被动、礼貌收尾。
- **任务二（60~80 词口语版改写）**：把任务一的投诉内容改写成向朋友吐槽的口头版。要求：正常使用缩写、短语动词、非正式词，语气自然。
- **任务三（180 词观点段）**：就 "Does technology make us lonelier?" 写一段议论文主体段。要求：至少 4 个 hedge（may / tend to / somewhat / arguably 类）、至少 2 个正式连接标记（however / nevertheless / overall 类）、名词化至少出现 1 次。

**Part B · B1 选择题：**

1. **B**。Never before 句首 → 现在完成时倒装 has there been。
2. **C**。Not only 句首 → did he miss。
3. **D**。分裂句中的对比插入语用 not：the intern, not the manager, who...。
4. **A**。上一句肯定 + have，回应用 So have I；B 项 So I have 意为"我确实去过"（确认对方），C 项助动词错，D 项意义相反。
5. **B**。否定句延续用 neither does。
6. **A**。名词化形式 merger（merge 的名词）。
7. **C**。正式报告要 received + a great deal of；tons / crazy / grabbed 全是非正式。
8. **B**。may + to some extent 是标准学术 hedging；A 项 obviously 绝对化，C、D 同病。
9. **D**。前句不利、后句项目活了下来，是转折 nevertheless。
10. **A**。Hardly 句首 → had + 主语 + 过去分词，hardly...when 固定搭配。

13. It was last May that the regulation took effect.

**Part B · B3 中译英（每题 3 个评分点，每点 1 分）：**

21. Never have I met such a patient teacher.（Never 句首倒装；have 提前；such a patient teacher）
22. Not until the exam ended did he remember that he had forgotten to write his name.（Not until + 从句；did + 主语；remember + 过去完成时"忘了写"）
23. It was constant practice that made her English improve rapidly.（It was...that 框架；强调对象 constant practice；improve rapidly 表"突飞猛进"）
24. It was in Boston that I first tasted an authentic lobster roll.（It was + 地点 + that；first tasted；authentic）
25. He speaks French, and so does his younger sister.（and so does；does 单三；语序正确）
26. The company's sudden decision to cut jobs shocked the entire industry.（decision 名词化；to cut jobs 后置修饰；shocked the entire industry）
27. Sharp fluctuations in the exchange rate led to a decline in exports.（fluctuations 名词化；led to / resulted in；a decline in exports）
28. Please inform us in writing whether you will be able to attend the meeting.（inform 正式词；in writing + whether；attend the meeting）
29. The new policy may alleviate traffic congestion to some extent.（may；alleviate；to some extent）
30. We would like to inform you that this service will be terminated as of next month. We are grateful for the trust that our clients have placed in us over the years. Should you require any assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.（inform + 被动 will be terminated；grateful for the trust；Should you require... 倒装条件式 + 全段无缩写）
