为什么外刊和学术报告读起来"密度很高"，而口语读起来"很松"？秘密引擎之一就是名词化：把动作打包成名词。这是 C1 写作的分水岭，学会它，你的邮件、报告、论文会立刻上一个档次。

## 本课目标

- [ ] 我能把常见动词、形容词转成对应的名词（decide → decision，possible → possibility）
- [ ] 我能把口语句子"升级"成名词化书面句
- [ ] 我能说出名词化如何提高信息密度、让文体更正式
- [ ] 我会用"名词化 + 被动"写出报告体句子
- [ ] 我能识别并修正"名词堆砌"式的过度名词化

## 语法讲解

### 一、先弄懂：什么是名词化

名词化（nominalization）：把动词或形容词"打包"成一个名词。类比一下：动词像电影里的动作镜头，名词化就是把某一帧动作定格、装裱，挂到墙上当"展品"用。中文里你早就见过："发展经济"变成"经济发展"，"他们毁掉了城市"变成"城市的毁灭"。

- They destroyed the city. → the **destruction** of the city 城市的毁灭
- We decided to wait. → our **decision** to wait 我们等待的决定
- It is possible that prices will rise. → the **possibility** of price rises 物价上涨的可能性

### 二、动词 → 名词的三条主要路径

英语构词靠词缀（affix，粘在词根前后的"零件"）。动词变名词最常见的三条路径：

| 路径 | 动词 → 名词 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 加 -tion（常有拼写变化） | decide → decision；destroy → destruction；implement → implementation；inform → information | The destruction of the city shocked the world. 城市的毁灭震惊了世界。 |
| 加 -ment | develop → development；agree → agreement；improve → improvement；establish → establishment | The improvement in sales surprised everyone. 销售的改善让所有人吃惊。 |
| 不规则变化 | grow → growth；arrive → arrival；discover → discovery；fail → failure；know → knowledge | After his arrival, the meeting began. 他到了之后，会议开始了。 |

坏消息是：哪类动词配哪条路径没有万能公式，要按"词族"成套地记。好消息是：高频的那几十个，写几次就熟了。

### 三、形容词 → 名词

| 形容词 → 名词 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| possible → possibility | There is a strong possibility of rain this evening. |
| important → importance | The importance of sleep is often underestimated. 睡眠的重要性常被低估。 |
| aware → awareness | The campaign raised public awareness of food waste. |
| efficient → efficiency | Efficiency is the company's top priority. |
| accurate → accuracy | We cannot guarantee the accuracy of the data. 我们无法保证数据的准确性。 |

### 四、为什么书面语偏爱名词化：信息密度

对比同一件事的两种说法：

- 口语版：We analyzed the data, and this showed that sales were falling, which meant that we had to change our strategy.
- 书面版：Our **analysis** of the data revealed a **decline** in sales, prompting a **change** of strategy.

书面版一句装下更多信息：主语从"人"换成了"事"，视角更客观，节奏更紧。这就是信息密度（information density）的力量，也是学术写作、商业报告、新闻分析共同的腔调。

### 五、名词化 + 被动 = 正式文体黄金组合

报告体里最常见的句型：**The + 名词化 + of + 宾语 + was + 过去分词**。

- The policy was implemented in May. → 更正式：The **implementation** of the policy was **completed** in May. 政策的实施于五月完成。
- An **agreement** was **reached** after three rounds of talks. 三轮谈判后达成了一项协议。
- Upon **arrival**, guests were **given** a welcome package. 抵达后，宾客们收到了欢迎礼包。

为什么是黄金组合：名词化提供"事"作主角，被动隐去"人"，两者叠加，句子立刻公事公办、稳重大气。

### 六、过度名词化的坑

名词化是引擎，不是越多越好。看这个反面教材：

- 人话版：The team implemented the policy effectively. 团队高效地落实了政策。
- 堆砌版：The effective **implementation** of the policy was achieved by the team. （还能忍）
- 灾难版：An assessment of the implementation of the improvement of the development of the system was conducted. （连环 of，读者已经窒息）

判断标准：一句话里出现三个以上"名词 + of + 名词 + of + 名词"，就该拆了。平衡原则：核心信息用名词化打包，动作和因果关系留给动词。

### 七、口语 → 书面"升级"三步法

第一步，找出核心动作；第二步，把它名词化；第三步，挂进正式句型。

- 原句：They arrived late, so we had to change the plan. → 升级：Their late **arrival** forced a **change** of plans.
- 原句：We analyzed the data and found a clear trend. → 升级：Our **analysis** of the data revealed a clear trend.

写作时先随手套用三步法，改稿时再用第六节的标准检查有没有堆过头。

## 中文母语者常见错误

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| The analyze of the data shows a trend. | The analysis of the data shows a trend. | analyze 是动词，作名词要换成名词形式 analysis |
| the destroy of the city | the destruction of the city | destroy 对应的名词是 destruction，不是在动词后面加 of 就行 |
| the important of education | the importance of education | 形容词 important 对应的名词是 importance |
| We must ensure the implement of the plan. | ...the implementation of the plan. | implement 作名词意思是"工具"，"实施"的名词是 implementation |
| Upon arrive at the hotel, ... | Upon arrival at the hotel, ... | arrive 是动词；固定搭配是 upon arrival |
| The discussion of the improvement of the development of the system was conducted. | We discussed how to improve and develop the system. | 连环 of 名词堆砌让人窒息；正式不等于把每个动词都变成名词 |

## 练习

**一、写出下列单词的名词形式**

1. destroy → ______
2. arrive → ______
3. possible → ______
4. important → ______
5. discover → ______
6. agree → ______

**二、名词化升级：把口语句子改写成书面句**

7. They analyzed the data and found a clear trend.
8. The company implemented the new policy last month.
9. We decided to postpone the launch because costs had risen.
10. The two sides finally agreed after long talks.

**三、判断与改错**

11. 下面哪句更正式？用一个词说明原因。
    A. The team analyzed the results and found a trend.
    B. The team's analysis of the results revealed a trend.
12. 改错：The possible of success cannot be ruled out.
13. 拆掉名词堆砌（保持原意，把动词还回来）：The implementation of the reduction of the cost of the system was achieved by the team.

**四、翻译（要求使用名词化）**

14. 新地铁线路的建成将大大缩短通勤时间。
15. 经过三轮谈判，双方终于达成协议。

## 练习答案

**一、名词形式**

1. destruction
2. arrival
3. possibility
4. importance
5. discovery
6. agreement

**二、名词化升级（示例答案，写法可略有出入，结构到位即可）**

7. The analysis of the data revealed a clear trend.
8. The implementation of the new policy was completed last month.
9. Our decision to postpone the launch was driven by rising costs.
10. An agreement was finally reached between the two sides after long talks.

**三、判断与改错**

11. B 更正式。原因：nominalization（名词化）。analysis 把动作打包成名词，信息密度更高，更贴近报告体。
12. The possibility of success cannot be ruled out.（possible 是形容词，名词是 possibility）
13. 示例：The team reduced the cost of the system. 或 The team managed to cut system costs.（连环 of 拆回动词句，意思不变，读起来轻松）

**四、翻译**

14. The completion of the new subway line will greatly reduce commuting time.
15. After three rounds of talks, an agreement was finally reached between the two sides.
