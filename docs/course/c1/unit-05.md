> **预计 6~8 小时，建议拆 3~4 天** | 前置：[第 4 课 · 名词化：正式文体的引擎](./unit-04.md) | 难度：C1

前四课你攒了一整套高级句式：倒装、分裂句、省略替代、名词化。这一课回答一个更难的问题：**什么时候用它们，什么时候千万别用。** 语域（register）指的就是"根据场合选择语言风格"的能力。它是中国学习者最缺的一课：语法全对，场合全错。

## 本课目标

- [ ] 能判断任意一段英文属于正式、中性还是非正式，并说出至少两条依据
- [ ] 掌握 10 组以上正式/非正式词汇替换，能双向改写
- [ ] 知道缩写形式（I'm / don't）能出现在哪里、绝不能出现在哪里
- [ ] 能把同一段话在正式、中性、非正式三档之间自由切换
- [ ] 写作和口语中语域全程一致，不再"上半身西装、下半身拖鞋"

## 语法讲解

### 一、什么是语域：同一件衣服，三种场合

语域就是语言的"着装"。穿西装参加婚礼没问题，穿去海滩烧烤就很怪。英语分三档：

| 档位 | 典型场合 | 典型特征 | 例句（同一个意思） |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 正式 formal | 论文、合同、给上级或客户的邮件、官方公告 | 不缩写、被动、名词化、拉丁词源的正式词 | I regret to inform you that the event has been canceled. |
| 中性 neutral | 工作聊天、一般邮件、口语考试 | 少量缩写、常用词、礼貌但直接 | I'm sorry to say the event has been called off. |
| 非正式 informal | 朋友短信、家人闲聊、网络社交 | 满手缩写、短语动词、俚语、语气词 | Bad news, the thing's off. Sorry! |

三句话意思完全一样，差别全在"穿什么"。判断语域看四个信号：**词汇正式度、有没有缩写、句法复杂度、语气词**。

### 二、正式 vs 非正式词汇对照表

英语词汇有个有趣的规律：正式词多是拉丁语、法语词源（长词），非正式词多是日耳曼词源（短词、短语动词）。同一件事，两套说法：

| 正式 | 中性/非正式 | 例句对照 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| purchase | buy / get | We purchased the equipment. / We got the equipment. |
| commence | start / begin | The meeting commenced at nine. / The meeting started at nine. |
| terminate | end / stop | They terminated the contract. / They ended the contract. |
| require | need | Applicants are required to... / Applicants need to... |
| assistance | help | Do you require assistance? / Do you need help? |
| inform | tell | Please inform us of any changes. / Please tell us if anything changes. |
| demonstrate | show | The data demonstrates... / The data shows... |
| sufficient | enough | Is one hour sufficient? / Is one hour enough? |
| prior to | before | Prior to departure... / Before we leave... |
| regarding | about | regarding your request / about your request |

记忆策略：右列是"日常款"，人人都会；**要专门记的是左列**，因为左列只在正式文体出现，见到它们的场合本身就能提醒你它们是正式词。反过来，写论文、写正式邮件时，主动把右列换成左列。

### 三、缩写形式的分布

缩写形式（contractions）指 I'm、don't、can't、it's、won't、I'd 这类。规则很简单：

| 场合 | 能用缩写吗 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 朋友短信、口语 | 随便用，不用反而生硬 | I don't think it's a big deal. |
| 中性工作邮件 | 可以少量用 | I'm attaching the file. Could you take a look? |
| 学术论文、正式邮件、合同 | 绝不缩写，写完整形式 | I do not think it is a significant issue. |

两点提醒：

- 完整形式自带"庄重感"，所以正式文体里 I am writing to inform you... 一个缩写都不能有。
- **cannot 是一个词**，正式文体不写 can not。这和中文直觉不同，很多 B2 学习者还在写成两个词。

对比感受一下：

- 非正式：We can't do it, and I'm sorry it's taken so long.
- 正式：We are unable to complete the task, and I apologize for the delay.（注意 not only 展开了缩写，还把 can't 升级成了 are unable to，这是缩写+词汇的双重升级）

### 四、正式文体特征回顾：把前四课串起来

到这里，C1 的拼图完整了。正式文体不是一个特征，而是**一组特征同时在场**：

| 特征 | 来源 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 不缩写 | 本课 | We are unable to... |
| 正式词汇 | 本课 | require / inform / sufficient |
| 被动语态 | B2 已学 | The samples were analyzed twice. |
| 名词化 | [第 4 课](./unit-04.md) | decide → make a decision / the decision |
| 倒装强调 | [第 1 课](./unit-01.md) | Not only did sales rise, but morale improved as well. |
| 分裂句聚焦 | [第 2 课](./unit-02.md) | It was the reform, not luck, that saved the firm. |

看一条"升级流水线"，同一个意思逐步正式化：

- 原句（非正式）：The boss said no, so we didn't buy the new system.
- 第一步，换正式词：The manager rejected the proposal, so we did not purchase the new system.
- 第二步，名词化：The manager's rejection of the proposal prevented the purchase of the new system.
- 第三步，被动+名词化收尾：The purchase of the new system was prevented by the manager's rejection of the proposal.

每升一步，句子更"端着"，也更客观冷峻。**升级到哪一档，取决于场合，不取决于你的心情。**

### 五、同一段话三档改写示范

场景：你要请假一天去看病，分别写给部门总监（邮件）、直属同事（微信）、老朋友（解释为什么爽约）。

**正式（给总监的邮件）：**

> Subject: Request for One Day of Leave
>
> Dear Ms. Carter,
>
> I am writing to request one day of leave on Friday, as I have a medical appointment that cannot be rescheduled. All pending tasks will be completed prior to my absence, and I have asked Tom to cover the client meeting if required. Please let me know if you need any further information.
>
> Kind regards,
> Wei Zhang

**中性（给同事的微信/Slack）：**

> Hi Sarah, I need to take Friday off for a doctor's appointment. I've finished the report, and Tom has agreed to cover the client meeting. Is that okay with you? Thanks!

**非正式（给老朋友）：**

> Hey! Can't do Friday after all, I've got a hospital thing. So sorry, rain check? I'll buy you coffee to make up for it.

逐项对比：称呼（Dear Ms. Carter / Hi Sarah / Hey）、请假说法（request one day of leave / take Friday off / can't do Friday）、词汇正式度（prior to / before... / 省略）、缩写（零 / I've / Can't, I'll）、结尾（Kind regards / Thanks! / 卖萌补偿）。**意思一样，衣服完全不同。**

### 六、场合选词决策表

| 场合 | 语域档 | 放心用 | 千万别用 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 学术论文、报告 | 正式 | 被动、名词化、hedging（下一课）、It could be argued that... | 缩写、get / buy、a lot of、kind of、very 多用 rather / considerably |
| 给上级、客户的邮件 | 正式偏中性 | I am writing to... / I would appreciate... / 完整形式 | gonna、cuz、ASAP 缩写、表情包 |
| 同事日常沟通 | 中性 | 普通缩写、短语动词、直接礼貌的请求 | 演讲腔、hereby、notwithstanding |
| 朋友聊天、短信 | 非正式 | 缩写满手、get、语气词、梗 | furthermore、I would like to inquire、purchase |
| 雅思等口语考试 | 中性为主，可稍非正式 | Well, actually, to be honest（下一课细讲） | 背出来的书面腔：hereby、nevertheless 满天飞 |

一张速记：**写下去的东西越"官方"，语言越端着；说出口的话越"私人"，语言越放松。拿不准时选中性档，两头不出错。**

## 中文母语者常见错误

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 口语考试说 I hereby declare that reading is important. | I'd say reading is really important. | hereby 只出现在法律文书和官方声明里，口语用它等于穿着法袍去烧烤，考官瞬间出戏 |
| 给朋友发微信 Furthermore, I would like to inquire about tomorrow's plan. | Also, what's the plan for tomorrow? | furthermore 是学术连接词，朋友聊天用它，像用文言文问朋友吃了没 |
| 论文里写 We don't think it's a big deal. | This issue is not considered significant. | 论文里缩写和口语词双双违禁；big deal 换 significant，don't think 换被动 |
| 正式邮件写 I'm writing cuz I need to get a refund. | I am writing to request a refund. | cuz 和 get 一出现，整封邮件的专业感归零；一个 cuz 能毁掉一页礼貌用语 |
| 混搭：Dear Sir, I am writing to inform you that I can't make it, sorry! | Dear Sir, I am writing to inform you that I am unable to attend. | 语域要全程一致。开头是西装，结尾突然拖鞋，比从头到尾拖鞋更刺眼 |
| get 一词走天下：I got a car, got a loan, got permission. | 正式场合：purchased a car, obtained a loan, secured permission | 中文"搞到 / 弄到"一个词万能，英文正式文体必须按对象换词：obtain、secure、receive |

## 练习

**Part A：语域判断。** 判断下列句子是正式（F）、中性（N）还是非正式（I），并写出一条依据。

1. I am writing to inquire about the position advertised on your website.
2. Hey, just checking if you're still coming tonight.
3. The experiment was conducted over a three-week period.
4. We're gonna grab some food. You in?
5. Could you send me the file when you get a chance?

**Part B：非正式 → 正式。** 用本课词汇把下列句子改写成正式文体。

6. We need to get more info about the new rule.
7. They ended the contract before it even started.
8. Tell us about any changes to your plans.
9. The report shows that sales went up a lot last year.
10. I want help with my visa application.

**Part C：正式 → 非正式。** 把下列句子改写成朋友之间的口头/短信版。

11. I would be grateful if you could inform me of your availability.
12. We regret to inform you that your application was unsuccessful.
13. The meeting has been postponed until further notice.
14. Should you require further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact me.

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**Part A：**

1. **F**。完整形式（I am）、正式词 inquire、书面句型 I am writing to...。
2. **I**。称呼 Hey、缩写 you're、口语短语 just checking。
3. **F**。被动语态 was conducted，无缩写，术语化表达。
4. **I**。gonna、grab some food、省略句 You in?。
5. **N**。礼貌但直接，常用词，无生僻正式词也无俚语，典型职场中性请求。

**Part B（参考答案，可有不同写法）：**

6. We require further information regarding the new policy.
7. The contract was terminated prior to its commencement.
8. Please inform us of any changes to your plans.（或 Please notify us of...）
9. The report demonstrates that sales increased substantially last year.
10. I would like assistance with my visa application.（或 I require assistance with...）

**Part C（参考答案）：**

11. Just let me know when you're free.
12. Sorry, you didn't get it this time.
13. The meeting's on hold for now. / They've put the meeting off for now.
14. Just give me a shout if you need anything.

判分小技巧：Part B 看三件事，缩写消失了吗？正式词换上了吗？短语动词收起来了吗？三条全中才算过关。
