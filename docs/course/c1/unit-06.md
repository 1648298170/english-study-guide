中文有"可能""大概""一般来说""说实话""话说回来"，英语有完全对应的一套机关：hedging（模糊限制语）和 discourse markers（话语标记）。它们是 C1 写作和口语里"分寸感"的来源：**不把话说死，恰恰是严谨；会用路标词，才像真的会聊天。**

## 本课目标

- [ ] 理解学术写作为什么必须 hedging，能用它避免绝对化表述
- [ ] 掌握 5 类高频 hedge（seem / tend to / might / somewhat / arguably 等）并能改写绝对句
- [ ] 能按功能（开启话轮、转换话题、表示态度、软化反对）分类使用话语标记
- [ ] 听力中能把话语标记当"路标"预判走向，精听时一听一个准
- [ ] 讨论中能用 That said...、To some extent... 委婉表达不同意见，不再硬邦邦地 but

## 语法讲解

### 一、什么是模糊限制语（hedging）

hedge 的本义是"树篱"，引申为"留余地"。模糊限制语就是给断言加缓冲的语言装置。先看一组对比：

- 不加 hedge：Social media destroys teenagers' attention spans.（把话说死：全部、必然、永远）
- 加 hedge：Social media **may** damage teenagers' attention spans **to some extent**.（可能、某种程度上）

两句意思几乎一样，学术分量天差地别。**为什么学术写作要 hedging？因为研究者拿的是样本，不是全宇宙。** 你调查了 500 名青少年，只能说"数据提示可能存在影响"，说"社媒摧毁注意力"是在替全人类发言，审稿人第一个不答应。不把话说死不是心虚，是把结论限定在证据范围内，这正是严谨的定义。

中文同理：论文写"结果表明……可能存在正相关"，没人写"结果表明……绝对正相关"。

### 二、五类核心 hedge 用法

按"缓冲什么"分五类，每类记两个就够用：

**1. 弱化确定性（"我不打包票"）：It seems / appears that...、might / may、presumably**

- 天气预报的措辞：It appears that the storm will bypass the city.（看起来风暴将绕过这座城市。）
- She might change her mind before the deadline.（截止日前她可能会改变主意。）
- 比较：He's late again. → He's presumably stuck in traffic.（直接断言迟到 vs 给出体面的推测）

**2. 弱化频率（"多数如此，不保证全部"）：tend to、generally speaking**

- Generally speaking, remote workers tend to report higher job satisfaction.（总体而言，远程工作者往往报告更高的工作满意度。）
- 对比感：Cats hate water.（所有猫？）→ Cats tend to dislike water.（多数猫，留了余地）

**3. 弱化程度（"有点，不是极端"）：somewhat、relatively、to some extent**

- The new policy is somewhat more flexible than the old one.（新政策比旧政策灵活一点。）
- To some extent, the criticism is fair.（在某种程度上，这个批评是公允的。）
- 注意 somewhat 比 a little 更书面，是正式文体的"有点"。

**4. 归属他人（"话是别人说的"）：It is suggested / argued / reported that...**

- It is suggested that the two events are related.（有观点认为这两件事相关。）
- It has been argued that remote work erodes company culture.（有人论证过远程工作侵蚀企业文化。）
- 观点甩给"有人"，你自己全身而退，学术写作最爱这一招。

**5. 保留立场（"可以争论"）：arguably**

- This is arguably the most influential study in the field.（这恐怕是该领域最有影响的研究。）
- arguably 的妙处：立场基本是你的，但承认"可以争论"，进可攻退可守。

| 类别 | 说什么 | 随身弹药 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 确定性 | 我不打包票 | may / might、It seems that...、presumably |
| 频率 | 多数如此 | tend to、generally speaking |
| 程度 | 有点而已 | somewhat、relatively、to some extent |
| 归属 | 有人说 | It is suggested that...、It has been argued that... |
| 保留 | 可以争论 | arguably |

**平衡警告**：hedge 不是越多越好。一句里塞三个缓冲（It might perhaps somewhat suggest...）就变成不敢说话了。**一句一个 hedge，最多两个**，是学术写作的常规剂量。

### 三、话语标记（discourse markers）：按功能分类

话语标记是组织话语的路标词：它们基本不改变句子的核心意思，却告诉听者"接下来是什么路"。口语里它们还有个隐藏功能：**争取思考时间**，比嗯嗯啊啊高级得多。

按四大功能分类：

**1. 开启话轮（"我要开始说了"）：Well、So、Actually**

- Well, I think it's a bit more complicated than that.（嗯，我觉得这事比那复杂一点。）
- So, where were we?（那，我们刚才说到哪了？）
- Actually, I have a slightly different take.（其实我的看法稍有不同。）

**2. 转换话题（"换个事说"）：By the way、Incidentally、That reminds me...**

- By the way, did anyone water the plants?（对了，有人浇花了吗？）
- Incidentally, the CEO mentioned your name in the meeting.（顺带一提，会上 CEO 提到了你的名字。）
- That reminds me, I still owe you lunch.（这倒提醒我了，我还欠你一顿午饭。）

**3. 表示态度（"先亮个牌"）：To be honest、Frankly、Surprisingly**

- To be honest, I didn't expect it to work.（说实话，我没想到它会成。）
- Frankly, the whole design is outdated.（坦白讲，整个设计都过时了。）
- Surprisingly, no one objected.（出人意料，没人反对。）

**4. 软化反对（"我不同意，但我体面"）：Well、Mind you、To some extent... but、Having said that**

- That's a fair point. Having said that, the data tells a different story.（有道理。话虽如此，数据讲的是另一个故事。）
- The plan sounds good. Mind you, we've heard similar promises before.（计划不错。不过话说回来，类似的话我们听过。）
- To some extent, yes, but the costs keep rising.（某种程度上是的，但成本一直在涨。）

**软反对公式**：先让一步（That's a fair point / To some extent, yes）+ 转折标记（Having said that / Mind you）+ 你的立场。比一个光秃秃的 but 有教养得多，口语考试里极加分。

### 四、正式 vs 非正式标记对照

话语标记也分语域，这是它与上一课的连接点：

| 功能 | 非正式/口语 | 正式/书面 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 转折 | but、still、mind you | however、nevertheless、that said |
| 顺带补充 | by the way、plus | incidentally、furthermore、moreover |
| 态度 | to be honest、honestly | frankly、it must be admitted |
| 归因 | I heard、apparently | it is reported that... |
| 总结 | so、basically | in short、overall、to sum up |

- 短信：The plan's risky. **Plus**, it's expensive.
- 报告：The plan is risky; **furthermore**, it is expensive.

一个意思，两副行头，选错档就撞上一课讲的错误。

### 五、四技能中的识别与运用

| 技能 | 作用 | 训练方法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 听 | 标记是路标：听到 Having said that 就知道要转折，听到 by the way 知道话题要岔开 | 精听 The Daily，每听到一个标记暂停跟读，一集集 5 个以上 |
| 说 | 标记买思考时间，流利度直线上升；软反对公式让讨论不伤人 | 录音回答争议问题，规定自己每段用一个不同标记 |
| 读 | 段首标记预告段落功能（However = 反方，Moreover = 加码） | 读外刊时把段首标记圈出来，观察作者怎么布防 |
| 写 | 学术 essay 的骨架就是正式标记：however、nevertheless、overall | 写完自查：段落之间有没有"路标"？全是 and / but 的文章像没有路牌的高速公路 |

## 中文母语者常见错误

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I very agree with you. | To a large extent, I agree. / I'd largely agree. | very 不能修饰动词 agree；要表达"非常同意"且留分寸，英语用程度状语组 |
| 论文写 I feel like maybe it's kind of bad for the economy. | This may have somewhat negative effects on the economy. | hedge 的方向对了，但 feel like / kind of 是口语档，学术写作要用 may / somewhat |
| Social media definitely destroys concentration.（议论文开头） | Social media may impair concentration, particularly among younger users. | definitely + destroys 双重绝对化，议论文里等于邀请对手一枪毙掉你 |
| Well, actually, to be honest, I mean, my answer is... | Well, to be honest, I think... | 标记堆三四个反而显犹豫、占时间；一到两个足够，它们是路标不是装饰 |
| As we all know, money is important.（论文开头） | It is widely acknowledged that money matters.（或干脆删掉） | as we all know 是中文"众所周知"的直译，预设读者全是哑巴，学术写作大忌 |
| 论文转折处用 By the way, the method has limits. | However, the method has limitations. | by the way 是聊天岔话题用的，学术转折要用 however / nevertheless |

## 练习

**Part A：选择填空。** 从括号中选一个最合适的词。

1. The results ______ that the trend is slowing.（prove / suggest / guarantee / confirm）
2. ______, the policy has been effective, though problems remain.（Generally speaking / Without doubt / Absolutely / Obviously）
3. It is ______ that the author intended the ending as irony.（suggested / demanded / proved / ordered）
4. The new system is ______ more efficient than the old one.（roughly / relatively / totally / completely）
5. ______, I think we are overthinking this.（To be honest / By the way / Incidentally / Moreover）

**Part B：加 hedge 改写。** 给下列绝对化断言加上合适的 hedge，保持原意方向。

6. Everyone hates Monday mornings.
7. This drug cures insomnia.
8. Remote work is the future.
9. Electric cars are cleaner than petrol cars.

**Part C：话语标记运用。**

10. 填入软化反对的标记：You make a fair point. ______, the data says otherwise.
11. 正式改写（by the way 换正式版）：The plan is risky. By the way, it is also expensive.
12. 在对话中插入转换话题标记：Great meeting you! ______, did you hear about the merger?
13. 用 relatively 改写：The exam was very difficult.
14. 补全标记：______, the deadline hasn't changed.（据我所知）

## 练习答案

<details><summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**Part A：**

1. **suggest**。数据只能"提示"，prove / confirm 太绝对，guarantee 更是离谱。
2. **Generally speaking**。后面有 though problems remain，前面必须是留余地的总述；其余三项全是绝对化。
3. **suggested**。讽刺意图只能"有人认为"，不能被 demand / prove / order。
4. **relatively**。相对比较级 more efficient 前配 relatively；roughly 管数字，totally / completely 与 more 矛盾。
5. **To be honest**。表达态度，引出个人真实看法；其余三项功能不对。

**Part B（参考答案）：**

6. Most people seem to dislike Monday mornings.（或 Generally speaking, people tend to dread Monday mornings.）
7. This drug appears to relieve some symptoms of insomnia.
8. Remote work may well become the norm in many industries.（或 It could be argued that remote work is the future of many industries.）
9. Electric cars tend to be somewhat cleaner than petrol cars.（至少 in terms of tailpipe emissions 可作补充）

**Part C：**

10. Having said that（或 That said / Mind you）。先让步再转折的标准软反对。
11. The plan is risky; incidentally / furthermore, it is expensive.（取"顺带补充"义时 furthermore 最自然）
12. By the way（或 Oh, by the way）。
13. The exam was relatively difficult.（也可 The exam was somewhat difficult，但题干要求用 relatively）
14. As far as I know。

自测剂量：Part B 若写出了"一句双 hedge"以上，检查是不是过量；Part C 若两题用了同一个标记，换一个再写一遍。
