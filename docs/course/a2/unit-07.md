## 语法讲解

### 第一步：什么是比较级和最高级

先解释术语。**比较级**用来比较两个东西，相当于中文的"更"；**最高级**用来在三个及以上里挑出"之最"，相当于中文的"最"。

- My brother is tall. 我哥很高。（原级：只做陈述）
- My brother is taller than me. 我哥比我更高。（比较级：两人相比）
- My brother is the tallest in our family. 我哥是我们家最高的。（最高级：全家范围）

| 级别 | 用途 | 形式 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 原级 | 陈述性质 | tall |
| 比较级 | 两者比较，常配 than | taller (than) |
| 最高级 | 三者以上的之最，常配 the | the tallest (in/of) |

### 第二步：单音节词直接加 -er / -est

**音节**是单词里含一个元音的发音单位：tall 一个音节，tall-er 两个音节。单音节形容词直接在词尾加 -er / -est。

- tall → taller → the tallest
- cheap → cheaper → the cheapest
- fast → faster → the fastest
- cold → colder → the coldest

例句：

- The subway is faster than the bus. 地铁比公交快。
- This phone is cheaper than that one. 这部手机比那部便宜。

### 第三步：两个变形细节：双写与变 y

**辅音双写**：单音节词、结尾是"辅音 + 元音 + 辅音"的，双写末尾辅音再加 -er / -est。

- big → bigger → the biggest（The blue box is bigger than the red one. 蓝箱子比红箱子大。）
- hot → hotter → the hottest（Summer is the hottest season here. 夏天是这里最热的季节。）

**辅音字母 + y 结尾**：先把 y 变成 i，再加 -er / -est。

- easy → easier → the easiest（This question is easier than that one. 这道题比那道容易。）
- busy → busier → the busiest（Friday is the busiest day of my week. 周五是我一周最忙的一天。）
- heavy → heavier → the heaviest

五条规则一张表：

| 形容词类型 | 变化规则 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 单音节 | 直接 + er / est | tall → taller → tallest |
| 单音节，辅元辅结尾 | 双写末尾辅音 + er / est | big → bigger → biggest；hot → hotter → hottest |
| 辅音 + y 结尾 | y 变 i + er / est | easy → easier → easiest；busy → busier → busiest |
| 多音节（两个音节及以上） | 前面加 more / the most | interesting → more interesting → the most interesting |
| 不规则 | 单独记 | good/well → better → best；bad/badly → worse → worst；far → further → furthest |

### 第四步：多音节词用 more / most

两个及以上音节的形容词不再动词尾，在前面加 more / the most。

- interesting → more interesting → the most interesting
- expensive → more expensive → the most expensive
- beautiful → more beautiful → the most beautiful
- popular → more popular → the most popular

例句：

- This book is more interesting than that one. 这本书比那本有趣。
- That was the most boring movie of the year. 那是今年最无聊的电影。
- Football is the most popular sport in the world. 足球是世界上最受欢迎的运动。

### 第五步：不规则变化

只有一小批高频词不规则，但个个常用，必须单独记。

| 原级 | 比较级 | 最高级 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| good / well 好 | better | best |
| bad / badly 坏 | worse | worst |
| far 远 | further / farther | furthest / farthest |

例句：

- Her English is better than mine. 她的英语比我的好。
- That's the best cake in this shop! 那是这个店里最好吃的蛋糕。
- Yesterday was the worst day of this month. 昨天是这个月最糟的一天。
- The airport is farther than the bank. 机场比银行远。（说实际距离时 farther 和 further 都可以，美式口语里 further 更常见）

### 第六步：than 与 as ... as

比较级后面接 **than**（比）：

- She is taller than her sister. 她比她姐姐高。
- I get up earlier than you. 我比你起得早。
- 口语里 than 后面常用人称代词宾格：than me / than him / than her

**as + 原级 + as** 表示"和……一样"；否定 not as ... as 表示"不如"：

- Tom is as tall as his father. 汤姆和他爸爸一样高。
- My room is as big as yours. 我的房间和你的一样大。
- The bus is not as fast as the subway. 公交不如地铁快。

注意：as ... as 中间永远用原级，不能说 as taller as。

### 第七步：最高级的范围：in 和 of

最高级要说清"在哪个范围里最"。**in + 大地方或团体**，**of + 几个并列对象**：

- He is the tallest boy in our class. 他是我们班最高的男孩。
- This is the most famous street in the city. 这是城里最有名的街。
- She is the youngest of the three children. 她是三个孩子里最小的。
- The yellow bag is the cheapest of the three. 黄色包是三个里最便宜的。

一个易错点：比较级用于两者，最高级用于三者及以上。只有两个对象时不说最高级：She is the taller of the two sisters.（两姐妹中她更高。）

## 中文母语者常见错误

| ❌ 错误说法 | ✅ 正确说法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| She is more taller than me. | She is taller than me. | taller 已是比较级，再加 more 属于重复比较 |
| This book is interestinger. | This book is more interesting. | 多音节词不加 -er，用 more |
| My bag is heavyer than yours. | My bag is heavier than yours. | 辅音 + y 结尾先变 y 为 i：heavier |
| He runs more fast than me. | He runs faster than me. | fast 是单音节，直接加 -er |
| Tom is tallest than me. | Tom is taller than me. | 比较级才配 than，最高级不配 |
| This is the most cheapest phone here. | This is the cheapest phone here. | cheapest 已是最高级，most 重复 |
| Lucy is as taller as Lily. | Lucy is as tall as Lily. | as ... as 中间用原级 |

### B 组：单项选择

1. This bag is ___ than mine.（A. heavy B. heavier C. the heaviest）
2. Tom is the ___ student in his class.（A. tall B. taller C. tallest）
3. This movie is ___ than the book.（A. more interesting B. interestinger C. most interesting）
