## 语法讲解

### 第一步：名词分两家：可数与不可数

先解释两个术语。**可数名词**（countable noun）是能一个一个数出来的东西：前面能加 a/an，也有复数形式。**不可数名词**（uncountable noun）是没法数"几个"的东西：通常是一整团、一种液体或一个抽象概念，不能加 a/an，也没有复数形式。

- I have an apple. / I have two apples. 我有一个苹果。/ 我有两个苹果。（苹果能一个一个数：可数）
- I drink water every day. 我每天喝水。（水只能论"多少"，论不了"几个"：不可数）
- ✗ a water / ✗ two waters：没有这种说法，要说 a glass of water（一杯水）
- The news is good this morning. 今早的消息不错。（news 长得像复数，其实不可数，动词用单数）

| 类型 | 能否加 a/an | 有无复数 | 本课生词里的代表 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 可数名词 | 能 | 有（apples, eggs） | apple, egg, vegetable, bottle, piece, kilo |
| 不可数名词 | 不能 | 没有 | water, bread, rice, milk, meat, sugar, coffee, juice, cheese, money, information, news |

记忆窍门：液体（water、milk、juice、coffee）、颗粒与糊状物（rice、bread、meat）、抽象概念（money、information、news）大概率不可数。中文说"一条新闻、两个信息"，不代表英语能加 a 和复数，这是中文母语者最大的坑。

### 第二步：much 和 many：论"多"

- **many + 可数名词复数**
  - Do you have many friends here? 你在这里朋友多吗？
  - There aren't many eggs in the fridge. 冰箱里鸡蛋不多了。
- **much + 不可数名词**
  - I don't have much time today. 我今天时间不多。
  - How much money do you need? 你需要多少钱？
- much 和 many 最常出现在否定句和疑问句里。肯定句里要表达"很多"，更自然的是 a lot of（两种名词都能搭配）：
  - We have a lot of rice at home. 我们家大米很多。

| 搭配 | 名词类型 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| many | 可数复数 | many apples, many friends |
| much | 不可数 | much water, much money |
| a lot of | 都可以（多用于肯定句） | a lot of eggs / a lot of water |

### 第三步：some 和 any：论"有一些"

- **some 用于肯定句**
  - I have some questions. 我有几个问题。
  - There is some milk in the glass. 杯子里有一些牛奶。
- **any 用于否定句和一般疑问句**
  - I don't have any questions. 我没有任何问题。
  - Is there any milk in the glass? 杯子里有牛奶吗？
- **例外要记住：主动提供或请求时，疑问句用 some**（因为你心里预期答案是"要"）
  - Would you like some tea? 要来点茶吗？
  - Can I have some water, please? 麻烦给我来点水，好吗？

| 句型 | 用 some 还是 any |
| --- | --- |
| 肯定句 | some |
| 否定句 / 一般疑问句 | any |
| 提供和请求的问句（Would you like... / Can I have...） | some |

### 第四步：a little 和 a few：论"一点点"

- **a little + 不可数名词**（一点点）
  - I put a little sugar in my coffee. 我在咖啡里放了一点糖。
  - We still have a little time. 我们还有一点时间。
- **a few + 可数名词复数**（几个）
  - I ate a few eggs this morning. 我早上吃了几个鸡蛋。
  - She made a few sandwiches for the trip. 她为旅行做了几个三明治。

| 量词 | 名词类型 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a little | 不可数 | a little water, a little money |
| a few | 可数复数 | a few apples, a few friends |

### 第五步：How much / How many：问数量与价格

- **How many + 可数名词复数**（问"几个"）
  - How many apples do we need? 我们需要几个苹果？
  - How many people are coming for dinner? 有几个人来吃晚饭？
- **How much + 不可数名词**（问"多少"）
  - How much rice do you want? 你要多少米饭？
  - How much water should I drink every day? 我每天该喝多少水？
- **How much 还能问价格**（钱也是不可数的）
  - How much is this bag? 这个包多少钱？
  - How much are the eggs? 鸡蛋怎么卖？（主语复数，用 are）

### 第六步：量词短语：给不可数名词配"量杯"

不可数名词自己不能数，但生活里必须数，怎么办？借一个"容器"或"单位"：数词 + 量词 + of + 名词。

- a cup of coffee 一杯咖啡 → two cups of coffee 两杯咖啡
- a glass of milk 一杯牛奶
- a bottle of water 一瓶水 → three bottles of water 三瓶水
- a piece of bread 一片面包 → two pieces of bread 两片面包
- a kilo of rice 一公斤大米

注意：数字大于 1 时，变复数的是量词，of 后面的名词永远不变：two bottles of water（✗ two bottles of waters）。

| 量词 | 中文 | 常见组合 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a cup of | 一杯 | a cup of tea / coffee |
| a glass of | 一杯（玻璃杯） | a glass of water / milk / juice |
| a bottle of | 一瓶 | a bottle of water / juice |
| a piece of | 一片，一块 | a piece of bread / cheese |
| a kilo of | 一公斤 | a kilo of rice / meat |

## 中文母语者常见错误

| ❌ 错误说法 | ✅ 正确说法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I have many informations. | I have a lot of information. | information 不可数，永远没有复数 |
| I need a money. | I need some money. | money 不可数，不能加 a；表"一些"用 some |
| There are many water in the bottle. | There is a lot of water in the bottle. | water 不可数，there be 用单数 is；"很多"用 a lot of / much |
| How many rice do you want? | How much rice do you want? | rice 不可数，用 How much |
| I want to buy two breads. | I want to buy two pieces of bread. | bread 不可数，计数要借量词 a piece of |
| The news are good today. | The news is good today. | news 形似复数、实为不可数，动词用单数 |

## 练习

### A 组：判断可数（C）还是不可数（U）

写出每个词是 C（可数）还是 U（不可数）：

1. apple
2. water
3. information
4. egg
5. money
6. bread

### B 组：填 some 或 any

1. I have ___ apples in my bag.
2. Do you have ___ milk?
3. There isn't ___ bread at home.
4. Would you like ___ tea?
5. We bought ___ rice and ___ vegetables.（两个空）

### C 组：选 much / many / a little / a few

1. How ___ money do you need for the trip?（much / many / a few）
2. There are ___ eggs in the box, so we can make a cake.（a few / a little / much）
3. There is only ___ milk left in the fridge.（a little / a few / many）
4. She asked ___ questions after class.（a few / a little / much）
5. We don't have ___ time before the movie.（much / many / a few）

### D 组：用 How much 或 How many 提问

1. ___ water do you drink every day?
2. ___ people are there in your family?
3. ___ is this bag?（提示：问价格）
4. ___ cups of coffee do you drink a day?

### E 组：填量词

1. a ___ of water（一瓶水）
2. two ___ of bread（两片面包）
3. a ___ of rice（一公斤大米）
4. three ___ of milk（三杯牛奶，玻璃杯）

### F 组：改错（每句有一个错误）

1. I have many informations about the city.
2. How many rice do you want?
3. The news are bad today.
4. There is a money on the table.
