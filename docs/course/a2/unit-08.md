## 语法讲解

### 第一步：什么是介词

**介词**是放在名词前面的小词，负责交代时间和地点的关系，比如 at six（在六点）、in the room（在房间里）。它们个头小，用错了整句话的意思就歪了。中文常常不用这类词（"周一见""家在北京路"），所以中文母语者在介词上最容易翻车。好消息是：A2 只需要掌握 at / on / in 三兄弟加一小组方位词，规则不多。

### 第二步：时间三件套：at / on / in

口诀：**at 时刻，on 某天，in 长段**。

**at + 具体时刻**

- The class starts at nine o'clock. 九点上课。
- We have lunch at noon. 我们中午十二点吃午饭。
- He goes to bed late at night. 他夜里睡得很晚。（at night 是固定搭配）

**on + 具体某一天（以及某天的早中晚）**

- Let's meet on Monday. 我们周一见。
- My birthday is on May 3rd. 我的生日是五月三号。
- The meeting is on Friday morning. 会议在周五上午。（具体某天的上午用 on！）
- We usually travel on the weekend. 我们通常在周末出游。（美式英语说 on the weekend）

**in + 较长时间段（月、年、季节、泛指的早中晚）**

- My vacation starts in July. 我七月开始休假。
- She was born in 1990. 她出生于 1990 年。
- It is cold in winter. 冬天很冷。
- I study English in the morning. 我上午学英语。（泛指"早上"用 in）

| 介词 | 时间范围 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| at | 时刻 | at six o'clock, at noon, at night |
| on | 具体某天 | on Monday, on May 3rd, on Monday morning |
| in | 月 / 年 / 季节 / 泛指早中晚 | in July, in 2026, in winter, in the morning |

**最容易错的对比**：in the morning（泛指早上）和 on Monday morning（具体周一的早上）。记住一条铁律：只要说到了"具体哪一天"，这一天里的早中晚就归 on 管。

### 第三步：地点三件套：at / on / in

思路：**at 一个点，on 一个面，in 一个里**。

**at + 把地点看成一个点**（站点、门口、家里）

- I'll wait at the bus stop. 我在公交站等你。
- Someone is at the door. 门口有人。
- She is at home / at school. 她在家 / 在学校。

**on + 表面或线**（墙面、桌面、楼层、街道）

- The picture is on the wall. 画在墙上。
- Your keys are on the table. 你的钥匙在桌上。
- My office is on the third floor. 我的办公室在三楼。
- I live on Green Street. 我住在格林街。

**in + 内部空间或大地方**（房间、盒子、城市、国家）

- Your gift is in the box. 你的礼物在盒子里。
- Mom is in the kitchen. 妈妈在厨房。
- He works in Shanghai. 他在上海工作。
- I grew up in a small town. 我在一个小镇长大。

| 介词 | 空间关系 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| at | 点（站点、门口、家里） | at the station, at the bus stop, at home |
| on | 面或线（表面、楼层、街道） | on the wall, on the second floor, on Main Street |
| in | 内部或大范围（屋、城、国） | in the room, in Beijing, in China |

### 第四步：方位词：说清前后左右

- **behind** 在……后面：The garden is behind the house. 花园在房子后面。
- **in front of** 在……前面：There is a tree in front of the school. 学校前面有一棵树。
- **between** 在两者之间：The bank is between the school and the park. 银行在学校和公园之间。
- **next to** 紧挨着：The café is next to the bookstore. 咖啡馆紧挨着书店。
- **near** 在附近：I live near the subway station. 我住在地铁站附近。
- **across from** 在……对面：The hospital is across from the bank. 医院在银行对面。（美式常用 across from，相当于英式的 opposite）
- **on the left / on the right** 在左边 / 右边：The library is on the left. 图书馆在左边。

| 方位词 | 中文 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| behind | 在后面 | behind the house |
| in front of | 在前面 | in front of the school |
| between A and B | 在 A 和 B 之间 | between the bank and the park |
| next to | 紧挨着 | next to the bookstore |
| near | 在附近 | near the station |
| across from | 在对面 | across from the bank |
| on the left / on the right | 在左边 / 右边 | on the left |

一个关键区分：**in front of** 指同一侧的前方；隔着马路"对面"要说 **across from**。你站在家门口说银行 in front of my house，听者会以为银行修在了你家院子里。

### 第五步：交通方式：by 与 on foot

**by + 交通工具**，中间不加 a / the：

- I go to work by subway. 我坐地铁上班。
- She goes to school by bike. 她骑自行车上学。
- They went to Xi'an by train. 他们坐火车去了西安。
- We flew to Sanya by plane. 我们坐飞机去了三亚。

**步行是唯一的例外：on foot**

- The school is near, so I go on foot. 学校很近，所以我走路去。

| 表达 | 中文 | 注意 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| by bus / by subway / by bike / by train / by plane | 坐公交 / 地铁 / 骑车 / 火车 / 飞机 | by 后面不加 a / the |
| on foot | 步行 | 唯一用 on 的，不能说 by foot |

## 中文母语者常见错误

| ❌ 错误说法 | ✅ 正确说法 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I will see you in Monday. | I will see you on Monday. | 具体某天用 on；in 只管月、年、季节 |
| The meeting is on September. | The meeting is in September. | 月份用 in |
| I was born on 1990. | I was born in 1990. | 年份用 in |
| I go to school by the bus. | I go to school by bus. | by + 交通工具，中间不加 the / a |
| He goes to work by foot. | He goes to work on foot. | 步行固定说 on foot |
| The bank is in front of my house.（想表达银行在马路对面） | The bank is across from my house. | in front of 是同一侧的前面；隔街相望用 across from |
| The class is in Monday morning. | The class is on Monday morning. | 具体某天的上午用 on，不用 in |

### B 组：地点介词 at / on / in 填空

1. I'm ___ home now.
2. The picture is ___ the wall.
3. She is ___ the kitchen.
4. They are waiting ___ the bus stop.
5. My office is ___ the third floor.
6. He lives ___ Shanghai.

### C 组：方位词选择

1. The bank is ___ the supermarket; they are on different sides of the street.（across from / in front of / behind）
2. The park is ___ the school and the library.（between / next to / behind）
3. Our classroom is ___ the teachers' office, just next door.（next to / across from / under）
4. The garden is ___ the house, at the back.（behind / in front of / across from）
5. The hotel is ___ the station; only two minutes on foot.（near / between / on）
6. Walk down this road, and the museum is ___.（on the left / in the left / to left）

### D 组：交通方式填 by 或 on

1. I go to work ___ subway.
2. She goes to school ___ bike.
3. We go there ___ foot.
4. They went to Xi'an ___ train.

### E 组：把中文翻译成英文

1. 会议在星期五上午十点。
2. 银行在邮局对面。
3. 我家住在南京路上。
4. 她坐在妈妈和爸爸中间。
